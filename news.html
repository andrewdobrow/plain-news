4:10 AM ET

A total solar eclipse will occur on August 12, 2026, with the path of totality crossing parts of Europe and offering partial visibility in some U.S. states. The event comes one day after multiple news outlets began tracking the eclipse's approach, providing viewers across multiple continents the opportunity to witness the celestial phenomenon.

The path of totality will primarily traverse Europe, where observers in the direct path will experience complete darkness as the moon passes between Earth and the sun. The Guardian reported that Europe will see the total eclipse, with specific guidance on the best viewing locations and safety protocols for watching the event. CNN detailed how scientists map the path of totality, the narrow corridor where observers can witness the complete blocking of the sun.

In North America, some U.S. states will be positioned to see a partial solar eclipse, where the moon will cover only a portion of the sun. Al Jazeera provided a solar eclipse tracker indicating specific times the eclipse will reach different locations on August 12. WLWT confirmed that certain U.S. states will have visibility of the partial eclipse later this week.

The event represents a significant astronomical occurrence, as total solar eclipses visible from populated land areas are relatively rare. Observers in the path of totality will experience the full eclipse effect, including the dramatic dimming of daylight and the appearance of the sun's corona, the outer atmosphere normally invisible due to the sun's brightness. Those viewing partial phases will see the moon take a bite-shaped section out of the sun's disk.

Safety experts emphasized the importance of proper eye protection when viewing any phase of the eclipse. Looking directly at the sun without appropriate solar filters can cause permanent eye damage. Special eclipse glasses that meet international safety standards are required for direct viewing, while indirect viewing methods such as pinhole projectors offer safe alternatives for observing the event.

The earthquake represents one of the deadliest natural disasters to affect Colombia in recent years. Rescue operations faced the challenging task of locating and extracting people from damaged buildings, particularly in urban areas where building collapses were most severe.

Authorities coordinated resources and personnel across multiple affected areas as emergency crews continued their search through the night and into subsequent days. The mobilization of rescue workers reflected the serious nature of the disaster and the urgent need to locate survivors before critical time windows closed.

Recovery and rebuilding efforts are expected to require considerable time as the full scope of damage becomes clearer. Turnout figures from Tuesday's voting will indicate whether the concerns about voter confusion materialized. Alabama election officials have not indicated whether measures will be implemented to prevent similar two-primary situations in future redistricting cycles.

This financing arrangement comes as Wall Street financial firms position themselves to capitalize on the rapid expansion of artificial intelligence technology. For Nvidia, the arrangement provides access to significant capital for expanding AI infrastructure without bearing the complete financial burden independently. The company continues to dominate the market for AI chips, with its graphics processing units serving as the standard hardware for training and running artificial intelligence models. The partnership extends Nvidia's influence beyond chip manufacturing into the broader ecosystem of AI infrastructure development.

The announcement arrives during a period of intense scrutiny surrounding AI investment levels. Datacenter sales reached $51.2 billion, beating predictions of $49 billion. CEO Jensen Huang addressed concerns about an AI bubble during the company's earnings call, stating that "from our vantage point, we see something very different" and emphasizing Nvidia's capabilities across multiple phases of AI development.

Recent weeks saw shares in Nvidia and other AI companies decline more than 10 percent from their peaks, with broader market selloffs reflecting these anxieties. Despite these concerns, analysts note that major technology companies remain extremely profitable and continue reinvesting billions of dollars into data centers, servers, and chips. The Wall Street financing partnership represents continued confidence in sustained demand for AI infrastructure across industries as companies integrate artificial intelligence into their operations.

Senate Republicans back Thune after Trump declines to endorse leader for another term

The disagreement between Trump and Thune centers on the legislative filibuster, a procedural ru...

6:00 AM ET

Senate Republicans are publicly backing Majority Leader John Thune after President Trump declined to endorse the South Dakota Republican for another term leading the chamber. GOP senators say they will support Thune's continued leadership, even if Trump decides to back a challenger.

The disagreement between Trump and Thune centers on the legislative filibuster, a procedural rule that requires 60 votes to advance most bills in the Senate. Trump has pushed for eliminating the rule to allow Republicans to pass legislation more quickly, while Thune and other Senate Republicans have resisted the change, arguing it protects minority party rights and the long-term function of the chamber.

The public rift marks a significant moment in Republican Party dynamics, testing whether institutional loyalty within the Senate can withstand presidential pressure. Thune's refusal to abolish the filibuster has put him directly at odds with Trump, who views the rule change as necessary to efficiently advance his legislative agenda.

Senate Republicans' decision to openly support Thune despite Trump's opposition demonstrates that some members of the conference are willing to assert independence from the White House. Their backing suggests that legislative experience and institutional relationships carry meaningful weight against presidential influence, at least within the Senate Republican conference.

Recent events have shown the tension between Trump and Senate Republicans on other issues as well. When some GOP senators voted with Democrats on war powers measures related to Iran, Trump criticized them harshly during a Capitol Hill lunch. Senator Bill Cassidy of Louisiana, who voted with Democrats initially, faced particularly heated criticism from the president, who had previously endorsed a rival candidate against him in a primary.

After receiving a White House briefing on Iran from Vice President JD Vance and Trump's special envoy Steve Witkoff, Cassidy changed his position and voted against a second war powers resolution. Senator Rand Paul of Kentucky also shifted his vote, saying he wanted to give the president space to negotiate. Trump later thanked both senators and Thune for the outcome, indicating he was satisfied with their reversal.

The contrasting situations highlight the complicated relationship between Trump and Senate Republicans. While some senators like Cassidy have been willing to adjust their positions after direct pressure and White House engagement, Thune appears to have maintained his position on the filibuster despite Trump's opposition.

Whether Trump will follow through on supporting a primary challenger to Thune remains uncertain. If he does, it could set up a significant battle over the future of Senate leadership and define how much influence a president can exert over the upper chamber. The outcome could reshape how Senate Republicans balance loyalty to the president against their own institutional priorities and traditions.
