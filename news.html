Yesterday, 7:31 PM ET

President Trump is scheduled to appear on NBC's Meet the Press on Sunday following a challenging week that has seen his approval ratings decline and his relationships with Senate Republicans deteriorate publicly. The interview comes as midterm elections approach and the president faces escalating political pressure.

Trump's approval ratings have reached concerning levels. According to Gallup polling data, his second-term approval rating fell to 36 percent in December, marking one of the lowest figures the polling organization has recorded. This represents a significant drop from his 47 percent approval rating when he began his second term.

The decline in approval ratings has occurred amid disagreements on multiple fronts, including economic challenges and policy disputes with members of his own party. Growing friction with Senate Republicans has become increasingly visible to the public, creating additional obstacles for the president as his party prepares for the midterm campaign season.

Trump has shown considerable frustration with polling organizations that report unfavorable numbers about his popularity. In January, after the New York Times released a poll showing his approval rating at 40 percent, down three percentage points since September 2025, Trump threatened to expand an existing defamation lawsuit against the newspaper. In a post on Truth Social, he wrote that the Times Siena Poll "will be added to my lawsuit against The Failing New York Times."

The New York Times defended the integrity of its polling work in response to Trump's criticism. Charlie Stadtlander, a spokesperson for the newspaper, stated: "President Trump likes polls that appear favorable to him and dislikes polls that do not. But whether a poll is good or bad for the president has no bearing on our methodology." The Times noted that its polling methodology has been widely recognized for its rigor.

Meanwhile, Gallup announced this week that it will cease tracking presidential approval ratings after nearly nine decades. The polling organization, which has measured presidential approval since Harry Truman's administration, said the decision reflects "an evolution in how Gallup focuses its public research and thought leadership." A company spokesperson emphasized that the choice was based solely on the organization's research priorities and goals, not external pressure.

The Gallup Presidential Approval Rating has served as one of the most influential measures of public opinion regarding presidential performance throughout American history. The data has captured significant moments, including George W. Bush's 90 percent approval rating in the days following the 9/11 attacks.

Trump's upcoming appearance on Meet the Press will provide an opportunity for the president to address the political headwinds he currently faces. The interview occurs at a critical moment as his administration navigates both internal party tensions and declining public support.

OpenAI has introduced Lockdown Mode, a new security feature designed to protect sensitive information from prompt injection attacks. The tool aims to reduce the risk that confidential data becomes exposed when bad actors attempt to manipulate ChatGPT through carefully constructed prompts.

Prompt injection attacks represent a growing security challenge as businesses increasingly integrate AI tools into workflows handling proprietary or sensitive information. These attacks work by embedding hidden instructions within user input that can trick AI systems into ignoring their safety guidelines. By embedding malicious commands in seemingly innocent requests, attackers can potentially extract confidential details that the AI was designed to protect.

The company acknowledges that Lockdown Mode does not eliminate vulnerability to prompt injection techniques entirely. However, the feature significantly reduces the likelihood that sensitive data gets shared during such attacks. OpenAI designed the tool primarily for enterprise and business users who deploy ChatGPT in environments where data security is critical, such as handling customer information, internal communications, or proprietary research.

Lockdown Mode represents OpenAI's response to security challenges that have intensified alongside widespread AI adoption. As organizations move beyond experimental phases into production deployments, the risk of data breaches through AI systems has become a serious concern. The feature provides stronger controls for companies integrating ChatGPT into sensitive operations.

The company has not disclosed specific technical details about how Lockdown Mode works or how it differs from existing safety measures built into ChatGPT. This lack of transparency means users cannot fully understand what protections the feature provides or what its limitations might be.

OpenAI's move aligns with broader industry efforts to address vulnerabilities specific to large language models. Researchers have demonstrated multiple sophisticated techniques for extracting training data or bypassing content restrictions through advanced prompting methods. As AI systems become more embedded in business operations, the security landscape continues to evolve, with new attack vectors regularly discovered.

The introduction of Lockdown Mode signals that OpenAI recognizes the tension between making AI tools useful for businesses and ensuring they remain secure against exploitation. While the feature addresses one category of security threat, organizations deploying ChatGPT must still implement comprehensive security strategies that extend beyond individual software features.

The availability and specific requirements for accessing Lockdown Mode remain unclear. Organizations interested in enhanced protections for sensitive data should contact OpenAI directly to understand whether the feature suits their particular security needs and operational requirements.

Yesterday, 5:50 PM ET

British actor Anthony Head, best known for playing Rupert Giles in Buffy the Vampire Slayer, has died at age 72 from complications due to pneumonia. His daughters Emily and Daisy Head confirmed he passed away peacefully, surrounded by family.

"It has been, and forever will be, an honour and a privilege to be his daughters, and to have witnessed first-hand the impact both he and his work have had on so many," they said in a statement.

Head's most iconic role was as the mentor and father figure to Sarah Michelle Gellar's character throughout Buffy's seven-season run from 1997 to 2003. In a tribute posted on Instagram, Gellar opened with a quote from her character: "Tell Giles I figured it out and I'm ok." She continued, "Well I don't have it figured out and I'm not ok. But I know I'm the lucky one because I knew you."

Born in Camden, London, Head came from an artistic family. His father was a documentary filmmaker who founded Verity Films, while his mother was an actor who appeared in the BBC crime drama Maigret. His brother was also an actor, best known for his lead role in the 1971 film Sunday Bloody Sunday.

Head began his professional career on stage, starring in a 1978 West End revival of Godspell alongside Su Pollard. Throughout the 1980s and 1990s, he appeared in various productions including the original West End production of the Abba musical Chess in 1988 and a 1990 production of The Rocky Horror Show as Dr. Frank-N-Furter. He also took roles in films including a 1981 adaptation of Lady Chatterley's Lover and 1987's A Prayer for the Dying with Bob Hoskins and Liam Neeson.

In the early 1980s, Head sang backing vocals for the British pop group Red Box and was featured on their 1986 debut album. He became widely recognized in the UK and US for his role in Nescafe Gold Blend television advertisements, which portrayed a slow-burn courtship between a British couple.

While filming Buffy in Los Angeles, Head remained committed to his family in England. He told The Guardian that he would return home every three or four weeks whenever the production schedule allowed, spending considerable money on airfares to maintain those connections.

After Buffy ended, Head took a recurring role in the BBC sketch show Little Britain and appeared in the BBC production of Merlin. In recent years, he played former football club owner Rupert Mannion in the Apple TV series Ted Lasso, which introduced him to new audiences and earned praise for his on-screen chemistry with co-creator Jason Sudeikis.

Head's daughters noted that his legacy will live on through the shows he worked on and the audiences who loved them.

These attacks work by embedding hidden instructions within user input that can trick AI systems into ignoring their safety guidelines. By embedding malicious commands in seemingly innocent requests, attackers can potentially extract confidential details that the AI was designed to protect.

The company acknowledges that Lockdown Mode does not eliminate vulnerability to prompt injection techniques entirely. OpenAI designed the tool primarily for enterprise and business users who deploy ChatGPT in environments where data security is critical, such as handling customer information, internal communications, or proprietary research.

Lockdown Mode represents OpenAI's response to security challenges that have intensified alongside widespread AI adoption. As organizations move beyond experimental phases into production deployments, the risk of data breaches through AI systems has become a serious concern. The feature provides stronger controls for companies integrating ChatGPT into sensitive operations.

The company has not disclosed specific technical details about how Lockdown Mode works or how it differs from existing safety measures built into ChatGPT. This lack of transparency means users cannot fully understand what protections the feature provides or what its limitations might be.

OpenAI's move aligns with broader industry efforts to address vulnerabilities specific to large language models. Researchers have demonstrated multiple sophisticated techniques for extracting training data or bypassing content restrictions through advanced prompting methods. As AI systems become more embedded in business operations, the security landscape continues to evolve, with new attack vectors regularly discovered.

The introduction of Lockdown Mode signals that OpenAI recognizes the tension between making AI tools useful for businesses and ensuring they remain secure against exploitation. While the feature addresses one category of security threat, organizations deploying ChatGPT must still implement comprehensive security strategies that extend beyond individual software features.

The availability and specific requirements for accessing Lockdown Mode remain unclear. Organizations interested in enhanced protections for sensitive data should contact OpenAI directly to understand whether the feature suits their particular security needs and operational requirements.
