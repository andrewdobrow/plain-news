Yesterday, 10:23 PM ET

The United States has reached an agreement with Venezuela covering more than 65 billion barrels of oil reserves, President Trump announced. The president described the deal as historic, saying it would give the US control over a significant portion of Venezuelan oil production. A US official said the arrangement would involve a partnership with a private company.

Venezuela's interim president said the agreement would help revive the country's economy. The deal represents an unusual arrangement between the two countries, which have had strained relations for years. Trump hailed the agreement as a major accomplishment for American energy interests.

The 65 billion barrels covered by the agreement represent a substantial share of Venezuela's total oil reserves, which are among the largest in the world. Venezuela's oil industry has deteriorated significantly in recent years due to underinvestment and economic turmoil. The country's production capacity has fallen dramatically from its historical peaks.

The structure of the deal involves American control through a partnership with a private company rather than direct government ownership. This arrangement allows the US to exercise influence over Venezuelan oil production while maintaining the appearance of commercial operations. The interim Venezuelan government framed the agreement as an economic necessity to rebuild the country's primary revenue source.

The deal comes as Trump enters the midterm campaign season facing economic challenges at home. The agreement could potentially increase oil supplies and ease pressure on energy prices, though the timeline for implementation and production increases remains unclear. The arrangement also marks a significant shift in US-Venezuela relations and could reshape energy markets if production recovers substantially.

Gunfire and explosions erupted near Niamey airport and an adjacent military base in Niger's capital early Wednesday morning, prompting a heavy security deployment to the area. The shooting and detonations began shortly after midnight and lasted about an hour, according to residents living near the airport.

Videos captured by local residents showed streaks of light across the sky, loud explosions, and flames several metres high, along with charred vehicles at the scene. Satellite images later revealed scorched earth near the airport's runway. Several aircraft parked at the airport sustained bullet damage, including planes belonging to Togolese airline Asky and Air Côte d'Ivoire.

General Abdourahmane Tchiani, who seized power in a 2023 coup, blamed the attack on the presidents of France, Benin and Côte d'Ivoire without providing evidence. Speaking on state television, he said: "We have heard them bark, they should be ready to hear us roar," signalling a potential escalation in tensions with neighbouring countries and France, which he views as supporting regional rivals.

However, multiple observers suggested the attack likely came from jihadist groups. Niger faces ongoing threats from armed organisations linked to Islamic State and al-Qaida affiliates operating across the tri-border region with Mali and Burkina Faso. Last weekend, a jihadist group claimed responsibility for destroying an army vehicle with an improvised explosive device east of Niamey.

Beverly Ochieng, an analyst at Control Risks, told Agence France-Presse that jihadist groups have recently increased their presence near Niamey. She noted the incident could align with tactics used by the al-Qaida-affiliated group JNIM "to continue targeting the security establishment as a way of delegitimising the juntas."

Niger state television reported that at least one of several assailants killed was a French national, showing footage of bloodied bodies without providing further evidence. The full scope of casualties remained unclear as authorities had not issued official statements about the attack.

Since Tchiani's 2023 coup, Niger has experienced significant geopolitical realignment, expelling French and US forces and turning toward Russia for military support. The country withdrew from the West African regional bloc alongside Mali and Burkina Faso to form the Alliance of Sahel States.

Security conditions have deteriorated markedly since the coup despite military promises of stability. The Global Terrorism Index 2025 reported that Niger recorded the largest increase in terrorism deaths globally in 2024, with deaths rising 94 percent to 930 total deaths, marking the country's worst ranking since the index began.

1:31 AM ET

The Trump administration is actively pursuing a deal to transfer a quarter-mile parcel of land inside Yosemite National Park to a Nevada-based private developer, according to reporting by the news outlet Notus.

National Park Service staff have been exploring how the agency can transfer the land to Kingsbarn Realty Capital, a company that already owns an 83-acre plot immediately outside the park's boundaries. The development company's CEO, Jeff Pori, wants to construct a short road connecting that exterior parcel to a central Yosemite route, according to Notus, which cited agency documents and unnamed sources with direct knowledge of the discussions.

The exterior land was previously owned by someone who attempted for decades to secure federal approval to build a private resort and construct a private road through the park. That access route would currently require travelers to take a journey more than 10 miles from the nearest park entrance to reach the property and popular park attractions.

Administration officials have proposed that the National Park Service transfer the land in exchange for equally valued land elsewhere in California. According to sources cited by Notus, the interior department's top political leaders support the arrangement, and the situation has created internal tension within the park service.

An interior department spokesperson disputed the reporting. "There has been no political pressure to reach a predetermined outcome," the statement said, calling the suggestion that the department is "secretly working to hand over National Park Service land to a private developer" false. The department added that no final decisions have been reached and that any land exchange would be "subject to all applicable federal laws, regulations and Departmental policies, including required environmental review and public notification processes."

The proposal has generated strong opposition from conservation advocates and lawmakers. Jayson O'Neill, spokesperson for Save Our Parks, characterized the effort as part of a broader "push to sell off public lands and privatize America's national parks" under interior secretary Doug Burgum.

"If mismanaging parks and stealing entrance fees for Trump's DC vanity projects wasn't enough, now Americans find out Doug Burgum and his cronies spent a year secretly negotiating to hand a piece of Yosemite national park over to a well-connected developer," O'Neill said.

Both of California's senators and Democratic congressman Jared Huffman, who represents California's north coast, have expressed opposition to developing the road. Huffman said the parks "belong to all of us" and should "be protected for every generation, not sold off to Trump's ultra-wealthy friends to profit off of."

Yosemite was designated a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1984 and attracts approximately four million visitors annually. The nearly 750,000-acre park in the Sierra Nevada has been protected under federal conservation laws established by the National Park Service Organic Act of 1916.

Researchers have identified ten key areas where the Roman Space Telescope could provide valuable observations and data once it becomes operational. The mission will add to the collection of active NASA space telescopes currently gathering data about the universe.

SpaceX has conducted numerous launches for NASA in recent years, establishing a regular schedule of scientific missions alongside its commercial operations. The telescope's deployment will expand the range of observations available to astronomers studying cosmic phenomena.

The launch will take place from a SpaceX facility, with mission preparations currently underway at the launch site. Multiple outlets have reported on the telescope's expected contributions to cosmic exploration and the anticipated timeline for the August 30 launch.

The Roman Space Telescope represents an important addition to NASA's fleet of space-based observation equipment. Liverpool will pay a guaranteed sum of just over £100 million, with additional fees based on individual and team achievements taking the total package to approximately £123 million.

Barcola, 23, will undergo a medical this weekend to complete the formalities of his move to Anfield. The French winger has been Liverpool's primary transfer target throughout the summer and made clear his desire to join the club, having dropped down the pecking order at PSG. He has two years remaining on his Paris contract but refused to sign an extension, pushing the French champions toward accepting a lower fee.

The transfer makes Barcola Liverpool's second-most expensive signing in history, behind Alexander Isak at £125 million. PSG originally signed the player from Lyon for €45 million in 2023, and he played a key role in their rise as Champions League winners. However, manager Luis Enrique's preference for Désiré Doué, Ousmane Dembélé and Khvicha Kvaratskhelia in their biggest matches last season prompted Barcola to reconsider his future. PSG have also added other attacking options this summer, including Ferran Torres, Maghnes Akliouche and Mika Godts, and have excluded Barcola from their matchday squads.

The signing raises fresh questions about Cody Gakpo's future at the club. The Netherlands international has attracted interest from Tottenham and Manchester City, and manager Andoni Iraola was unable to guarantee the winger would remain at Liverpool beyond the deadline. "When the market is open you cannot guarantee anything with anyone," Iraola said.

Despite securing Barcola, Liverpool still need to replace Mohamed Salah on the right wing. The club has had two bids rejected by Brighton for Yankuba Minteh and enquired about Crystal Palace's Ismaïla Sarr without making a formal offer.

Iraola indicated the club would attempt to strengthen in other areas if opportunities arise, particularly given the long-term injuries affecting defender Conor Bradley and striker Hugo Ekitiké. "Ideally is the magic word," Iraola said. "We don't know what we can do, but it's true the priority has been mostly in the wide areas."

Parton rose from poverty in Tennessee to become a cultural icon and prolific songwriter, writing 3,000 songs including hits like "Jolene" and "9 to 5." She built a career spanning more than six decades and achieved success as an actress, appearing in major Hollywood films. Beyond entertainment, she became an accomplished businesswoman with ventures ranging from the Dollywood theme park to cosmetics and baking mixes.

Her philanthropic work was equally impressive. The Dollywood Foundation distributes approximately 25 million books annually through her Imagination Library across the US, Canada, Australia, the UK and Ireland. She also donated one million dollars for COVID-19 vaccine research, which formed the basis for the Moderna vaccine.

Following news of her death, tributes poured in from across music, film, and politics. Jane Fonda, her co-star in "9 to 5," wrote an emotional Instagram post, describing Parton as "the voluptuous embodiment of empathy" and noting that "more than anyone I have ever known, Dolly will always be with us because she was so present, so powerful, so generous and playful."

Pitbull performed at a concert in New York, calling Parton "a good human being with a good heart" who believed in him when others didn't.

Other celebrities paying tribute included Beyonce, Paul McCartney, Elton John, and Christina Aguilera. The White House lowered its flag to half-mast in her honor.

In Tennessee, Governor Bill Lee announced plans to rename Nashville International Airport after Parton as a lasting tribute to the entertainer who maintained deep ties to her home state throughout her life. Searches for Dolly Parton merchandise surged dramatically following her death, with online searches for T-shirts increasing by 10,000 percent as fans sought ways to commemorate her legacy.

Edgeworth wrote best-selling fiction that incorporated sophisticated economic concepts accessible to general readers.
