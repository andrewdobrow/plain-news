The administration has begun preliminary talks with OpenAI about a potential government stake in the AI startup. Trump indicated the administra...

12:53 AM ET

President Trump said the federal government is exploring whether to take ownership stakes in leading artificial intelligence companies, a shift from the traditional hands-off approach the US has long taken toward the technology sector. The administration has begun preliminary talks with OpenAI about a potential government stake in the AI startup.

Trump indicated the administration may move forward with profit-sharing arrangements with AI companies within the coming week. Under such an arrangement, the government would receive partial ownership in exchange for providing support or access to computing resources. Officials have described the approach as a mechanism to ensure the public benefits from AI development while preserving American leadership in the field.

The proposal has attracted commentary from across the political spectrum. Senator Bernie Sanders published an opinion piece arguing that artificial intelligence should be treated as a public resource, suggesting that citizens should own half of AI companies. The idea represents a significant departure from the low-regulation stance that has shaped US technology policy for decades.

The timing reflects broader questions emerging about how AI development should be regulated, how it should be governed to protect public safety, and who should gain financially from technological breakthroughs. OpenAI, valued at more than 100 billion dollars in private fundraising rounds, has received substantial investments from Microsoft and other private capital sources. Adding federal ownership would introduce government involvement into an industry sector that has been built primarily through private investment.

The administration has not disclosed potential details about any government equity positions, including their size, structure, or which companies beyond OpenAI might participate. The legal and regulatory mechanisms for establishing such arrangements remain uncertain, as does the specific process through which the government would obtain equity. Policy experts expect the proposal will face scrutiny from Congress and will raise complex questions about the appropriate boundaries between government involvement in private industry and maintaining fair market competition.

The discussions highlight the growing importance of artificial intelligence to national economic strategy and security. As AI companies have attracted unprecedented levels of private investment and achieved enormous valuations, policymakers have increasingly focused on questions about public benefit and national interest.

The proposal also comes as the Trump administration is developing broader strategies for AI policy. The administration has announced plans to meet with major AI companies to discuss the potential profit-sharing framework within the coming week, signaling the priority the White House places on these discussions.

Whether Congress would approve legislative changes needed to enable direct government equity ownership in private companies, and what constitutional questions might arise from such arrangements, remain unclear. The proposal will likely generate significant debate among lawmakers, economists, and technology industry representatives about the proper role of government in directing private sector development.

The Lincoln carrier strike group represents the most visible element of a US military buildup in the Middle East following a violent crackdown against anti-government demonstrations in Iran last month. Canada has implemented a ban on cattle imports from Texas following confirmation of a second infected calf in the state. The action comes as Texas officials declared a state of disaster in response to the outbreak.

The screwworm is a parasitic fly larva that feeds on living tissue in warm-blooded animals. The pest was successfully eradicated from the United States in 1966 but continues to exist in parts of Central and South America. The recent cases represent the first confirmed detections in Texas in decades.

The identification of the second infected animal prompted immediate action from Canadian agricultural authorities. The import ban covers all cattle movements from Texas as officials work to contain the outbreak and prevent the parasite from spreading further across borders and regions.

Texas agriculture officials have activated response teams to survey livestock populations and establish quarantine zones around affected areas. The state's disaster declaration provides access to emergency resources and enables the rapid deployment of veterinary personnel to locations where the outbreak is occurring. Federal and state agencies are coordinating inspection protocols and developing treatment strategies to address the crisis.

The outbreak presents significant economic concerns for the Texas cattle industry, which ranks among the largest in the nation. Canada serves as a major trading partner for U.S. livestock exports, and this ban threatens to disrupt established supply chains and potentially affect market prices for cattle producers.

Authorities are focused on determining how the infestation began and implementing preventive measures to stop additional cases from occurring. The discovery of infected animals has raised concerns about the potential for broader trade restrictions from other states and countries if the outbreak cannot be quickly contained.

The screwworm's presence in the region marks a concerning development after decades without detection in the United States. The parasite's ability to infect livestock has prompted swift international response, with trading partners like Canada taking precautionary measures to protect their own herds from potential exposure.

Texas officials are working with federal agencies to coordinate a comprehensive response that addresses both the immediate threat to livestock in the state and the longer-term implications for regional cattle trade. The situation remains fluid as investigation and containment efforts continue.

The ban highlights the interconnected nature of North American agriculture and how disease outbreaks in one region can quickly affect trade relationships and economic activity across borders. Officials emphasize the importance of rapid detection and response protocols in managing livestock health crises that could have far-reaching consequences for the industry.

Wembanyama then had a clean look from the elbow at the buzzer, but the shot caromed off the back rim. This victory made the Knicks only the third team to win the first two games of an NBA Finals on the road, joining the 1993 Chicago Bulls and the 1995 Houston Rockets.

Brunson continued his standout performance in crucial moments, leading the Knicks' offense through another clutch game. The All-NBA guard has been instrumental in the team's success, particularly in close contests. After stealing Game 1 with a furious fourth-quarter comeback, the Knicks demonstrated their ability to win in hostile environments.

Josh Hart made an unusual impact in Game 2, with what was described as a quiet statistical performance by his standards. Despite his lower numbers, Hart's teammates praised his willingness to adapt to whatever the team needed. He matched a Finals record previously held by Larry Bird in the game.

The Knicks are riding exceptional momentum heading into Game 3. The team has won 11 consecutive games, many by substantial margins, and brings a fresh appearance with a week of rest before the Finals began. Their defense has been particularly strong, posting the best defensive net rating of the NBA playoffs, which could prove crucial against the Spurs' young core.

The Spurs face an uphill battle returning to New York. No team has ever overcome a 3-0 deficit in the NBA Finals, making Game 3 critical for San Antonio's championship hopes. Coming off a physically demanding series against the Oklahoma City Thunder, the Spurs may be dealing with fatigue as they attempt their comeback.

Yesterday, 9:22 PM ET

Anthony Head, the British actor best known for playing Rupert Giles in Buffy the Vampire Slayer, has died at age 72. He passed away peacefully from complications due to pneumonia, surrounded by his family.

"It has been, and forever will be, an honour and a privilege to be his daughters, and to have witnessed first-hand the impact both he and his work have had on so many," his daughters Emily and Daisy Head said in a statement.

Head portrayed Giles, the school librarian and mentor to Sarah Michelle Gellar's vampire slayer, throughout the series' seven-season run from 1997 to 2003. The character became one of the most beloved figures in the show, guiding Buffy Summers through her supernatural battles with wisdom, humor, and emotional depth.

Sarah Michelle Gellar paid tribute to Head on Instagram, beginning with a quote from her character: "Tell Giles I figured it out and I'm ok." She continued: "Well I don't have it figured out and I'm not ok. But I know I'm the lucky one because I knew you."

Beyond Buffy, Head enjoyed a lengthy career spanning several decades. He was perhaps best known in the UK and US for his role in Nescafe Gold Blend television advertisements, which featured a slow-burn courtship between a British couple. He also appeared in the BBC sketch show Little Britain as a prime minister constantly fawned over by a flirtatious assistant, played by David Walliams.

Head took on additional roles in the BBC production of Merlin, and more recently appeared in Ted Lasso, where he played former football club owner Rupert Mannion and worked alongside co-creator Jason Sudeikis. His film work included appearances in The Iron Lady and The Inbetweeners Movie.

His career began on the stage, where he starred in the 1978 West End revival of Godspell. He later played Freddie Trumper in the original West End production of the Abba musical Chess in 1988, and performed as Dr Frank-N-Furter in the 1990 West End production of The Rocky Horror Show. In the early 1980s, he also sang backing vocals for the British pop group Red Box.

Born in Camden, London, Head came from an artistic family. His father founded Verity Films and worked as a documentary filmmaker, while his mother was an actress who appeared in the 1960s BBC crime drama Maigret.

During Buffy's production, Head commuted between Los Angeles and England, where his partner Sarah and two daughters lived. "I'd try to go home to them every three or four weeks," he told the Guardian. "I spent quite a lot of money on airfares, but we made it work."

His daughters noted that Head loved his work and considered himself fortunate to have worked with talented people across productions spanning several decades. "His legacy will live on in the shows he was a part of and in the audiences that love them," they said.

Yesterday, 7:01 PM ET

Square Enix announced Final Fantasy VII: Revelation, the final installment in its remake trilogy, at Summer Game Fest Live. The game will release simultaneously across PC, PS5, Xbox Series X/S, and Nintendo Switch 2 in spring 2027. A debut trailer showcased gameplay footage from the upcoming title.

The remake trilogy modernizes the 1997 original with updated graphics and expanded narrative. The first two games, Final Fantasy VII Remake and Final Fantasy VII Rebirth, launched in 2020 and 2024 respectively.

Guild Wars 3 Announced for PC and PlayStation 5 with Beta Planned for Fall 2027

ArenaNet revealed Guild Wars 3 at Summer Game Fest, marking a new chapter for the massively multiplayer online role-playing game franchise.

Yesterday, 5:56 PM ET

The developer announced Guild Wars 3 will launch on PC and PlayStation 5, with a beta test scheduled for fall 2027. The game represents what the company describes as a modern evolution of the MMORPG genre. ArenaNet released a trailer showcasing the new title at the Summer Game Fest event.

Guild Wars 2, the current game in the franchise, continues to operate with its player base intact. The original Guild Wars launched in 2005, and Guild Wars 2 followed in 2012. The franchise has maintained a following in the competitive MMORPG market dominated by titles like World of Warcraft and Final Fantasy XIV.

Maine Senate candidate addresses past criticism at campaign rally

Democratic candidate Graham Platner told supporters his background has been used against him in the race against Senator Susan Collins.

Yesterday, 9:33 PM ET

Democratic Senate candidate Graham Platner held a rally in Maine where he characterized recent controversies as a deliberate effort to undermine his campaign. He is denying accusations of physical aggression toward former girlfriends, including allegations reported by The New York Times.

Supporters at the event rejected negative stories about Platner and dismissed suggestions that the controversies disqualify him from challenging Republican Senator Susan Collins. Platner's campaign team views the scrutiny as a sign that his candidacy is gaining traction.
