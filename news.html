2:15 PM ET

A flesh-eating parasite has returned to the United States for the first time in six decades, triggering emergency restrictions on livestock movement between Texas and Mexico. The new world screwworm fly was confirmed in a calf in south Texas, approximately 50 miles from the Mexican border, marking the first detection in the state since 1966.

The parasite's reappearance has created immediate international consequences. Canada has banned cattle imports from Texas, while a second infected calf was subsequently identified in the state. Texas Governor Greg Abbott declared a disaster over the outbreak as state and federal officials mobilized to contain the spread.

The screwworm fly poses a serious threat to livestock agriculture. Female flies lay eggs in open wounds on warm-blooded animals, and the larvae feed on blood and living flesh. If untreated, infected animals can die. The parasite was eradicated from the United States in the 1970s after causing tens of millions of dollars in losses. Officials are particularly concerned given that beef prices across the country are currently at record highs.

"For months, the screwworm has advanced rapidly through Mexico," said Sid Miller, Texas agriculture commissioner. "Even though billions of sterile flies have been dispersed by USDA, the screwworm has still advanced over 1,100 miles from southern Mexico to Texas."

The USDA has defended its response, with Agriculture Secretary Brooke Rollins stating there is no threat of mass infestation. She emphasized that the Texas case was the first confirmed detection since 1966 and that there is "no reason to believe this incursion will result in establishment of the pest in our country."

To combat the outbreak, the USDA announced in March that it had partnered with the Army Corps of Engineers and Mortenson Construction to build a new sterile fly production facility at Moore Airbase in Edinburg, Texas. The program relies on releasing sterile male flies, which mate with wild females and produce eggs that do not hatch, gradually reducing the population over time.

Miller has urged the Trump administration to deploy the screwworm adult suppression system, or Swass, which combines attractants, bait, and targeted insecticides to reduce adult populations before sterile fly releases complete the eradication effort. This approach successfully eliminated screwworms from Mexico and Texas during previous campaigns.

The parasite's southward progression through Mexico last year forced ranchers to resort to crude treatments. High medicine costs led some to apply gasoline or lime to open wounds in attempts to extract worms. The border restrictions now prevent Texas cattle from reaching those markets while disrupting the traditional livestock trade that has long connected the two countries' agricultural sectors.

8:00 AM ET

America's crude oil stockpiles have dropped to their lowest level in more than two decades, marking the sixth consecutive week of declines. The shrinking reserves come as the Trump administration's conflict with Iran disrupts global oil markets and pushes exports from the Gulf Coast to record levels.

The Strategic Petroleum Reserve and commercial crude inventories have both declined sharply in recent weeks. Record exports from Gulf Coast refineries have accelerated the drawdown, as American oil flows to international markets facing supply constraints. The consecutive weekly drops represent one of the most sustained periods of inventory decline in recent years.

Despite the inventory crunch, oil prices have not surged to the extreme levels some analysts predicted. Multiple factors have kept prices in check, including increased production from other suppliers and demand adjustments in response to higher costs. The supply shock, while significant, has not translated into the catastrophic price spikes that historically accompanied major Middle East disruptions.

The low inventory levels raise concerns about America's ability to respond to future supply disruptions. The Strategic Petroleum Reserve exists specifically to buffer against such shocks, but sustained drawdowns limit that capacity. Energy analysts are watching closely to see whether the administration will take steps to rebuild reserves or whether exports will continue at current pace.

The situation reflects broader tensions in global energy markets. The Iran conflict has removed significant supply from international markets, while robust demand and limited spare capacity from other producers have left little room for error. Gulf Coast refineries continue operating at high capacity to meet both domestic and international demand, but the pace of inventory decline suggests the current trajectory is unsustainable without either reduced exports or increased domestic production.

The creative team and stars behind Taxi Driver gathered at New York's Tribeca film festival to celebrate five decades since the film's release and reflect on its enduring cultural significance. Director Martin Scorsese, actor Robert De Niro, actress Jodie Foster, and screenwriter Paul Schrader participated in a screening at the BMCC Tribeca Performing Arts Center to examine the film's lasting impact.

Released in February 1976, Taxi Driver immediately became a cultural phenomenon. The film earned the Palme d'Or at Cannes and received four Academy Award nominations, including best picture and best actor for De Niro. Foster, who was just 12 when the film was shot, earned a nomination for best supporting actress.

Scorsese emphasized the film's universal appeal despite its age. "It's a sense of being isolated, it's about being lonely and not being able to communicate or connect," he said. "For me, that's universal. It's always going to speak to young people." Schrader noted the film's remarkable staying power. "If we marked the 50th anniversary of a film in 1976, we'd be talking about a 1926 movie," he said. "So it is very peculiar."

De Niro's character, Travis Bickle, is an insomniac cab driver navigating a grimy New York City while struggling with isolation and an inability to connect with others. The script, written by Schrader, meticulously details his descent into violence. "Each page was like a razor blade," Scorsese recalled of reading the screenplay.

De Niro reflected on the film's continued relevance. "Today, I do understand that people are still lonely, especially with the internet, and in light of the pandemic. People are getting more isolated and getting into worlds they shouldn't get into, becoming obsessed with something negative."

The film proved disturbingly prophetic. Though Columbine occurred decades later in 1999, Taxi Driver was shown during the trial of John Hinkley Jr., who attempted to assassinate Ronald Reagan in 1981 while allegedly attempting to impress Foster. The archetype of the alienated young man with violent tendencies has only intensified in modern times, paralleling the rise of extremist internet groups.

Foster discussed the character's complexity. "When I first read the script, there was something absolutely 100% true and authentic about the character's disaffection and loneliness that I didn't really understand at the time," she said. "That's the draw of the antihero."

Scorsese also contrasted the film's gritty 1970s New York setting with today's city. When they filmed in 1975, crime peaked and the city faced financial crisis. "There was something during the shoot where you could feel the temperature and a kind of violence all around you," he said. Schrader lamented the city's transformation. "It was alive," he said of New York in the 1960s and 1970s.

President Donald Trump issued a full pardon for Steve Buyer, a former Indiana Republican congressman who was convicted of insider trading in 2023. The pardon wipes away Buyer's criminal record and any penalties associated with the conviction.

Buyer served in the House of Representatives from 1993 to 2011, representing Indiana's 4th and 5th congressional districts during his tenure. After leaving Congress, he worked as a consultant and lobbyist before his legal troubles began.

In 2023, a federal jury convicted Buyer on charges related to insider trading. Prosecutors alleged he used nonpublic information to make stock trades that generated illegal profits. The conviction came after an investigation into his financial activities following his departure from Congress.

The pardon is the latest in a series of clemency actions by Trump, who has used his presidential pardon power extensively. Trump has previously granted pardons to political allies, former staff members, and others convicted of federal crimes. The pardon power, granted to the president by the Constitution, allows the chief executive to forgive federal crimes and remove associated punishments.

Buyer's pardon adds to ongoing debates about presidential clemency and its use for individuals with political connections to the president. The move drew criticism from ethics watchdogs who argue that pardons for white-collar crimes by former elected officials undermine public trust in government accountability.

Taxi Driver Filmmakers and Stars Reunite for 50th Anniversary

The creative team and stars behind Taxi Driver gathered at New York's Tribeca film festival to celebrate five decades since the film's release and reflect on its enduring cultural significance. Director Martin Scorsese,...

4:00 PM ET

The creative team and stars behind Taxi Driver gathered at New York's Tribeca film festival to celebrate five decades since the film's release and reflect on its enduring cultural significance. Director Martin Scorsese, actor Robert De Niro, actress Jodie Foster, and screenwriter Paul Schrader participated in a screening at the BMCC Tribeca Performing Arts Center to examine the film's lasting impact.

Released in February 1976, Taxi Driver immediately became a cultural phenomenon. The film earned the Palme d'Or at Cannes and received four Academy Award nominations, including best picture and best actor for De Niro. Foster, who was just 12 when the film was shot, earned a nomination for best supporting actress.

Scorsese emphasized the film's universal appeal despite its age. "It's a sense of being isolated, it's about being lonely and not being able to communicate or connect," he said. "For me, that's universal. It's always going to speak to young people." Schrader noted the film's remarkable staying power. "If we marked the 50th anniversary of a film in 1976, we'd be talking about a 1926 movie," he said. "So it is very peculiar."

De Niro's character, Travis Bickle, is an insomniac cab driver navigating a grimy New York City while struggling with isolation and an inability to connect with others. The script, written by Schrader, meticulously details his descent into violence. "Each page was like a razor blade," Scorsese recalled of reading the screenplay.

De Niro reflected on the film's continued relevance. "Today, I do understand that people are still lonely, especially with the internet, and in light of the pandemic. People are getting more isolated and getting into worlds they shouldn't get into, becoming obsessed with something negative."

The film proved disturbingly prophetic. Though Columbine occurred decades later in 1999, Taxi Driver was shown during the trial of John Hinkley Jr., who attempted to assassinate Ronald Reagan in 1981 while allegedly attempting to impress Foster. The archetype of the alienated young man with violent tendencies has only intensified in modern times, paralleling the rise of extremist internet groups.

Foster discussed the character's complexity. "When I first read the script, there was something absolutely 100% true and authentic about the character's disaffection and loneliness that I didn't really understand at the time," she said. "That's the draw of the antihero."

Scorsese also contrasted the film's gritty 1970s New York setting with today's city. When they filmed in 1975, crime peaked and the city faced financial crisis. "There was something during the shoot where you could feel the temperature and a kind of violence all around you," he said. Schrader lamented the city's transformation. "It was alive," he said of New York in the 1960s and 1970s.
