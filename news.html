5:38 PM ET

US stock markets suffered their worst decline of the year on Friday as investors fled technology stocks and reassessed interest rate expectations following stronger-than-expected jobs data. The Nasdaq fell 4.2 percent, marking its steepest single-day drop since April 2025, while the S&P 500 declined more than 2.6 percent, ending nine consecutive weeks of gains.

The selloff centered on chip and technology companies, with semiconductor stocks experiencing sharp declines. Broadcom and Intel shares dropped along with other major chip manufacturers including AMD, Micron, Qualcomm, and Super Micro. The technology-heavy Nasdaq bore the brunt of selling pressure as investors rotated away from artificial intelligence and megacap technology stocks that had driven much of the market's recent gains.

The market decline came after the Labor Department reported that US employers added 172,000 jobs in May, substantially exceeding economist forecasts. The robust hiring data prompted traders to increase their expectations that the Federal Reserve may resume raising interest rates to combat persistent inflation. Higher interest rates typically reduce the appeal of growth-oriented technology stocks, whose valuations depend heavily on future earnings.

Bitcoin also fell below $60,000 for the first time since the November 2024 election, reflecting a broader shift in investor sentiment away from digital assets and toward other investment opportunities.

The Dow Jones Industrial Average, which includes fewer technology companies, posted modest gains and reached a record level, suggesting investors sought refuge in more traditional industrial and financial stocks. Market analysts said the divergence demonstrated that some sectors could continue performing well even as the technology trade cooled. The sharp contrast between the Dow's performance and the Nasdaq's decline highlighted the concentrated nature of recent market weakness in the technology sector.

The selloff underscores ongoing tensions for investors balancing inflation concerns against growth prospects. While strong employment data signals economic resilience, it also raises the likelihood of additional Fed rate increases that could slow business expansion. This dynamic has created uncertainty about whether technology stocks, which soared on optimism about artificial intelligence applications, can sustain their momentum.

Analysts noted that the market rotation suggests investors are reassessing their strategy after months of technology dominance. Some are moving capital into defensive sectors and traditional value stocks that typically perform better in higher interest rate environments. The employment report effectively reset market expectations about the Fed's path forward, with traders now pricing in a greater probability of rate increases rather than the cuts that had been anticipated earlier in the year.

The day's trading illustrated how sensitive markets remain to economic data and Federal Reserve policy signals. As investors digest the implications of stronger hiring for inflation and interest rates, the question remains whether the technology sector's recent strength has run its course or if this represents merely a temporary pullback before renewed gains.

4:35 PM ET

The Centers for Disease Control and Prevention released new modeling showing that the current Ebola outbreak in the Democratic Republic of the Congo could sicken more than 20,000 people in the next three months without prompt intervention. The projection places the outbreak among the most severe Ebola crises on record if the situation continues unchecked.

Since the outbreak was identified in mid-May, the Bundibugyo strain of Ebola has caused 344 confirmed cases and 60 deaths in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, along with 15 confirmed cases and one death in neighboring Uganda. The virus may have begun spreading much earlier than initially recognized. World Health Organization director Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus said the outbreak could have started as early as January, giving the virus a significant head start before detection and response efforts began.

The CDC's warning emphasizes that this projection is not inevitable. Health officials stress that rapid implementation of containment measures can prevent the outbreak from reaching such catastrophic levels. Standard response strategies include contact tracing, isolating infected patients, ensuring safe burial practices, and strengthening healthcare infrastructure in affected areas.

Contact tracing represents a critical challenge to containing the spread. Currently, only about 45 percent of contacts are being followed up in affected regions. Tedros stated that reaching above 90 percent contact tracing would be essential to getting ahead of the outbreak. The low contact tracing rates stem from multiple obstacles, including insecurity and displacement in Ituri province, the most heavily impacted area.

Community mistrust poses another significant barrier to response efforts. Tedros reported that some community leaders expressed skepticism about whether Ebola is real, while others worried that outbreak response activities would divert resources from other critical health services. Additionally, blanket travel restrictions imposed by some countries, including the United States, are disrupting supply chains and hindering the international response.

Laboratory and diagnostic capacity remains limited in the most affected areas and neighboring regions. This capacity gap contributed to a testing backlog that caused a sudden drop in reported suspected cases from over 1,000 to 116 as officials worked through pending test results to confirm or rule out infections.

Despite the absence of a vaccine or treatment specifically for the Bundibugyo strain, health officials note a reason for cautious hope. Six people in the Democratic Republic of the Congo and two in Uganda have recovered from Ebola, demonstrating that survival is possible with access to proper care and early treatment. The key to recovery is ensuring that infected individuals reach health facilities promptly after symptoms appear.

Public health experts view the CDC's modeling as an urgent call to action for the international community. The three-month timeline underscores how quickly the situation could deteriorate without coordinated global response efforts focused on containment and community engagement.

5:00 PM ET

Security researchers have discovered a vulnerability in Creative's Sound Blaster Katana V2X speaker that allows attackers to infect computers through Bluetooth without any user action. The exploit, called Pwnd Blaster, targets the speaker's firmware and can compromise connected PCs remotely.

The vulnerability works through the speaker's Bluetooth connection to a computer. Once a speaker is within range, an attacker can exploit weaknesses in how the device communicates with the host system. The attack does not require the user to click anything, open files, or interact with the speaker in any way. The speaker simply needs to be connected to the computer via USB and have Bluetooth enabled.

Creative, the manufacturer of the Sound Blaster Katana V2X, does not consider the behavior a security vulnerability. The company has not indicated whether it plans to issue a firmware update or take other remedial action. The speaker retails for approximately $300 and is marketed as a premium PC audio solution.

Security professionals have expressed concern about the discovery. The ability to compromise a system through a trusted peripheral device represents a significant attack vector that many users would not anticipate. Unlike traditional malware that requires downloading suspicious files or visiting compromised websites, this exploit leverages hardware that users intentionally connect to their systems.

The revelation has prompted some computer users to reconsider their connected devices. Experts recommend that users disconnect Bluetooth speakers when not in use and maintain updated firmware for all peripheral devices. The incident highlights the expanding attack surface created by the growing number of connected devices in typical computing environments. As more hardware incorporates wireless connectivity, the potential for similar exploits in other devices remains a concern for cybersecurity professionals.

4:01 PM ET

The Cleveland Browns have traded star edge rusher Myles Garrett to the Los Angeles Rams in exchange for linebacker Jared Verse and three draft selections. The deal reshapes both franchises as the Browns continue their roster rebuild and the Rams pursue another championship run.

Garrett, 30, is a two-time NFL Defensive Player of the Year and seven-time Pro Bowl selection. Since Cleveland selected him first overall in 2017, he has established himself as one of the league's elite defensive players. He leads all players drafted since 2017 with 125.5 sacks, 413 pressures, and 149 tackles for loss. In 2025, Garrett set the single-season sack record with 23, earning All-Pro recognition even as the Browns finished 5-12 and missed the playoffs for the second consecutive year.

Garrett had requested a trade in 2024 after the Browns struggled to a 3-14 record, expressing his desire to play for a championship contender. Despite this request, he signed a four-year, $160 million extension one month later, making him the highest-paid non-quarterback in league history at the time. The deal includes $122.8 million guaranteed and an average annual salary of $40 million.

The trade became possible after the Browns and Garrett agreed to modify his contract in March, deferring option payments to the 2026-28 seasons. A payment originally due March 28 was moved to near the start of the regular season, providing the financial flexibility necessary for the move.

Browns general manager Andrew Berry acknowledged the difficult decision in a statement. "As discussions intensified we were stuck at a legitimate crossroads: do we hold on to a truly generational player who has become the identity of our team, or do we make the difficult decision that we think is best for the organization over the long run?" Berry said.

The Browns received a 2027 first-round selection, a 2028 second-round pick, and a 2029 third-round selection along with Verse. The 25-year-old linebacker was a first-round draft pick by the Rams in 2024 and earned Defensive Rookie of the Year honors. He has recorded 12 career sacks and 22 tackles for loss while also earning Pro Bowl selection this past season.

For the Rams, acquiring Garrett adds elite pass rush talent to a defense that lost to the eventual champion Seattle Seahawks in last season's NFC championship game. The franchise has a history of making significant trades to improve its roster, including the 2021 blockbuster quarterback trade with Detroit that brought Matthew Stafford to Los Angeles. Stafford led the Rams to a Super Bowl victory in his first season and won the Associated Press NFL MVP award last season. Garrett's arrival marks the first time the reigning MVP and Defensive Player of the Year will be teammates.

Anthony Head, the British actor best known for playing Rupert Giles in Buffy the Vampire Slayer, has died at age 72 from complications due to pneumonia. His daughters Emily and Daisy Head confirmed his passing in a statement, saying he "passed away peacefully, surrounded by his family."

Head's most iconic role was as the school librarian and mentor to Sarah Michelle Gellar's character throughout Buffy the Vampire Slayer's seven-season run from 1997 to 2003. The character of Giles, known as a "Watcher," became a beloved figure among fans of the supernatural series. Creator Joss Whedon had long planned a spinoff called Ripper that would have explored Giles' younger years, though the project never came to fruition.

Beyond his work on Buffy, Head had a remarkably diverse career spanning decades across theatre, film, and television. He appeared in the BBC sketch comedy show Little Britain, where he played a British prime minister constantly fawned over by a flirtatious assistant. He also featured in the BBC's Merlin series and took film roles in The Iron Lady and The Inbetweeners Movie.

More recently, Head played Rupert Mannion, a former football club owner, in the Apple TV+ series Ted Lasso, where he worked alongside co-creator Jason Sudeikis and was praised for their on-screen chemistry.

Head was born in Camden, London, to artistic parents. His father founded the documentary production company Verity Films, while his mother was an actor who appeared in the 1960s BBC crime drama Maigret. His brother was also an actor known for his lead role in the 1971 film Sunday Bloody Sunday. This creative environment shaped Head's early life, with his mother making elaborate dressing-up costumes that completely immersed the young actor in character play.

He began his professional career on stage, starring in a 1978 West End revival of Godspell. His theatre work became a cornerstone of his career, including roles in productions of Chess, where he played Freddie Trumper, and The Rocky Horror Show, where he starred as Dr. Frank-N-Furter in 1990. In the 1980s, he also sang backing vocals for the British pop group Red Box, appearing on their 1986 debut album.

Head gained significant recognition in Britain and America through Nescafé Gold Blend television advertisements that featured a slow-burn romance between two neighbours. When he took the Buffy role, he relocated to Los Angeles while his partner Sarah and two young daughters remained in England. He made frequent trips home, arranging his filming schedule to allow visits every three to four weeks.

His daughters remembered him as someone who loved his work and felt fortunate to work with talented colleagues across productions spanning several decades. They noted that his legacy would continue through the shows he appeared in and the audiences who cherish them.

A federal prosecutor in California announced multiple investigations into alleged voter fraud following accusations by President Trump that Democrats engaged in misconduct during this week's primary elections. Bill Essayli, the first assistant U.S. attorney for the Central District of California, revealed the probes on Friday without disclosing specifics about what allegations are being examined.

The announcement came as California completed its gubernatorial primary election, where Democrats Katie Porter, Tom Steyer, and Xavier Becerra and Republicans Chad Bianco and Steve Hilton competed for their party nominations to replace Governor Gavin Newsom. Trump made public accusations of election fraud against Democrats in the state after primary voting concluded.

The Justice Department sent a federal prosecutor to observe ballot processing in Los Angeles on Friday as Trump continued to claim that California Democrats were "rigging" the results. State officials have rejected the allegations, though the delay in election results has prompted concerns about misinformation regarding California's election integrity.

Election officials have explained that counting votes in California is taking longer than in other states due to the volume of ballots and processing procedures. Experts have warned that ballot counting could extend for weeks or even a month before final results are determined in the governor's race, the Los Angeles mayoral election, and congressional contests.

Essayli's announcement represents a notable development in the ongoing tensions between the Trump administration and California's Democratic leadership. The Central District of California, one of four federal judicial districts in the state, covers a significant portion of Southern California including Los Angeles, Orange, Riverside, San Bernardino, San Luis Obispo, Santa Barbara, and Ventura counties.

Federal election fraud investigations are relatively uncommon and typically require substantial evidence of organized attempts to interfere with voting processes or ballot counting. The Justice Department has not disclosed information about the specific nature of the fraud allegations or which aspects of the primary election are under investigation.

The timing of the investigations, announced immediately after the primary and following presidential accusations, represents an unusual convergence of federal law enforcement activity and electoral politics. California has traditionally been a Democratic stronghold, and disputes between state officials and the Trump administration have become a consistent element of recent political dynamics. Trump has long promoted election conspiracy theories and made similar claims of fraud in previous elections without providing evidence.

Creative, the manufacturer of the Sound Blaster Katana V2X, does not consider the behavior a security vulnerability. Security professionals have expressed concern about the discovery. The ability to compromise a system through a trusted peripheral device represents a significant attack vector that many users would not anticipate. Unlike traditional malware that requires downloading suspicious files or visiting compromised websites, this exploit leverages hardware that users intentionally connect to their systems.

The revelation has prompted some computer users to reconsider their connected devices. Experts recommend that users disconnect Bluetooth speakers when not in use and maintain updated firmware for all peripheral devices. The incident highlights the expanding attack surface created by the growing number of connected devices in typical computing environments. As more hardware incorporates wireless connectivity, the potential for similar exploits in other devices remains a concern for cybersecurity professionals.

Iranian national team players have been granted US visas to participate in the 2026 World Cup despite ongoing diplomatic tensions.

4:30 PM ET

The Iranian national soccer team has secured US visas to compete in the 2026 World Cup, a significant development given the strained diplomatic relations between the two countries. The approval allows the team to participate in the tournament despite ongoing tensions.

The visa approvals come as many Iranian fans face barriers to attending matches in person due to visa rejections and the broader conflict between the nations. Los Angeles' Iranian community, known as Tehrangeles, now has a team to follow in the tournament.

Lincoln Memorial Reflecting Pool refilled after Trump-ordered repainting

The Reflecting Pool in Washington has been refilled after President Trump had it painted in what he called American flag blue.

5:12 PM ET

The Lincoln Memorial Reflecting Pool has been refilled following a repainting project ordered by President Trump. The pool was drained to allow workers to coat it in a color named "American flag blue."

After weeks of renovation work, visitors to the iconic National Mall landmark have begun inspecting the results. Many have noted that the changes are subtle, with some questioning what difference the expensive project made to the pool's appearance.

Capcom Announces Resident Evil: Veronica Remake for 2027

The remake of the 2000 survival horror game Code: Veronica will launch next year.

5:17 PM ET

Capcom will release Resident Evil: Veronica in 2027, a remake of the 2000 game Resident Evil Code: Veronica. The announcement came during a gaming showcase event.

The original Code: Veronica took place three months after Resident Evil 2 and concurrently with Resident Evil 3: Nemesis. The story follows Claire Redfield, a character from the franchise's earlier installments.
