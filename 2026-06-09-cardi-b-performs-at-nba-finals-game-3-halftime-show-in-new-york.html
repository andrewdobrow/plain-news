Cardi B Performs at NBA Finals Game 3 Halftime Show in New York

Cardi B performed during the halftime show of NBA Finals Game 3 at Madison Square Garden. The star performed 'Bodega Baddie' and 'Bodak Yellow' during what was described as a hard-hitting set.

The performance represented a hometown moment for the Bronx-born rapper, who has long championed her New York roots in her music and public appearances. The halftime show added to the entertainment spectacle surrounding the Finals game in New York City.
