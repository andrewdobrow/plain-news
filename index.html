According to the military's statement, intelligence had confirmed the vessel was traveling along known drug trafficking routes in the eastern Pacific and was engaged in narco-trafficking operations. The lack of transparent evidence identifying targeted vessels as drug smuggling operations has sparked debate among experts and advocates both domestically and internationally. Critics argue that the military is using lethal force as its primary method of interdiction rather than attempting to board vessels or take individuals into custody.

The campaign continues despite the Trump administration managing multiple international conflicts, including the war with Iran. The administration has justified the increased military action in maritime zones as necessary to combat narcotics smuggling operations in the region, framing the strikes as counterterrorism measures.

The operations highlight a broader tension between counternarcotics enforcement and international humanitarian concerns, with the mounting death toll raising questions about oversight and accountability in these military actions.
