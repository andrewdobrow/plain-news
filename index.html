1:09 AM ET

A Blue Origin New Glenn rocket exploded during a static fire test at Cape Canaveral Space Force Station's Launch Complex 36 in Florida on Thursday evening. The explosion occurred at 9pm ET seconds after the scheduled "hotfire" test began, creating a massive fireball that destroyed the launchpad. The blast was visible across Florida's space coast, with an orange glow seen as far south as Fort Pierce, 185 kilometers away.

The incident threatens NASA's ambitious timeline for lunar exploration. NASA had just announced on Tuesday that Blue Origin won a contract to launch the first of three planned missions this year to begin construction of a $20 billion moon base. The New Glenn rocket is also the vehicle Blue Origin intends to use to deliver landers to the moon for NASA's Artemis program, including crewed landers for future astronaut missions.

Jeff Bezos, Blue Origin's founder, confirmed that all company employees were safe and uninjured. He described it as "a very rough day" on social media, stating that the company was already investigating the cause. "It's too early to know the root cause but we're already working to find it," Bezos said. "Very rough day, but we'll rebuild whatever needs rebuilding and get back to flying. It's worth it."

NASA Administrator Jared Isaacman acknowledged the difficulty of developing heavy-lift rockets. "Spaceflight is unforgiving, and developing new heavy-lift launch capability is extraordinarily difficult," he posted. NASA said it would conduct a full evaluation of timeline impacts following a thorough investigation of the anomaly.

The explosion represents another significant setback for Blue Origin as it competes with SpaceX for NASA contracts. Last month, a payload from the third New Glenn flight ended up in the wrong orbit, prompting the Federal Aviation Administration to ground the rocket temporarily. Thursday's test was the first static fire test since the FAA cleared the vehicle to return to flight the previous week.

The incident has implications for NASA's lunar goals. The Artemis III mission, planned for 2027, will test Blue Origin's Blue Moon lander. Artemis IV, scheduled for 2028, will mark the first time humans return to the moon since 1972, with either Blue Origin or SpaceX providing the lunar lander.

Shockwaves from the explosion were felt across Florida's Atlantic coast, with residents reporting shaking homes in Cape Canaveral and Cocoa Beach. Emergency officials reported no threat from fumes or other hazards, though flames continued burning at the launchpad more than two hours after the blast. Residents and aerospace observers captured images and videos of the fiery plume that spread across the night sky.

Death toll reaches nine in Washington paper mill explosion

Yesterday, 9:54 PM ET

Nine people are confirmed dead after a massive chemical tank explosion at a paper mill in Longview, Washington, with two workers still unaccounted for and presumed dead, according to reports from multiple news outlets.

The disaster occurred on Tuesday morning at the Nippon Dynawave Packaging Co facility when a tank containing more than 500,000 gallons of chemical mixture ruptured. The explosion released a caustic liquid capable of causing severe burns and lung injuries. Eight other people were injured in the blast, including a firefighter who responded to the emergency.

The chemical tank contained a caustic mixture used in the kraft pulping process to break down wood chips into paper pulp. White liquor, as the substance is called, can cause severe chemical burns on contact with skin and serious lung damage if inhaled.

Matt Amos, Longview fire battalion chief, explained that the six workers whose remains were initially recovered had been congregating in an area where employees gather during shift changes as they await their work assignments.

Among those confirmed dead was Gilbert Bernal, a grandfather who worked as an electrician at the plant. According to his friend Todd Cornwell, Bernal was known for his generosity. "He was one of the most genuinely good people that you've ever met. He would give you the shirt off his back if you needed it. He was always there willing to help in whatever needed to be done," Cornwell said.

CJ Doran, 26, was among those presumed dead. According to a verified fundraising post, he was a husband described as "the spiritual leader of their family, the joy of their home, and the family provider."

Other victims included John Forsberg, a father of two young children, and Jared Ammons, who had two children with another on the way. Braydon Finkas, an electrician at the plant, was also killed. His friend Rex Czuba described Finkas as deeply involved in his community. "He was a really big part of the town. He really jumped in and became a part of the community so quickly," Czuba said.

Recovery efforts have been slow and deliberate due to hazards posed by remaining chemicals and other industrial dangers. Crews have been avoiding the area closest to the tank out of concern for further collapse and have worked with engineers to determine whether damaged buildings around the tank are safe to enter. Workers must decontaminate remains before turning them over to the coroner's office for identification.

Authorities said the cause of the disaster remains under investigation and have not released the names of all victims. However, friends and relatives have begun confirming identities and posting online fundraisers to support the families of those killed.

The explosion represents one of the deadliest workplace accidents in the United States in recent decades.

Beyond Star Wars, Lucas edited several iconic films of the era. Her credits include American Graffiti and Taxi Driver, both of which showcased her skill in shaping narrative through cutting. She was married to Star Wars creator George Lucas at the time of the original trilogy's production, though the two later divorced.

Her editing contributions to Star Wars proved particularly influential. The film's fast-paced action sequences and emotional rhythms owed much to her decisions in the editing room. Along with her co-editors, she helped transform hours of footage into a coherent and thrilling adventure that would redefine popular cinema.

Lucas worked during a transformative period in American filmmaking. The 1970s saw a wave of director-driven films that challenged Hollywood conventions, and editors like Lucas played a crucial role in bringing those visions to the screen. Her work on Taxi Driver, directed by Martin Scorsese, demonstrated her versatility in handling both intimate character studies and large-scale spectacles.

The Oscar she received for Star Wars recognized not just technical proficiency but creative vision. The editing choices made during post-production helped establish the rhythm and energy that audiences responded to, turning a space fantasy into a cultural phenomenon.

Lucasfilm's tribute acknowledged her significant contributions to the Star Wars legacy. Her work on the original film laid the foundation for one of the most successful franchises in entertainment history, influencing generations of filmmakers and editors who followed.
