Energy-intensive industries have been particularly affected by the cost pressures. Manufacturing operations requiring significant power inputs have seen profitability decline, leading some facilities to scale back production. This has contributed to the overall stagnation in factory activity.

The latest figures will likely inform future policy decisions as Chinese officials work to shore up economic growth. Manufacturing represents a crucial component of the country's economy, and sustained weakness could have broader implications for employment and investment.

3:01 AM ET

Microsoft has drawn criticism from the cybersecurity community after threatening researchers with criminal investigation for disclosing security exploits. The company's response to vulnerability disclosures has prompted backlash from security professionals who say the approach discourages responsible reporting of software flaws.

Multiple security researchers have reported receiving threats of legal action from Microsoft after identifying and disclosing vulnerabilities in the company's products. One researcher described feeling humiliated by the company's response and pledged what they called a significant data release in retaliation.

The dispute centers on Microsoft's handling of zero-day exploits, which are previously unknown vulnerabilities that can be used to attack systems before patches are available. Security researchers typically follow a practice of responsible disclosure, notifying companies privately about flaws before publishing details publicly.

Microsoft reportedly contacted law enforcement about at least one researcher's activities. The move has been characterized by observers as a departure from standard industry practice, where companies typically work collaboratively with security researchers to address vulnerabilities.

Security expert Kevin Beaumont described Microsoft's approach to zero-day exploits as problematic, suggesting the company bears responsibility for the current situation. The tech publication The Verge reported that Microsoft is threatening legal action specifically over the disclosure of exploits.

The Register, a technology news site, reported that at least one researcher expressed fear of personal consequences, saying Microsoft's actions could ruin their life. The researcher had been working to identify security flaws in Microsoft products.

The incident has sparked broader debate within the cybersecurity industry about the relationship between technology companies and independent security researchers. Critics argue that threatening researchers discourages the identification and reporting of vulnerabilities, potentially leaving users exposed to security risks.

Microsoft has not publicly detailed its criteria for when it pursues legal action against researchers. The company's stance has been described by multiple outlets as creating uncertainty among security professionals about how to safely report vulnerabilities they discover in Microsoft products.

Victor Wembanyama led the Spurs to victory with 22 points and seven rebounds in the decisive game played Saturday night. The 7-foot-4 French center was named Western Conference Finals MVP for his performance throughout the series. After the game, Wembanyama became emotional about reaching the Finals. "Realizing that some part of a childhood dream is going to come true," he said when asked about his tears. "We're still hungry, we want more. This feeling is so powerful, I can't explain it."

While Wembanyama's performance was crucial, his teammates also made significant contributions. Julian Champagnie scored 20 points, including six three-pointers, while Dylan Harper provided important minutes off the bench. Luke Kornet, Wembanyama's backup, made a crucial block at the rim during the fourth quarter as Oklahoma City mounted a comeback attempt.

Champagnie praised the team's collective effort after the victory. "We never knew if we were going to get this far but when you've got the greatest player in the world things happen," he said.

For the Thunder, Shai Gilgeous-Alexander, the reigning NBA MVP, delivered a strong performance with 35 points and nine assists. However, none of his teammates scored more than 20 points as Oklahoma City's championship reign came to an end. The Thunder played without Jalen Williams due to a hamstring injury.

Thunder coach Mark Daigneault acknowledged the loss but remained confident about his team's future. "You have to grow from every experience, including the tough ones," he said. "And it's the NBA, there are tough ones. We can also be really disappointed. There's nobody that we don't think we can beat, respectfully."

The Spurs' Game 7 victory on the road is particularly notable, as such wins are rare in playoff basketball. San Antonio will face the Knicks in the NBA Finals with Game 1 scheduled for Wednesday in San Antonio. The matchup holds historical significance, as the last time these teams met in the Finals was in 1999, when the Spurs won the series 4-1.

The Spurs have built their recent roster around Wembanyama, who was acquired through the draft, as they seek to return to championship success for the first time since winning the title in 2014. The young center has established himself as the centerpiece of their championship contention, demonstrating defensive and offensive prowess throughout their playoff run.
