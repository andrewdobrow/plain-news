President Trump appointed Bill Pulte, head of the Federal Housing Finance Agency, to serve as acting director of national intelligence, replacing Tulsi Gabbard who departed from the role last month. The decision sparked immediate criticism from Democrats and concern from Republicans about Pulte's suitability for the position.

Pulte brings no known national security or intelligence experience to the role. His background is centered on housing finance and real estate, including his leadership of the FHFA, which regulates government-backed mortgage lenders Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac. Trump announced the appointment on his Truth Social platform, stating that Pulte has "deep experience managing the most sensitive matters in America." Pulte will retain his position at the FHFA while serving as acting intelligence director.

The appointment has raised concerns among both parties. Senate Majority Leader John Thune told reporters that the intelligence community needs professionals and warned that if Pulte is nominated for permanent confirmation, he will face "a lengthy road ahead" including hearings and a confirmation process.

The concern extends beyond Pulte's lack of experience. During his tenure at the FHFA, Pulte has made public allegations against Trump's political opponents, including accusations of mortgage fraud against Federal Reserve Governor Lisa Cook, who has denied wrongdoing. Thune's comments suggesting the need for professionals rather than a "weaponized" intelligence director appeared to reference these concerns about Pulte pursuing Trump's rivals.

Pulte comes from significant wealth as the heir to PulteGroup, a major home construction company founded by his grandfather. However, he no longer holds any official connection to the company after being pushed off the board in 2020. His own family has publicly distanced itself from him. The PulteGroup's charitable foundation issued a 2023 statement clarifying that Pulte does not represent the organization and does not serve in any capacity for it.

The Office of the Director of National Intelligence oversees the nation's intelligence community, coordinating information from agencies including the CIA, NSA, and FBI. The position typically requires extensive experience in national security operations. The office coordinates threat assessments and national security briefings across multiple federal intelligence agencies.

Gabbard's departure left the intelligence director position vacant. Trump had been discussing replacing her with cabinet members as early as April, according to people briefed on the discussions. Gabbard's time in the role was marked by conflict, including being largely sidelined during Trump's foreign policy actions concerning Venezuela and Iran.

The acting appointment allows Pulte to serve temporarily without Senate confirmation, though a permanent director would require approval from the Senate Intelligence Committee and full Senate vote.

Russia employed an enormous arsenal in the assault, firing 476 drones and 48 missiles at Ukrainian targets. The strikes hit energy and transport infrastructure across multiple regions, forcing emergency power cuts as residents faced frigid temperatures. Explosions were reported in the western city of Lviv and the northwestern city of Kharkiv, while residents in Kyiv sought shelter in metro stations.

"Every brazen attack against ordinary life shows that the pressure on Russia is insufficient," Zelenskyy said. "Effective sanctions and assistance to Ukraine can change this."

The timing of the bombardment coincides with reports of a new peace proposal being quietly developed by American and Russian officials. According to reports, the draft plan would require Ukraine to surrender territory it controls in the east and significantly reduce its military size. Zelenskyy has previously rejected such conditions as non-starters.

The attack underscores Ukraine's continued vulnerability to Russian aerial assaults despite air defense systems provided by Western allies. Ukrainian officials have repeatedly requested additional air-defense missiles to protect population centers from such strikes.

The death toll may rise as rescue operations continue. Many people remain listed as missing in the rubble of destroyed residential buildings. The scale of the assault, involving hundreds of drones and dozens of missiles, demonstrates Russia's sustained capacity to launch large-scale coordinated attacks deep into Ukrainian territory.

The bombardment also coincides with heightened tensions in the broader region. SpaceX Merges with xAI Ahead of Major Public Offering

SpaceX has acquired Elon Musk's artificial intelligence company xAI in a $1.25 trillion merger that combines the aerospace firm with AI capabilities, chatbot technology, and the social media platform X. The deal values SpaceX at $1 trillion and xAI at $250 billion, positioning the combined entity for a stock market listing expected to value it well above $1 trillion.

The merger consolidates multiple parts of Musk's business empire as SpaceX prepares to go public. According to the companies' joint announcement, the combination creates "the most ambitious, vertically-integrated innovation engine on (and off) Earth, with AI, rockets, space-based internet, direct-to-mobile device communications and the world's foremost real-time information and free speech platform."

The deal reflects Musk's strategy of developing space-based datacenters powered by solar satellites to support artificial intelligence operations. The companies stated that current AI advances depend on massive terrestrial datacenters requiring enormous amounts of power and cooling. "Global electricity demand for AI simply cannot be met with terrestrial solutions, even in the near term, without imposing hardship on communities and the environment," the announcement said. "In the long term, space-based AI is obviously the only way to scale."

The public offering is expected for early summer, timed to coincide with a planetary alignment and Musk's 55th birthday on June 28, when Jupiter and Venus will appear in close proximity.

SpaceX's valuation has grown substantially. In December, the company sent a letter to investors indicating an expected value of $800 billion. The new merger agreement reflects a significant increase from that figure. Meanwhile, xAI's valuation has also risen dramatically during the AI boom, with the company announcing a $20 billion Series E fundraise last month that valued it at $230 billion.

Musk confirmed the acquisition on Monday with a brief "yes" response to a post on X referencing merger reports that Reuters had first disclosed.

The deal comes as multiple major technology companies prepare for public offerings. Anthropic, the AI firm behind the Claude chatbot, has confidentially filed for an initial public offering and announced raising $65 billion in funding that valued the company at $965 billion. OpenAI is also expected to pursue a public listing this year.

Speculation about the SpaceX-xAI merger has circulated for days ahead of Monday's announcement. The news provides positive momentum for Musk following Tesla's recent earnings report showing declining revenues and a struggling automotive business. The combined SpaceX-xAI entity will integrate Starlink satellite internet, rocket launches for commercial and government customers, and AI infrastructure into a single operation preparing for Wall Street debut.
