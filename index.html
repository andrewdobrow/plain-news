Iran announced it will stop exchanging messages with the United States through intermediaries and move to close the Strait of Hormuz following weekend military clashes between the two nations. The announcement came after American and Iranian forces traded fire, with US Central Command stating it intercepted Iranian ballistic missiles targeting American troops in Kuwait. The US subsequently struck Iranian radar and drone sites after Iran downed an American drone.

The escalation marks a significant setback to indirect negotiations that had been underway to de-escalate regional tensions. Iranian officials accused the Trump administration of ceasefire violations, citing Israeli military operations in Lebanon against Hezbollah as part of their justification for suspending talks.

The situation has rapidly deteriorated beyond the initial weekend exchanges. Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps seized two ships in the Strait of Hormuz, the Panama-flagged MSC Francesca and Liberia-flagged Epaminondas, claiming they were attempting to exit the waterway covertly. This marked the first time Iran has seized vessels since the conflict began on February 28. The seizures follow US actions against Iranian vessels, including firing on and seizing an Iranian cargo ship and boarding an Iranian oil tanker in the Indian Ocean.

Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf, speaker of the Iranian parliament and lead negotiator, stated that reopening the Strait of Hormuz would be impossible while the US and Israel committed what he called flagrant ceasefire breaches. He characterized the US naval blockade as the hostage-taking of the world's economy and warned that neither military aggression nor bullying would help America achieve its goals.

The Strait of Hormuz handles roughly 20 percent of global oil and liquefied natural gas during peacetime. The closure has created severe economic consequences worldwide. About 20,000 seafarers and 2,000 ships have become stranded in the Gulf region, according to the International Maritime Organization. Energy shortages have cascaded through global markets, with fuel price hikes triggering inflation concerns across multiple economies. Germany halved its 2026 growth forecast to 0.5 percent, while Greece announced 500 million euros in additional aid for households and farmers struggling with increased costs.

Planned peace negotiations in Islamabad failed to materialize. Pakistan had prepared to host talks with a US delegation led by Vice President JD Vance, but the Iranian delegation never departed for the meeting and the American delegation remained in Washington.

President Trump responded by insisting that negotiations would continue and that everything would work out well, despite Iran's announcement of suspended talks. The White House press secretary said Trump was satisfied with the naval blockade and understood Iran was in a weak position, claiming Iran loses approximately 500 million dollars daily through economic restrictions.

The breakdown in communications comes as earlier hopes for a diplomatic resolution have dimmed considerably.

Oil prices climbed above $97 a barrel following a fresh exchange of military strikes between the United States and Iran, creating significant volatility in energy markets. The price surge reflects investor concerns about potential further escalation in the Middle East, a region critical to global energy supplies.

The spike in oil prices came as both countries continued diplomatic communications, introducing uncertainty about whether the military exchanges might lead to broader conflict or be contained through negotiations. The Strait of Hormuz has become a focal point for tensions, with the U.S. conducting strikes on Iranian radar and drone control sites while Iran targeted American forces in Kuwait.

Stock markets displayed mixed reactions to the geopolitical turmoil. The Dow Jones Industrial Average declined while the S&P 500 remained relatively flat near record levels. Technology stocks, particularly Nvidia and Microsoft, provided support for equity indices as investor enthusiasm about artificial intelligence applications helped offset concerns tied to the military conflict.

The impact on fuel prices has been substantial across multiple regions. In Northern Ireland, petrol prices jumped 19 percent since late February, while diesel rose 35 percent. Filling a 50-liter tank now costs an average of 75 pounds for petrol and 91 pounds for diesel, compared with 63 pounds and 67 pounds respectively at the end of February. Across the broader UK, petrol prices have climbed 16 percent and diesel 30 percent since the conflict began. Northern Ireland has experienced some of Europe's largest fuel price increases, with only seven other European countries recording sharper gains in petrol prices.

In England, the north saw the sharpest regional increases in petrol prices, with drivers paying an average of 154 pence per liter, up 17 percent from 132 pence per liter when hostilities began. Some rural stations in England and Scotland are charging between 180 and 210 pence per liter.

Major retailers have all raised prices significantly. Shell stations now charge an average of 158 pence per liter for standard unleaded petrol, while BP and Esso-branded stations average 157 and 155 pence respectively. These represent increases of 15 to 16 percent from late February levels.

The conflict has also disrupted European manufacturing. Eurozone factories reported their largest jump in input costs in four years during May, driven by higher energy and raw material prices stemming from the Iran war. Supply chain delays have risen to their highest levels since the pandemic, adding further upward pressure on prices.

Energy price increases have broad economic implications, affecting transportation costs, manufacturing expenses, and consumer spending. Rising fuel costs could intensify inflationary pressures, influencing Federal Reserve policy decisions. Market participants continue monitoring both the military situation and diplomatic progress to assess the trajectory of energy prices in coming weeks.

A meteor entered the atmosphere and exploded off the coast of Massachusetts, producing loud booms heard across parts of the United States and Canada. The event prompted widespread reports from residents who witnessed the bright flash or heard the sonic boom as the space rock broke apart.

Footage captured the meteor as it streaked through the atmosphere before exploding. NASA reported that fragments of the meteorite may have landed in Cape Cod Bay, potentially making them recoverable for scientific study. The space agency has not yet confirmed whether any recovery efforts are underway.

Meteors enter Earth's atmosphere regularly, but most burn up completely before reaching the ground. When larger fragments survive the journey and land on Earth, they are called meteorites. The explosion that produced the audible booms occurs when the meteor breaks apart under intense heat and pressure as it travels through the atmosphere at high speed.

The widespread reports from both the US and Canada indicate the meteor was particularly bright and the explosion was powerful enough to generate shock waves audible over a large geographic area. Such events, while uncommon, provide valuable opportunities for scientists to study material from space that has remained largely unchanged since the formation of the solar system.

NASA and other agencies often rely on eyewitness reports and video footage to help determine the trajectory and potential landing sites of meteorites. If fragments are recovered from Cape Cod Bay, they could offer insights into the composition and origin of the meteor.

Raymond Berry, a Hall of Fame receiver who became one of professional football's most accomplished figures, has died at age 93. His career spanned decades and included success both as a player and coach.

Berry played his entire career with the Baltimore Colts, where he established himself as one of the most prolific pass catchers in NFL history. He led the league in receptions three times and became known for his precise route running and reliability as a target. His partnership with quarterback Johnny Unitas became one of football's most celebrated receiver-quarterback combinations, helping define the Colts' dominance during the 1950s and 1960s.

After his playing career ended, Berry transitioned to coaching and eventually became head coach of the New England Patriots. During his tenure with the team, he guided them to a Super Bowl appearance, adding another chapter to an already impressive football legacy. His success on both sides of the sideline demonstrated a comprehensive understanding of the sport that few have matched.

Berry's induction into the Pro Football Hall of Fame recognized his impact on the game. He became known throughout his career for meticulous preparation and attention to detail, characteristics that defined his approach to the receiver position. His techniques and work ethic became studied by coaches and players alike, influencing how the position developed over time.

The passing of Berry represents a significant loss to professional football. He bridges multiple eras of the sport, from the Colts' championship years to his later work with the Patriots. His contributions as a player who revolutionized receiving and as a coach who led competitive teams span generations of football history. Berry's career came during a formative period when the NFL was establishing itself as a major American sports institution, and he played a meaningful role in that development.

His precise approach to route running and his partnership with Unitas are remembered as foundational moments in NFL history. The combination of their talents demonstrated how preparation and execution could create excellence on the field. Berry brought those same principles to his coaching, implementing the same dedication to detail that had made him a successful player.

Throughout his life, Berry exemplified the evolution of professional football from its earlier years into the modern era. His impact extended beyond individual statistics or wins and losses, instead influencing how future generations of players and coaches approached their craft. The combination of his Hall of Fame playing career and significant coaching accomplishments made him one of the sport's most complete figures.

Berry's death removes another direct connection to the NFL's earliest decades, when the sport was building its foundation as America's dominant professional athletic competition. His legacy will endure through the players and coaches he influenced and the standards he set for excellence at the receiver position.

HBO's drama series Euphoria has concluded after three seasons, marking the end of a show that became a cultural phenomenon since its 2019 debut. The final season aired recently, though critical reception has been decidedly negative, with reviewers calling it a bleak and exploitative work that fails to justify its lengthy five-year wait between seasons.

The show's production faced significant delays due to the COVID-19 pandemic and Los Angeles fires, resulting in only 18 episodes across its entire run. Cast members appeared visibly reluctant during the press tour, with minimal social media engagement and Zendaya describing the filming experience as a "whirlwind" in a notably ambiguous interview.

Set five years after season two's conclusion, the third season follows recovering addict Rue (Zendaya) as she becomes entangled with drug dealer Laurie, working as a mule smuggling drugs between Mexico and the US. The storyline employs cinematic sequences drawing from westerns and blaxploitation films. Rue finds herself working for a club owner named Alamo while unable to escape her dire circumstances.

Meanwhile, Cassie (Sydney Sweeney) pursues traditional domesticity with Nate (Jacob Elordi), though their relationship is complicated by Cassie's interest in creating OnlyFans content. The show's treatment of sex work feels simultaneously voyeuristic and judgmental, critics argue, while casting choices like Grammy winner Rosalía as a stereotypical stripper character reinforce problematic representations.

Creator Sam Levinson has described the series as exploring themes of surrender and spirituality, while also serving as a tribute to late actor Angus Cloud, who played Fezco and died in 2023. The season represents Eric Dane's final on-screen role as Nate's father, Cal.

The relationship between Rue and Jules (Hunter Schafer), once celebrated as authentic queer representation on television, has devolved into another vehicle for exploring the sex economy. Critics note that Jules, previously developed as an artist and compelling character, becomes a blank canvas for male fantasy rather than a fully realized person.

While performances from Zendaya, Sweeney, and Adewale Akinnuoye-Agbaje are described as excellent, the season itself has been criticized as grimly determined to shock viewers without purpose. The show that once balanced surreal storytelling with dark humor now relies primarily on depicting characters in unrelenting misery, leaving cast members visibly eager to move on from the project.

HBO has not announced plans for spinoffs or continuations, suggesting Euphoria's story has concluded definitively.
