Apple releases upgraded Siri with AI capabilities

Apple announced a major overhaul of its Siri voice assistant at its annual developer conference, integrating the tool with Apple Intelligence and rebranding it as "Siri AI." The upgrade will become widely available in the fall, marking a significant shift in the company's approach to artificial intelligence.

The revamped Siri will function more like modern AI chatbots such as ChatGPT and Google Gemini rather than a traditional web-based question-and-answer tool. Mike Rockwell, Apple's vice-president of Siri engineering, demonstrated the assistant's new capabilities during the keynote, showing how it can handle complex, multi-step requests within a single interface. In one example, Rockwell asked Siri to identify a beach from a photograph, then requested a nearby friend's address and directions to the location, all completed without switching between apps.

The partnership powering Siri AI involves Google's Gemini model as part of a billion-dollar collaboration between the two tech giants. The new assistant will have a dedicated app and can access Apple's native applications across mobile devices and computers to assist with daily planning, information retrieval and navigation. The tool will also perform tasks including proofreading, shopping, adding calendar events and extracting nutrition information from photos of food.

Apple acknowledged that it has lagged behind competitors in deploying AI features over the past decade. Craig Federighi, Apple's senior vice-president of software engineering, noted that while some companies are "racing forward" with AI development, Apple prioritizes creating technology that serves users' actual needs.

The company's timing comes after a previous false start. Apple initially promised an updated Siri in 2024 but delayed the release, and later settled a class-action lawsuit for 250 million dollars over allegations that it had misrepresented the assistant's capabilities. Some consumers received payouts of up to 95 dollars.

Beyond the Siri upgrade, Apple introduced new child safety features designed to give parents greater control over their children's device usage. The company is implementing tools to limit content visibility, restrict who children can contact and control app access. A new safety feature in Safari will require parental approval before children can browse unfamiliar websites. The system will also blur messages containing gore or violence.

The child safety announcement follows increased scrutiny over harmful content, including so-called "nudification" apps. Apple partnered with the American Academy of Pediatrics to provide parents with recommended screen time limits.

The Siri AI announcement came during Tim Cook's final keynote as CEO. Cook is stepping down in the fall after leading Apple for 15 years, during which the company became one of the world's most profitable publicly traded corporations.
