Apple overhauls Siri with AI capabilities at annual developer conference

Apple unveiled a major redesign of its voice assistant Siri at its Worldwide Developers Conference 2026, introducing AI-powered features that transform the assistant from a voice-controlled tool into what the company calls an AI companion. The announcement came during the opening keynote at Apple Park, marking a significant shift in how users will interact with Apple devices.

The updated assistant, referred to as Siri AI, can now perform more complex tasks beyond simple voice commands. Apple is adding AI-powered features across multiple apps including Safari, Shortcuts, and the Password app. The company also announced that Siri will receive its own dedicated app, giving the assistant a more prominent presence in the iOS ecosystem.

The conference, which kicked off at 10 a.m. Pacific Time, included announcements about iOS 27 and Apple Intelligence alongside the Siri updates. The week-long event features developer sessions and product demonstrations. This year's conference holds particular significance as it marks CEO Tim Cook's final WWDC with the company.

Among the new features, Apple introduced a redesigned Shortcuts app that allows users to describe workflows in plain language prompts rather than manually building them. The company also revealed a Siri in Camera feature designed to simplify bill splitting by allowing users to point their iPhone at a restaurant bill and select their items to split the tab via Apple Cash. Apple VP of Software Sebastien Marineau-Mes demonstrated this feature during the presentation.

Additional updates announced at the conference include improvements to the Photos app with new AI editing features, including a spatial Reframe tool that uses AI to adjust perspectives. The Health app received updates that enable it to detect perimenopause based on cycle patterns. Apple also introduced systemwide dictation capabilities and expanded parental controls with more detailed screen time management features. The Image Playground, Apple's AI image generator, received updates aimed at making it more competitive with other AI image tools.
