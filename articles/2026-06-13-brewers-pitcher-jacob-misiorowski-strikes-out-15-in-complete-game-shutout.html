Brewers pitcher Jacob Misiorowski strikes out 15 in complete-game shutout

Milwaukee Brewers pitcher Jacob Misiorowski delivered an exceptional performance on the mound, striking out 15 batters in a complete-game shutout against the Philadelphia Phillies. The Brewers won 6-0, with Misiorowski allowing just one hit over nine innings.

The right-hander set a record for strikeouts in a complete-game shutout thrown under 100 pitches, a feat known as a Maddux. During the game, he threw a pitch clocked at 104.5 miles per hour, continuing to showcase the elite velocity that has defined his season. The performance marked his first career shutout.

What made Misiorowski's outing particularly notable was the radar gun readings throughout the contest. Reports indicated he reached 105 miles per hour on at least one pitch, which would represent the fastest pitch ever recorded by a starting pitcher in a major league game. His combination of overwhelming speed and precision kept the Phillies consistently off balance.

Misiorowski's game ranks among the greatest individual pitching performances in Brewers franchise history. Complete-game shutouts with double-digit strikeouts have become increasingly uncommon in modern baseball, where teams typically manage pitch counts carefully and rely heavily on bullpen support. His ability to maintain his velocity deep into the game stood out as particularly exceptional.

The complete-game shutout showcased a different approach to pitching than what has become standard in recent years. Misiorowski demonstrated that a starter with dominant stuff could still log a full nine innings while keeping his pitch count reasonable, a rarity in contemporary baseball strategy.

The victory strengthened Milwaukee's position in the National League playoff race. Misiorowski has emerged as a key component of the Brewers' starting rotation this season, and this dominant performance underscored his continued development into one of baseball's premier young pitchers. His combination of elite velocity and control suggests he will remain a central figure in the team's postseason plans.

The performance also added another chapter to the ongoing conversation about fastball velocity in modern baseball. As radar gun technology has improved and become more precise, velocity records have become increasingly common, yet Misiorowski's outing still managed to stand out as historically significant.

For the Brewers organization, the game provided an encouraging sign about the depth of their pitching staff heading into the stretch run of the season. Misiorowski's ability to pitch deep into games while striking out batters at an elite rate offers the team a potential advantage as they pursue postseason success.
