Five arrested for alleged plot to attack UFC event at White House

Five individuals have been arrested and charged with plotting to attack a UFC event at the White House, the Department of Justice announced. Officials alleged that the plotters discussed sending explosive-loaded drones to the event and shooting people who fled.

The arrests represent a serious security threat to a public event at the executive mansion. The UFC event at the White House marks an unusual venue for the mixed martial arts organization, though the source material does not specify when the event was scheduled to take place or whether it has been canceled or postponed in light of the alleged plot.

The alleged conspirators reportedly planned a coordinated attack using both aerial and ground tactics. According to officials, the group discussed deploying drones loaded with explosives to target the event venue while also planning to open fire on attendees attempting to escape. The Department of Justice did not release the identities of those arrested or specify what charges they face.

The investigation and arrests underscore ongoing concerns about security threats to high-profile government venues and events. The White House has hosted various sporting and entertainment events over the years, typically requiring extensive security screening and planning. The alleged plot involving drone technology reflects evolving security challenges faced by law enforcement and Secret Service personnel responsible for protecting the executive mansion and its grounds.

The Department of Justice has not provided additional details about how authorities uncovered the alleged plot, whether the suspects had acquired explosives or weapons, or how far advanced the planning had progressed before the arrests were made.
