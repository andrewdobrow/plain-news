Authorities disrupt alleged drone and gun attack planned for White House UFC event

Federal authorities arrested at least five people in connection with an alleged plot to attack a UFC cage-fighting event at the White House, according to the Justice Department. The suspects planned to use drones and firearms in the attack on the event, which President Trump has branded as Freedom 250.

An FBI criminal complaint details the alleged terror plot targeting the White House event. Among those arrested was a teenager, according to reports. The Secret Service and FBI worked together to identify and apprehend the suspects before any attack could be carried out.

A Secret Service official stated that the agency chose not to leak information about the alleged plot during the investigation. The decision to keep the threat confidential until arrests were made reflects standard security protocols for protecting high-profile events at the White House. The UFC event was scheduled to take place on White House grounds as part of a public celebration.

The arrests represent a significant national security incident involving a planned attack on the seat of American government. The White House has hosted various public events under the current administration, but an attack plot of this nature raises questions about security measures for large gatherings at the executive mansion. Federal prosecutors are expected to provide additional details about the charges and evidence as the case proceeds through the court system.

The investigation demonstrates coordination between multiple federal law enforcement agencies to counter domestic threats. Authorities have not disclosed whether the suspects had any prior criminal history or connections to known extremist groups. The case remains under active investigation as prosecutors build their case against the arrested individuals.
