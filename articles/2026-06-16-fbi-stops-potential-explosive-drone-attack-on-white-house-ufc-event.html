FBI stops attack plot targeting White House UFC event

Federal authorities disrupted an alleged terrorist plot targeting a UFC event at the White House, the Justice Department announced. Five men were arrested across multiple states in connection with the conspiracy to attack the "Freedom 250" event.

The arrests took place over the weekend in Ohio, Missouri, Nebraska, and California. According to charges filed by the Department of Justice, the five men allegedly planned a coordinated assault involving both aerial and ground-based weapons.

The suspects reportedly intended to deploy drones armed with explosives around the UFC event to force an evacuation. Following the evacuation, they allegedly planned to position snipers to fire upon "high value targets" within the fleeing crowd.

Acting Attorney General Todd Blanche emphasized the swift response by law enforcement. "The FBI, our law enforcement partners and our U.S. Attorneys did what they do every day to make America safe through quick response and vigilance in investigating, disrupting, and dismantling this alleged plan before it could be carried out," Blanche said. He added that the administration would "take immediate and aggressive action to identify and prosecute those who incite and plan acts of violence."

Court filings unsealed by the government reveal that the group expressed grievances about various issues, including corruption, the Epstein files, and data centers. These motivations were cited in the charges against the men.

The investigation identified the conspiracy before the event took place, allowing authorities to prevent the planned operation. The UFC Freedom 250 event proceeded as scheduled after the threat was neutralized.

Security experts have pointed to this incident as evidence of evolving terrorist tactics. The use of drones equipped with explosives represents a method that law enforcement agencies increasingly monitor as a security concern.

The FBI led the investigation that uncovered the plot, working alongside other law enforcement partners and U.S. Attorneys' offices to build their case. The multi-state nature of the operation required coordination between federal agencies across different jurisdictions.

The incident highlights the security challenges posed by large-scale public events held at sensitive government locations. The White House decision to host the UFC event meant implementing enhanced security measures to protect attendees and government officials present.

The investigation remains active as officials work to determine whether additional individuals were involved in planning or supporting the alleged attack. Authorities continue to gather evidence and interview witnesses to establish the full scope of the conspiracy.

The successful disruption of the plot underscores the ongoing work of federal law enforcement to identify and prevent violent attacks before they occur. The charges represent the government's commitment to prosecuting those involved in planning violence against public events and officials.
