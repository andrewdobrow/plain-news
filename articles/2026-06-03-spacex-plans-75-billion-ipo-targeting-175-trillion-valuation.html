SpaceX sets $135 share price for record-breaking public offering

SpaceX announced a target share price of $135 per share as it prepares for what would be the largest stock market debut in history. The space exploration company founded by Elon Musk set the price earlier than market analysts expected, according to multiple reports.

The offering will consist of 555.6 million shares at the $135 price point, establishing a total valuation of $1.77 trillion for the company. This valuation would make SpaceX worth more than any other company has been valued at during an initial public offering. The shares are expected to begin trading as early as Thursday, though the exact timing was not confirmed in available reports.

The planned IPO comes amid questions from some market observers about whether the company's valuation is justified. Some analysts have expressed concern that SpaceX may be significantly overvalued at the proposed price, though the company has maintained its operations in commercial spaceflight, satellite internet through its Starlink division, and contracts with government agencies including NASA.

SpaceX has been operating as a privately held company since its founding, with funding coming from private investment rounds. The decision to go public represents a significant shift in the company's capital structure and will subject it to the disclosure requirements and quarterly reporting obligations that come with being a publicly traded corporation.

The timing of the offering puts SpaceX on track to raise substantial capital that the company can use for its ongoing operations and future expansion plans. The successful completion of the IPO would give public market investors their first opportunity to buy shares in the space exploration company, which has grown from a startup into one of the most prominent players in the commercial space industry.
