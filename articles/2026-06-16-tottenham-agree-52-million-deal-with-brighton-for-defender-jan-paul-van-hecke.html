Brighton sign Croatia defender Vuskovic from Tottenham for club record £46 million

Brighton have completed the signing of Croatia defender Luka Vuskovic from Tottenham Hotspur for a club record fee of £46 million, with an additional £4 million available through easily achievable add-ons. The 19-year-old has agreed a five-year contract with the option of a further year.

Vuskovic becomes Brighton's most expensive signing, surpassing their previous transfer record. Tottenham retain matching rights should Brighton agree to sell the player in future, plus a 20 percent sell-on clause if he moves to another club.

The centre-back joined Tottenham last summer from Croatian club Hajduk Split for £10 million but never featured for the north London side. He spent the previous season on loan at Hamburg to gain playing experience before this permanent move to Brighton.

Despite his youth, Vuskovic has already earned recognition at international level. The defender became Croatia's youngest World Cup player when he started against England, demonstrating his readiness for top-level football despite his age.

Brighton have built a reputation for acquiring young talent and developing players before selling them on at significant profit. Vuskovic fits this established model, arriving as a prospect with international experience and considerable room for development. His signing reflects Brighton's continued strategy of investing in promising young players who can grow within their system.

The substantial fee paid by Brighton signals the club's confidence in the player's potential and their ambition in the transfer market. The club option for an additional year provides flexibility for both parties as Vuskovic settles into his new environment and competes for a starting position in Brighton's defence.

Vuskovic's transfer comes as part of significant activity in the transfer market involving several Premier League clubs. Tottenham, managed by Roberto De Zerbi, have been particularly active in rebuilding their squad following a disappointing 17th place finish last season. Beyond the Vuskovic sale, Spurs have strengthened their squad through multiple signings as they look to improve on their previous campaign.

Brighton's pursuit of Vuskovic demonstrates their continued investment in defensive reinforcements. The five-year commitment with the additional option shows the club's long-term planning and belief that the Croatian centre-back can develop into a key player for their future.

The signing represents Brighton's latest addition to their squad as they prepare for the upcoming season. Vuskovic will have the opportunity to establish himself as a regular starter in the Premier League following his loan spell abroad, providing him with consistent first-team football at the highest level of English football.
