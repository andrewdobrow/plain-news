Grand Theft Auto 6 earns 99 Metacritic average in critical reviews, tying industry record

Grand Theft Auto 6 has received a 99 out of 100 average score on review aggregator Metacritic, tying the record held by The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time since 1998. The open-world action game from Rockstar has drawn sweeping praise from critics ahead of its release, with reviewers calling it a landmark achievement that justifies 13 years of anticipation since the previous installment.

Critics highlighted the game's graphics, storytelling, and world building as groundbreaking. Gaming website IGN called the title unprecedented, unbelievable, and unmissable. Eurogamer awarded five stars and said Rockstar had pushed back the horizon of video games once again, with reviewer Chris Tapsell writing that the game is truly awesome. The Washington Post said the game raises the bar at every level, while Bloomberg reviewer Jason Schreier called it a masterpiece and wrote that he often found himself wondering how it even exists.

Reviewers singled out the relationship between lead characters Jason and Lucia as a standout element. The Guardian's Keza MacDonald wrote that they feel like real people rather than puppets and that she found it impossible not to care for them. Paul Tassi of Forbes said he had never seen a better video game relationship than the one they share. The game's setting, Leonida, inspired by Miami, was described by The Guardian as so lifelike that at times it beggars belief.

Some critics noted that the game's increased realism might alter the chaotic tone of earlier titles. The New York Times reviewer Yussef Cole wrote that the game has matured beyond its memorable car crashes and stickups, with consequences to every action, though he said some of his favorite moments have been careening through streets with squad cars in hot pursuit. Rockstar has not revealed the game's production cost, but some estimates place it above $1 billion.
