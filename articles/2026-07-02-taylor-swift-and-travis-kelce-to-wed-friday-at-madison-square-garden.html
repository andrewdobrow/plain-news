Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce wedding underway at Madison Square Garden

Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce are celebrating their wedding at Madison Square Garden in New York City this weekend, creating what has been called a major cultural event. The couple, who began dating in 2023, secured the iconic Manhattan venue for their ceremony after months of public speculation.

The weekend festivities kicked off Thursday evening with a rehearsal dinner attended by approximately 100 guests. A permit obtained by the Associated Press details the schedule, showing black SUVs arriving at the arena at 6:30pm for the pre-party celebration. The main event is scheduled to run from 5pm Friday through 4am Saturday, with street closures and multiple entrance tents erected around the building.

Notable celebrity guests have been spotted arriving in New York, including actor Graham Norton, musicians Ed Sheeran and Sabrina Carpenter, and writer and actress Phoebe Waller-Bridge. Video footage from the rehearsal circulated online, with viewers noting the presence of actor Adam Sandler among attendees.

In advance of the wedding, Swift and Kelce donated 26 million dollars to charities across the United States, according to a representative who confirmed the donation to the Guardian. The 20 named organizations include food banks and music education nonprofits located in cities meaningful to the couple: Nashville, where Swift launched her music career; Kansas City, home of Kelce's NFL team the Kansas City Chiefs; and New York City. Recipients include City Harvest, Food Bank for NYC, the Los Angeles Regional Food Bank, and Dolly Parton's Imagination Library, among others.

Swift previously discussed her wedding plans during an appearance on The Graham Norton Show, describing it as large-scale and lavish. "It's huge," she said. "I know it's gonna be fun to plan because I think the only stressful weddings are the ones where you have a small amount and people are on the bubble."

The choice of Madison Square Garden represents an unconventional venue for a celebrity wedding, as such ceremonies typically occur at private estates or destination locations. The arena, primarily known for sporting events and concerts, required a special event permit from New York City authorities. The permitting process for an event of this scale requiring street closures can cost up to 66,000 dollars per day.

Swift is one of the most successful recording artists of her generation, while Kelce plays tight end for the Kansas City Chiefs. Their relationship has attracted extensive media coverage since becoming public, and their wedding has generated unprecedented public interest. The couple's ceremony at Madison Square Garden has drawn significant attention from fans and media outlets as the weekend celebrations unfold.
