Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce Begin Multi-Day Wedding Celebration

Pop star Taylor Swift and Kansas City Chiefs tight end Travis Kelce have begun a multi-day wedding celebration at Madison Square Garden in New York, with preparations underway for what organizers describe as a large-scale event.

According to a permit filed with New York City's permitting office, a "pre-party celebration" is scheduled for Thursday evening at 6:30 pm local time with approximately 100 guests. The main wedding event is scheduled to run from 5 pm on Friday through 4 am Saturday morning. Street closures around the arena are in effect through Saturday midday, with multiple entrance and drive-through tents being erected around the venue.

The couple has made headlines not only for the wedding itself but also for their charitable giving ahead of the event. Swift and Kelce have donated 26 million dollars to charities across the United States, according to a representative who confirmed the donation to the Guardian. The 20 named charities include organizations in locations meaningful to the couple: Nashville, where Swift began her music career; Kansas City, home of Kelce's NFL team; and New York City, where the wedding is taking place. The recipients include City Harvest and Food Bank for New York City, the Los Angeles Regional Food Bank, and various music education organizations.

Fans have noted that the 26 million dollar figure breaks down to two times Swift's well-known lucky number, 13. The couple's decision to donate before rather than after the wedding aligns with Swift's established practice of giving back. Following her Eras Tour, she gave six-figure bonuses and handwritten letters to her crew members.

A number of high-profile guests have been spotted arriving in New York ahead of the celebrations. Actresses Lena Dunham and Phoebe Waller-Bridge, model Gigi Hadid, musician Ed Sheeran, TV personality Graham Norton, music producer Jack Antonoff, and singer Sabrina Carpenter have all been photographed in formal attire near Madison Square Garden.

Supply deliveries visible around the arena included chicken wings, ground beef, and baked goods from a Brooklyn bakery, suggesting extensive catering preparations for the multi-day event. The permit process for an event of this scale, which requires street closures, can cost as much as 66,000 dollars per day according to the New York Times.

The wedding timing coincides with July Fourth weekend and America's 250th anniversary celebrations. Swift previously described her planned wedding as a large-scale affair when appearing on the Graham Norton show, saying "It's huge" and noting that she would ensure the event remained fun to plan by maintaining an appropriately sized guest list.
