Trump urges Netanyahu not to retaliate against Iran missile attack

President Trump called Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu on Sunday, urging him to refrain from retaliating against Iran's missile attack and to pursue diplomatic solutions instead. Trump had previously indicated his intention to make this appeal to Netanyahu.

Despite Trump's request, the Israeli Air Force struck Iranian military targets in central and western Iran on Monday morning. The strikes marked Israel's first direct attack on Iran since an April ceasefire and were launched in response to Iran's initial missile barrage. Trump has rejected suggestions that the escalating conflict contradicts his 2024 campaign pledge to avoid new wars.
