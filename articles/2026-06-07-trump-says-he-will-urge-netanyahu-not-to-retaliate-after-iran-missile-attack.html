Trump urges Netanyahu not to retaliate against Iran missile attack

President Trump called Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and asked him not to strike back after Iran launched missiles at Israel, according to a senior U.S. official and an Israeli source familiar with the conversation. The call came after Tehran fired missiles at Israel in what officials described as a response to an earlier Israeli strike on Beirut.

Trump told Axios before making the call that he planned to urge Netanyahu against retaliation. The president's attempt to restrain Israel's response reflects his administration's effort to prevent escalating tensions between Israel and Iran from undermining ongoing diplomatic negotiations with Tehran.

Despite Trump's appeal, the Israeli Air Force conducted strikes on military targets in central and western Iran on Monday morning, according to the Israel Defense Forces. These strikes mark the first time Israel has attacked Iran since a ceasefire took effect on April 8.

The exchange of military action between the two countries raises concerns about the future of U.S.-Iran talks. A U.S. defense official stated that the American military was not involved in the Israeli strikes. According to sources, the Trump administration did not authorize the earlier Israeli strike in Beirut that prompted Iran's response.

The escalating cycle of attacks threatens to unravel negotiations aimed at ending the broader regional conflict. Further military exchanges could lead to a resumption of full-scale war and derail diplomatic progress.

Trump has faced questions about whether the conflict with Iran contradicts his 2024 campaign pledge of "no new wars." The president rejected the suggestion that military action against Iran violates that commitment, dismissing criticism of his handling of Iran policy.

The situation began when Israel struck southern Beirut on Sunday morning. Iran responded with a missile attack against Israel the same day, which Trump sought to prevent from escalating further through his call with Netanyahu. However, Israeli military officials proceeded with their planned response despite the president's request for restraint.

The escalation comes as international efforts continue to manage tensions in the Middle East. The Israeli strikes on Iranian military targets represent a significant development in the ongoing conflict, occurring after months of relative calm following the April ceasefire agreement.

Both Israeli and American officials have indicated concern about the potential for further deterioration. The timing of these events underscores the fragile nature of current peace efforts in the region and the challenge of maintaining diplomatic channels while managing military responses to provocations.

The situation demonstrates the complexity facing the Trump administration in balancing its desire to pursue negotiations with Iran while supporting its Israeli ally's security concerns.
