Dua Lipa and Callum Turner Marry in Sicily

Pop star Dua Lipa and British actor Callum Turner married in Sicily this summer in a high-profile wedding that generated significant excitement and some controversy in the local community.

The couple first met in 2023 at the River Cafe in west London but did not begin dating until a year later when they reconnected at a restaurant in Los Angeles. Turner, 36, is widely regarded as a leading candidate to play the next James Bond. Lipa, 30, is known for hit songs including "Levitating" and "Don't Start Now."

The pair became engaged weeks before their wedding. Last July, they visited Palermo together on holiday, posting photographs on Instagram that showed them enjoying the city's baroque architecture, cathedral frescoes and sunset boat trips. The couple also visited local bars and restaurants, including Colletti, a city centre establishment. Their visible affection for Palermo fueled speculation about a potential wedding in the Sicilian capital.

Wedding planning reports emerged suggesting that rooms at Villa Igiea, a five-star art nouveau hotel, had been reserved for guests. Sources indicated that while guest accommodations were booked, the ceremony itself would take place elsewhere. Various Palermo locations were mentioned in local media as possible venues, including the gallery for modern art and the recently restored church of Santa Maria dello Spasimo.

The wedding announcement sparked considerable enthusiasm among Palermo residents, many of whom felt honored by the couple's choice of location. One 13-year-old resident expressed excitement at the prospect of seeing Lipa in a wedding dress walking through the city's streets. Local business owners, including the bar owner who had welcomed the couple during their previous visit, expressed pride in hosting an international star.

However, the high-profile event also divided local opinion. Some residents questioned the disruption caused by the wedding's scale and security requirements. One local was quoted comparing the event's impact to what might be expected for a papal visit, reflecting concerns about the public interest and logistical challenges the celebration generated.

Lipa's connection to Palermo extended beyond the wedding plans. In 2023, she appeared in promotional images wearing a Palermo football club shirt for a sportswear campaign, further cementing her association with the city.

The wedding represents a significant moment for both celebrities in their respective fields. Turner has appeared in films including "The Boys in the Boat" and "Masters of the Air," while Lipa has established herself as one of pop music's biggest international stars in recent years. Their Palermo wedding underscores their prominence and their connections within the entertainment industry.
