Shakira and Burna Boy Open FIFA 2026 World Cup with Performance of Dai Dai

Shakira and Burna Boy performed their collaboration "Dai Dai" at the opening ceremony of the FIFA 2026 World Cup, launching the tournament with a high-energy musical showcase. The performance marked the official start of the competition and introduced the event to a worldwide television audience.

The opening ceremony brought together multiple international acts beyond the headliners. The lineup included appearances from Maná, J Balvin, Los Ángeles Azules, Belinda, and Danny Ocean. This diverse roster of performers reflected the global scope of the tournament and blended different musical genres and traditions on a single stage.

The 2026 World Cup represents a historic moment for the competition. For the first time, the tournament is being hosted across three countries: the United States, Canada, and Mexico. The opening ceremony set the tone for the month-long soccer competition that followed. The tournament also expanded to include 48 teams, marking a significant change from the traditional 32-team format used in previous World Cups.

Shakira brings considerable experience to World Cup performances. She has participated in previous tournaments and recorded official World Cup songs, making her involvement in the opening ceremony a continuation of her long association with the event. Burna Boy, a prominent Nigerian artist, brought Afrobeats to the global stage during his collaboration with Shakira, combining his distinctive sound with her pop and Latin influences.

Opening ceremonies have become major cultural events in their own right, extending well beyond the realm of sports. These spectacles blend athletic competition with music and entertainment, creating memorable moments for global audiences. The selection of artists and musical selections typically reflects the host nations while also showcasing the international character of the World Cup.

The performance of "Dai Dai" introduced viewers worldwide to the artistic direction and energy the tournament organizers wanted to convey. By choosing performers from different continents and musical backgrounds, the ceremony emphasized the inclusive and worldwide nature of the competition. The combination of Shakira's established pop and Latin credentials with Burna Boy's rising prominence in Afrobeats created a genuinely international musical moment.

The ceremony's emphasis on musical diversity served practical purposes as well. It appealed to audiences across the three host nations while celebrating the participating countries from around the globe. The choice to feature established artists alongside emerging talent helped bridge different generations of music fans tuning in to watch the tournament's beginning.

As the opening ceremony concluded with the performance of "Dai Dai," it marked the official transition from anticipation to competition. The tournament's expansion to 48 teams and three countries meant that more nations would participate than ever before, and the opening ceremony's musical selections reflected this broader global representation on soccer's biggest stage.
