Brighton sign winger Femi Azeez from Millwall for up to £15 million

Brighton completed a deadline-day signing of winger Femi Azeez from Millwall in a deal worth up to £15 million. The transfer was finalized on the last day of the summer transfer window, securing the services of the attacking player for the Premier League club.

Azeez joins Brighton after his time with Millwall, where he developed his skills as a winger. The deal structure includes performance-related add-ons that could bring the total value to £15 million, reflecting Brighton's investment in the young talent. The club acted quickly to secure the signing before the transfer window closed.

The acquisition adds depth to Brighton's attacking options as they navigate the Premier League season. Azeez will join a squad that has been building its roster through the summer transfer window, with the club making strategic additions to strengthen various positions. The winger's arrival provides manager with another option in the attacking third.

Brighton's transfer activity on deadline day demonstrated their commitment to improving the squad. The club negotiated the deal with Millwall to bring Azeez to the Premier League, where he will face a higher level of competition. The transfer represents a significant step in the player's career as he moves from the Championship to England's top flight.

The completion of the Azeez signing capped Brighton's summer transfer business. The club invested heavily in new talent, with the winger joining other new arrivals as part of the team's ongoing development. Brighton will now integrate their new signings into the squad as they continue their Premier League campaign.
