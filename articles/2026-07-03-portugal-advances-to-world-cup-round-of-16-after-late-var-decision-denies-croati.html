Portugal advances to World Cup knockout stage with last-minute win over Croatia

Portugal secured a dramatic 2-1 victory over Croatia in their final group stage match, advancing to the Round of 16 and eliminating the Croatian side from the tournament. The decisive moment came in stoppage time when Gonçalo Ramos headed in a goal, sending Portugal through to face Spain in the knockout rounds.

Cristiano Ronaldo opened the scoring from the penalty spot earlier in the match, but the 41-year-old veteran was substituted before the dramatic conclusion. Ronaldo watched from the bench as his team secured the win that ensured their progression.

Croatia mounted a comeback during the match to equalize, setting up a tense finish. The team appeared destined for extra time when they scored late, but video review ruled the goal offside, denying them a chance to extend the contest. The VAR decision proved pivotal, allowing Portugal to claim the three points and knockout stage berth.

The loss marked the end of what was likely Luka Modrić's final World Cup campaign. The 40-year-old midfielder has been central to Croatia's national team for more than a decade, helping lead them to the 2018 final and a third-place finish in 2022. His departure from the tournament represents a significant moment in Croatian football history.

Portugal's victory showcased the squad's depth beyond their established star. Despite Ronaldo's substitution, the team found a way to secure the crucial result through Ramos and other players. The win demonstrated that Portugal possesses viable options across their roster for the knockout stages ahead.

The Iberian neighbors' Round of 16 matchup between Portugal and Spain promises to be one of the tournament's marquee fixtures. The two nations have a long history of competitive matches, and their meeting sets up what many expect to be an entertaining encounter.

Questions linger about whether Ronaldo will retain his starting position going forward. Manager Roberto Martínez decided to bring the forward off the bench against Croatia, a tactical choice that may continue into future matches. The decision suggests the coaching staff is managing Ronaldo's minutes as the tournament progresses.

The final moments of the Portugal-Croatia match encapsulated the intensity of group stage qualification football. Portugal's counterattack caught Croatia vulnerable while they pushed for a late equalizer, leaving them exposed defensively. Ramos capitalized on that opportunity to send his team through.

The dramatic finish underscored how tournament football can turn on single moments. Croatia's disallowed goal illustrated how technology now plays an integral role in World Cup outcomes, with VAR determining which teams advance and which go home.
