Disney Announces Maleficent Roller Coaster and Villains Land Details for Magic Kingdom

Disney revealed new details about its upcoming Villains Land at Magic Kingdom during the D23 expo, including concept art and models for a Maleficent-themed roller coaster. The announcement marks one of the first villain-focused lands at a Disney park.

The new land will feature dark magic and ancient ruins as central design themes. Disney Parks Blog described the area as "delightfully bewitching" in its official announcement. The Maleficent roller coaster represents one of two new attractions planned for the expansion, with both rides serving as anchor attractions for the themed area.

The Villains Land will be located at Magic Kingdom in Walt Disney World's Florida resort. The project creates a dedicated space for Disney villain characters who traditionally serve as antagonists in the company's animated films. Maleficent, the villain from Sleeping Beauty, will serve as the centerpiece for one of the land's major attractions.

Disney executives presented concept models and artwork during the D23 expo showcasing the visual direction for the attractions. The roller coaster will incorporate theming based on Maleficent's character and design elements connected to her story. Specific operational details such as height, speed, or special effects were not disclosed during the initial announcement. The second ride planned for Villains Land was also revealed during the presentation, though limited details were shared about that attraction.

The Villains Land project represents a significant shift in Disney's approach to theme park design. Traditionally, Disney has emphasized heroes and princesses in its attractions, so this expansion focuses on the darker elements of Disney storytelling. The move reflects potential guest interest in villain-centered narratives and experiences.

This announcement comes alongside several other major Disney World expansion projects revealed at D23. The company announced that Monstropolis, a Monsters, Inc. themed land, will open at Disney's Hollywood Studios in 2027. Additionally, a new Indiana Jones attraction called "Indiana Jones and the Myth of the Jade Serpent" is in development for Disney's Animal Kingdom, featuring Harrison Ford reprising his iconic role as an animatronic character.

Michael Hundgen, Vice President Creative at Walt Disney World Portfolio for Walt Disney Imagineering, expressed enthusiasm about the company's expansion plans. These projects demonstrate Disney's ongoing commitment to developing new themed areas and attractions that appeal to diverse guest interests.

The Villains Land project represents a notable departure from Disney's historical emphasis on heroic characters, offering park visitors a chance to explore the stories and worlds of Disney's most memorable antagonists. The combination of the Maleficent roller coaster and a second unnamed attraction promises to create an immersive experience centered on darker Disney narratives.
