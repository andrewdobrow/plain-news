France advances to World Cup quarterfinals with penalty kick victory over Paraguay

France secured a quarterfinal berth at the 2026 World Cup with a hard-fought victory over Paraguay in Philadelphia. Kylian Mbappé converted a second-half penalty to break the deadlock against a Paraguay side that employed physical tactics throughout the match.

The goal was Mbappé's 19th in World Cup competition, moving him within one of Lionel Messi in the race for the Golden Boot at this tournament. France, considered among the favorites to win the World Cup, had previously dismantled opponents in earlier rounds but needed to find a different approach against Paraguay's defensive strategy.

Paraguay's tactics drew criticism from observers who described their approach as using dark arts to frustrate the French attack. The physical nature of the match forced France to adapt their style of play. Mbappé addressed the tactics after the match, stating that France could also get their hands dirty when necessary. The Paraguay coach made no complaints about the result despite his team's elimination.

The victory in Philadelphia continues France's pursuit of the World Cup title as they navigate the knockout stages. Their next opponent in the quarterfinals has not yet been determined. The match marked a test of France's ability to win in different ways beyond their attacking dominance shown in group play.

France's progression sets up a potential deep run in the tournament as they seek to claim the World Cup. The team has demonstrated both offensive firepower in earlier matches and the resilience to grind out results when facing determined defensive opposition. Paraguay exits the competition after pushing one of the tournament's strongest teams to find an alternative path to victory.
