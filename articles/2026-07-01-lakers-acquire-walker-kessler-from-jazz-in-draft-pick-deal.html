Lakers acquire Walker Kessler from Jazz in draft pick deal

The Los Angeles Lakers have acquired center Walker Kessler from the Utah Jazz in a trade centered on future draft compensation. The Lakers are sending unprotected first-round picks in 2031 and 2033 to Utah, along with first-round pick swaps in 2028 and 2030.

The trade marks a significant investment by the Lakers in the young center, who provides rim protection and rebounding. By parting with two unprotected first-round selections and two swap rights, Los Angeles has committed substantial future assets to upgrade its frontcourt.

In addition to acquiring Kessler, the Lakers are signing three players: Sandro Mamukelashvili, Quentin Grimes, and Collin Sexton. The signings give Los Angeles additional depth and versatility across multiple positions as the team reshapes its roster.

The Jazz receive valuable draft capital that extends their collection of future picks. The unprotected nature of the 2031 and 2033 selections gives Utah potential lottery assets depending on where the Lakers stand in those seasons. The swap rights in 2028 and 2030 provide additional flexibility for Utah's front office to position itself for future draft classes.

The move represents the Lakers' focus on adding a younger core piece while maintaining veteran talent. Kessler brings defensive presence and efficiency around the basket, addressing needs that have been priorities for the organization. The accompanying signings of Mamukelashvili, Grimes, and Sexton suggest a broader roster construction strategy as Los Angeles looks to compete in the Western Conference.
