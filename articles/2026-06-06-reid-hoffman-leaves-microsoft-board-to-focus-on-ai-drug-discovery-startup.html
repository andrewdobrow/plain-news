Reid Hoffman Leaves Microsoft Board to Lead AI Drug Discovery Startup

Reid Hoffman has stepped down from Microsoft's board of directors after a decade to focus on Manus, his artificial intelligence drug discovery startup. The LinkedIn co-founder announced his departure to concentrate full-time on the healthcare technology venture.

Hoffman joined Microsoft's board in 2017 following the company's acquisition of LinkedIn for $26.2 billion. During his tenure, he provided strategic guidance as Microsoft expanded its cloud computing business and invested heavily in artificial intelligence partnerships, including its multibillion-dollar collaboration with OpenAI. The board position proved financially rewarding over the past ten years.

Manus represents Hoffman's latest entrepreneurial effort in the intersection of artificial intelligence and biotechnology. The startup applies machine learning and computational methods to accelerate the discovery and development of new pharmaceutical compounds. Drug discovery traditionally requires years of laboratory research and clinical trials, with artificial intelligence promising to reduce timelines and costs by identifying promising molecular candidates more quickly.

The departure reflects a broader trend of technology entrepreneurs returning to hands-on company building after serving in advisory or board roles. Hoffman's move follows recent industry discussions about founders taking more direct operational control of their ventures, sometimes referred to as founder mode. His exit from Microsoft's board eliminates potential conflicts of interest as Manus may compete or partner with major technology companies in the AI space.

Microsoft has not announced Hoffman's replacement on its board. The company continues to pursue aggressive expansion in artificial intelligence applications across its product lines, from cloud services to productivity software. Hoffman's tenure coincided with Microsoft's market capitalization growing to over three trillion dollars, driven largely by its cloud and AI businesses.
