Trump Announces Todd Blanche as Attorney General Nominee

President Trump announced at a White House dinner that he will nominate Todd Blanche as attorney general. A video of the event was posted on social media by a White House aide. Trump described Blanche as a "very talented guy" in connection with the nomination.

Republican senators are watching the nomination closely as it moves forward, particularly regarding the future of Department of Justice funding.
