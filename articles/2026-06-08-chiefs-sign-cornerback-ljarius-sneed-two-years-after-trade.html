Chiefs sign cornerback L'Jarius Sneed to one-year deal

The Kansas City Chiefs have reunited with veteran cornerback L'Jarius Sneed, signing him to a one-year contract worth up to $5 million, according to multiple reports from NBC Sports, ESPN, NFL.com and other outlets.

Sneed returns to Kansas City after time away from the franchise. During his previous tenure with the Chiefs, he established himself as a key defensive contributor and won two Super Bowl championships with the team, demonstrating his ability to perform in high-pressure playoff situations.

The one-year deal structure provides both sides with flexibility heading into the offseason. The contract includes incentives that could push the total value to $5 million based on performance, allowing the Chiefs to address their cornerback depth while maintaining roster flexibility for future moves.

The signing represents a reunion between Sneed and a defensive system he knows well. His familiarity with Kansas City's defensive schemes and coaching staff should enable a quick transition as the team prepares for the upcoming season. The cornerback position has been an area of focus for the Chiefs as they continue building their roster for another championship run.

Sneed's return adds veteran experience to a secondary that continues to evolve. His previous success in Kansas City's defense and his postseason experience should provide valuable depth and leadership in the secondary. The combination of his familiarity with the system and proven track record during critical games makes him a valuable addition to the roster.

The move reflects the Chiefs' overall strategy of bringing back proven veterans while maintaining flexibility. Rather than committing to a lengthy deal, the one-year structure allows both the team and player to reassess their situations after the season concludes.

Throughout his time with the Chiefs, Sneed has proven himself as a reliable presence in the secondary. His ability to perform under pressure during the team's championship runs highlighted his value to the defense. The reunion now gives Kansas City another option as they look to compete at the highest level.

The signing comes as the Chiefs continue their offseason roster construction. Adding Sneed addresses an immediate need at cornerback while the team pursues additional moves to strengthen other areas of the roster.

With his Super Bowl experience and knowledge of the Chiefs' defensive system, Sneed should be able to make an immediate impact when the season begins. His return represents a familiar pairing that could pay dividends as the team works toward another championship season.
