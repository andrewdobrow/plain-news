Chiefs sign cornerback L'Jarius Sneed to one-year deal

The Kansas City Chiefs have reunited with veteran cornerback L'Jarius Sneed, signing him to a one-year contract worth up to $5 million, according to reports from multiple sources including NBC Sports and ESPN.

Sneed spent his first four seasons with the Chiefs after being drafted by the team in 2020. During his tenure, he developed into a starter and won two Super Bowl championships with the franchise. He left Kansas City in free agency but is now returning to address defensive needs heading into the upcoming season.

The signing comes after the Chiefs traded away cornerback Trent McDuffie, creating a significant gap in their secondary. By bringing back Sneed, the team is filling that void with a player who already understands the defensive system and has proven success at the position.

The contract structure provides flexibility for both sides. The one-year term allows Sneed an opportunity to earn a longer deal while giving the Chiefs a low-risk option to address a roster need. The maximum value reflects the current market rate for experienced cornerbacks capable of stepping into starting roles immediately.

Sneed's familiarity with Kansas City's defensive schemes should enable a smooth transition for the team as it prepares for the new season. Having a returning player who has already worked within the system can accelerate his integration with the team.

The move is part of a broader effort by the Chiefs to maintain their status as championship contenders. The front office has been active in reshaping the defensive backfield through a combination of trades and free agent signings. By reinforcing the secondary, the team is attempting to keep its championship window open while adapting to roster changes.

Sneed brings valuable playoff experience to the cornerback position. His two Super Bowl rings demonstrate his ability to perform at the highest level during crucial games. For a team like Kansas City, which has established itself as a perennial title contender, adding a player with championship experience is a strategic move.

The signing represents one of several moves the Chiefs have made to address their defense this offseason. With the loss of McDuffie creating a clear need at cornerback, the decision to bring back Sneed rather than pursue alternatives on the open market suggests the organization values his experience and knowledge of the team's defensive concepts.

As the Chiefs continue preparations for the upcoming season, Sneed's return provides them with an experienced starter ready to make an immediate impact in the secondary.
