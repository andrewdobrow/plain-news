Newcastle United signs defender Amar Dedic from Benfica for $30 million

Newcastle United has completed the acquisition of defender Amar Dedic from Benfica in a £29.5 million transfer. The 24-year-old Bosnia and Herzegovina international becomes the club's sixth signing of the summer transfer window and is expected to feature in Newcastle's upcoming match against Liverpool.

The transfer was largely motivated by Dedic's prior relationship with Newcastle's new manager Matthias Jaissle. The pair previously worked together at RB Salzburg, where Dedic played under the German coach. Speaking about the move, Dedic emphasized this connection as central to his decision to join Newcastle. "Working with Matthias again was a big factor for me," he said. "We know each other well; he knows what I can bring to the pitch and I'm really happy to play for him again. It's a special feeling and a real honour to join Newcastle."

Dedic arrives at a time when Newcastle faces defensive challenges in the right-back position. Tino Livramento remains sidelined with a calf injury, leaving the club short of experienced options in that area. Following his appointment earlier this month, Jaissle identified Dedic as a solution to address this gap. The signing reflects Newcastle's confidence in Dedic's ability to step in and contribute immediately to the squad.

The defender's versatility adds value to Newcastle's defensive setup. In addition to his primary role at right-back, Dedic is comfortable operating on the left flank, making him a flexible option for Jaissle's tactical approach. His familiarity with the manager's playing philosophy should help facilitate a smooth transition into the team.

The completion of Dedic's transfer caps an eventful summer period for Newcastle under their new manager. The club has been active in the transfer market as they work to reshape the squad following significant changes in the dugout. Dedic's arrival demonstrates Newcastle's willingness to pursue targets from top European clubs to strengthen their roster.

The right-back will now focus on integrating into his new surroundings and preparing for competitive action. With Liverpool visiting on Sunday, Dedic may have limited time to settle before facing one of the Premier League's most demanding opponents. Nevertheless, his experience in top-flight football should support his ability to adapt quickly.

The transfer represents a significant investment in Newcastle's defensive capabilities and reflects the club's ambitions during the current window. Dedic's combination of experience, youth, and tactical understanding of Jaissle's methods positions him as a key addition to Newcastle's backline as the season progresses.
