Pastor and school bus driver arrested on child sex crime allegations

Law enforcement arrested two individuals on allegations involving child sex crimes, including a pastor and a school bus driver. Both arrests underscore concerns about child safety in positions of trust within the community.

The dual arrests involve individuals who regularly interact with children through their professional roles. School bus drivers transport students daily across Treasure Coast school districts, while pastors hold positions of spiritual authority in local congregations.

Authorities have not released additional information about the specific charges or which school district or church the individuals were affiliated with. The arrests come as local law enforcement agencies continue prioritizing investigations involving crimes against children.
