AC Milan and Italy defender Franco Baresi dies at 66

Franco Baresi, one of the most recognizable defenders of Italian football's golden era in the 1980s and 1990s, has died at the age of 66. AC Milan confirmed his passing on Friday.

"The entire history of AC Milan is in tears following the passing of Franco Baresi," the Italian club said in a statement. "His example and integrity will be forever etched into the club's DNA, just as his iconic No 6 shirt is."

Baresi spent his entire career at AC Milan, becoming inseparable from the club's identity during one of its most successful periods. As captain and defensive leader, he shaped Milan's dominance in Serie A and European competitions across two decades. His style of play combined physical strength with tactical intelligence and technical ability, allowing him to read the game and organize the defense at the highest level.

On the international stage, Baresi represented Italy with distinction, establishing himself as one of the finest defenders of his generation. He featured prominently in Italy's World Cup campaigns and earned recognition as one of the greatest players to represent the Azzurri. His performances at major tournaments cemented his status among Italian football's most celebrated figures.

Baresi's influence extended beyond individual matches and trophies. He fundamentally changed how defenders were perceived in football, demonstrating that those playing at the back could be as influential and celebrated as attacking players. This shift helped define an entire era of Italian football and set standards of excellence that influenced generations of defenders who followed.

After his playing career ended, Baresi maintained close ties to AC Milan, serving the club in various capacities and representing it as an ambassador. His presence remained a constant connection to the club's storied history and traditions.

The football community responded with widespread tributes following the announcement. Baresi had undergone surgery on a pulmonary nodule, according to reports at the time of his death.

His legacy in football extends beyond the silverware he won during his career. Baresi demonstrated that defenders could be central figures in a team's success, worthy of the same recognition and celebration as any other player. He embodied both the values of AC Milan and the broader traditions of Italian football, representing an approach to the game that emphasized tactical sophistication and defensive excellence.

The loss represents a significant moment for the global football community, particularly for those connected to AC Milan and Italian football. Franco Baresi's name remains synonymous with defensive mastery and leadership, and his passing marks the end of an era in the sport's history.
