Formula 1 extends Las Vegas race contract through 2037

Formula 1 will continue racing in Las Vegas until at least 2037 after the series extended its contract with the city by 10 years.

The Las Vegas Grand Prix has secured its place on the Formula 1 calendar for more than a decade following the contract extension announcement. The street circuit runs through the heart of Las Vegas, creating a distinctive nighttime racing experience that has generated substantial interest among both dedicated racing enthusiasts and general sports viewers since its recent return to the calendar.

The extended agreement reflects Formula 1's strategic focus on expanding its footprint across North America. The United States now hosts three Formula 1 races annually, with events held in Las Vegas, Austin, Texas, and Miami, Florida. These three venues form the backbone of the sport's American racing schedule and will remain anchored through the next decade.

Both Formula 1 and Las Vegas officials view the extended partnership as mutually beneficial. The race brings significant tourism revenue to the city while providing Formula 1 with a high-profile event in one of America's most recognizable entertainment destinations. For teams, sponsors, and fans, the long-term commitment creates stability for planning future participation in the annual event.

Formula 1 has pursued aggressive expansion under its current ownership structure, actively adding new races and developing relationships with major markets globally. The Las Vegas extension follows this broader growth strategy, securing a marquee venue for the long term. The commitment through 2037 establishes the sport's strong presence in Las Vegas as it competes for attention within America's crowded sports marketplace.

The Las Vegas Grand Prix represents a significant investment from both the sport and the city in building a distinctive racing experience. The nighttime street race format sets it apart from other Formula 1 events and has helped attract viewership beyond traditional motorsports fans. The race's popularity and the partnership's success have made the case for continued commitment from both parties.

The 10-year extension demonstrates confidence from leadership on both sides that the Las Vegas Grand Prix will continue delivering value and generating interest. The stability provided by the extended contract allows for long-term planning around infrastructure, sponsorships, and event operations. For Las Vegas, maintaining Formula 1 as an annual fixture supports the city's position as a premier destination for major sporting events and entertainment.

This contract extension solidifies Formula 1's North American strategy and reinforces Las Vegas's role as a key market for the sport's future development in the United States.
