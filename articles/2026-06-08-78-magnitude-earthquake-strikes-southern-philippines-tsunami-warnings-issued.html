Earthquake in Philippines Kills at Least 15

A magnitude 7.8 earthquake struck the southern Philippines on Monday, killing at least 19 people and injuring more than 200 others. The powerful quake caused buildings to collapse across multiple areas and generated a 3-foot tsunami that reached nearby coasts.

Rescue operations were underway as authorities assessed the damage. Officials warned residents to avoid entering damaged buildings and structures due to the risk of aftershocks following the initial tremor.
