Five Injured in Stabbing at Penn Station in Manhattan

Five people were injured in a stabbing at Penn Station in Manhattan, according to the New York Fire Department. The incident occurred at one of the busiest transit hubs in the United States, which serves Long Island Rail Road, New Jersey Transit, Amtrak, and several subway lines.

A suspect is in custody following the attack. The Fire Department responded to the scene and transported the injured individuals for treatment. One person sustained serious injuries, while the others suffered less severe wounds.

Penn Station handles hundreds of thousands of commuters and travelers daily, making it a critical transportation nexus for the New York metropolitan area. The station connects regional rail services with the city subway system and serves as a gateway for intercity rail travel along the Northeast Corridor.

Authorities have not released information about what led to the stabbing or the identity of the suspect. The investigation into the incident continues as law enforcement works to determine the circumstances surrounding the attack.

The stabbing adds to ongoing concerns about public safety in major transit facilities across the country. Penn Station has been the focus of security discussions in recent years as city and transit officials work to balance accessibility with passenger safety in one of the nation's most heavily trafficked transportation centers.
