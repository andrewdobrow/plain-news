Trump Targets 59 Countries with New Tariffs Over Forced Labor

The Trump administration announced plans to impose tariffs up to 12.5 percent on 59 countries and the European Union. The administration is targeting nations that have not adequately addressed forced labor in manufacturing, potentially affecting imports across multiple continents.

The move follows a February Supreme Court decision that invalidated many of Trump's previous tariffs. The forced labor justification provides a new legal basis for this round of trade restrictions.
