Liverpool agree £123 million deal for PSG forward Bradley Barcola

Liverpool have finalised a deal to sign Paris Saint-Germain winger Bradley Barcola in a transfer worth up to £123 million. The move represents a significant investment in the club's attacking options ahead of Tuesday's transfer deadline.

The agreement came after a breakthrough in negotiations on Wednesday night, with PSG lowering their initial asking price of £145 million. Liverpool will pay a guaranteed sum of just over £100 million, with additional fees based on individual and team achievements taking the total package to approximately £123 million.

Barcola, 23, will undergo a medical this weekend to complete the formalities of his move to Anfield. The French winger has been Liverpool's primary transfer target throughout the summer and made clear his desire to join the club, having dropped down the pecking order at PSG. He has two years remaining on his Paris contract but refused to sign an extension, pushing the French champions toward accepting a lower fee.

The transfer makes Barcola Liverpool's second-most expensive signing in history, behind Alexander Isak at £125 million. PSG originally signed the player from Lyon for €45 million in 2023, and he played a key role in their rise as Champions League winners. However, manager Luis Enrique's preference for Désiré Doué, Ousmane Dembélé and Khvicha Kvaratskhelia in their biggest matches last season prompted Barcola to reconsider his future. PSG have also added other attacking options this summer, including Ferran Torres, Maghnes Akliouche and Mika Godts, and have excluded Barcola from their matchday squads.

The signing raises fresh questions about Cody Gakpo's future at the club. The Netherlands international has attracted interest from Tottenham and Manchester City, and manager Andoni Iraola was unable to guarantee the winger would remain at Liverpool beyond the deadline. "When the market is open you cannot guarantee anything with anyone," Iraola said.

Despite securing Barcola, Liverpool still need to replace Mohamed Salah on the right wing. The club has had two bids rejected by Brighton for Yankuba Minteh and enquired about Crystal Palace's Ismaïla Sarr without making a formal offer.

Iraola indicated the club would attempt to strengthen in other areas if opportunities arise, particularly given the long-term injuries affecting defender Conor Bradley and striker Hugo Ekitiké. "Ideally is the magic word," Iraola said. "We don't know what we can do, but it's true the priority has been mostly in the wide areas."
