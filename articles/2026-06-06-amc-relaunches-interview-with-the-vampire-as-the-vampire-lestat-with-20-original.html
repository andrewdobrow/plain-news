AMC Relaunches Interview With the Vampire as The Vampire Lestat With 20 Original Songs

AMC premiered The Vampire Lestat at New York's Beacon Theatre on June 2 with star Sam Reid performing six original songs in full character as vampire-turned-rocker Lestat de Lioncourt. The relaunch of Interview With the Vampire features 20 original songs as the series shifts focus to the rock star persona of its lead vampire character.

Reid performed at the premiere party as the fictional rock star, creating an immersive experience for attendees. The network is positioning the series relaunch with significant marketing efforts and a concert format that blends the show's supernatural themes with musical performance.
