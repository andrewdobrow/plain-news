Browns guard Joel Bitonio retires after 12 seasons

Cleveland Browns guard Joel Bitonio announced his retirement after 12 seasons with the team. The seven-time Pro Bowl selection and two-time All-Pro spent his entire NFL career with Cleveland after the team selected him in the second round of the 2014 draft.

Bitonio became one of the most consistent offensive linemen in franchise history, earning Pro Bowl honors in seven of his 12 seasons. He was named first-team All-Pro twice during his career. The guard started 179 games for the Browns, providing stability on the offensive line through multiple coaching changes and quarterback transitions.

The Nevada product was part of the Browns' offensive line during the team's playoff appearances in recent years. He played a key role in protecting the quarterback and opening running lanes as Cleveland worked to rebuild its roster. His durability and performance made him one of the few constants during a period of significant roster turnover for the franchise.

Bitonio's retirement removes a veteran presence from the Browns' offensive line heading into the upcoming season. The team will need to find a replacement for one of the most decorated players in recent franchise history. His departure marks the end of an era for Cleveland's offensive line, which will undergo significant changes without the longtime starter.

The announcement comes during the offseason as teams prepare for training camp. Bitonio's decision to retire now gives the Browns time to evaluate their options at the guard position before the start of the season.
