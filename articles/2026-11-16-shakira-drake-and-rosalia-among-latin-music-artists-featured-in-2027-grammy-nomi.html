Shakira, Drake, and Rosalía Among Latin Music Artists Featured in 2027 Grammy Nominations

The 2027 Grammy nominations highlight Latin music's continued prominence, with Spanish singer Rosalía earning the second-most nominations overall and a chance to follow Bad Bunny's historic 2026 Album of the Year win. If her album Lux wins the category, Rosalía would become the first woman to win Album of the Year with a Spanish-language project. Drake received a nomination for Best Latin Song for his feature on Karol G's "Ahí," marking a surprising recognition given his longstanding tensions with the Recording Academy and his decision to stop submitting his own work after a 2019 win.

Several Latin artists received their first Grammy nominations, including Colombian musician Ryan Castro, who earned two nods in Best Música Urbana Album for his solo album and a collaborative project with J Balvin. Argentine musician Milo J was nominated for Best Latin Song and Best Latin Rock or Alternative Album, while Venezuelan American singer Elena Rose secured her first nomination in the Best Latin Pop Album category.

The Best Latin Pop Album category drew criticism for grouping together artists with distinct musical styles under one umbrella. Nominees include Karol G, Mon Laferte, Carín León, Rawayana, and Elena Rose, with their albums spanning genres from alternative to norteño, salsa, and soul. Critics noted the category fails to properly honor the varied Latin genres the artists represent.

Latin music's global reach was reflected in nominations outside traditional Latin categories. Anitta's album Equilibrivm received a nomination for Best Global Music Album, while Shakira's World Cup song "Dai Dai" was nominated for Best Global Music Performance. Songwriter Edgar Barrera earned his third consecutive nomination for Songwriter of the Year, Non-Classical, after losing in the category the previous two years.
