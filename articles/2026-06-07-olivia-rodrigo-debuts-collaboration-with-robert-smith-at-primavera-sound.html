Olivia Rodrigo debuts collaboration with Robert Smith at Primavera Sound

Olivia Rodrigo surprised fans at Barcelona's Primavera Sound festival by bringing Robert Smith of The Cure onstage for a special performance. The collaboration, titled "What's Wrong With Me," marks one of the most closely guarded secrets in pop music heading into 2026, with Rodrigo's new album set to release within days of the premiere.

The duet had been kept under wraps before the album launch. Rodrigo performed an unannounced set at the music festival, where she revealed the new track by inviting Smith to join her partway through the performance. The surprise premiere introduced fans to what will become a full collaboration on her forthcoming record.

This marks a significant moment for both artists. Smith, the legendary frontman of The Cure, brings decades of iconic songwriting and distinctive vocal styling to the partnership. The collaboration represents a noteworthy pairing between a veteran alternative rock figure and one of pop music's biggest contemporary voices.

The decision to debut the song live at a major festival rather than through traditional release channels added to the event's impact. Fans in attendance witnessed a historic musical moment as two artists from different eras and genres came together on the Barcelona stage. The secrecy surrounding the project had kept even industry observers guessing about what Rodrigo would include on her next project.

Primavera Sound proved to be the ideal venue for such a reveal. The Barcelona festival has long been known for hosting surprise performances and special moments that generate significant buzz within the music community. By choosing this platform, Rodrigo ensured the collaboration would reach both her existing fanbase and broader festival audiences.

The announcement that Smith appears on Rodrigo's album confirms speculation among music fans about potential celebrity collaborations on her next release. With the album arriving so soon after the Primavera Sound premiere, the collaboration will be available to listeners shortly after its live debut.

Smith has remained an influential figure in music throughout his career, and his involvement in new projects continues to draw attention from fans and critics alike. The partnership with Rodrigo demonstrates his willingness to work across musical generations and styles. For Rodrigo, collaborating with an artist of Smith's stature represents a significant artistic statement and adds another dimension to her evolving sound.

The "What's Wrong With Me" duet will give listeners a chance to hear how these two vocal styles blend and what creative direction their partnership takes. The song will be among the tracks on Rodrigo's forthcoming album, which arrives within days of the Primavera Sound performance.
