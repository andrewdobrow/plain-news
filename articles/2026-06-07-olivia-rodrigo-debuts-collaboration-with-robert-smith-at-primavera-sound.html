Olivia Rodrigo Debuts Song With Robert Smith at Primavera Sound

Olivia Rodrigo unveiled a new duet titled "What's Wrong With Me" featuring Robert Smith of The Cure during a surprise performance at Primavera Sound festival. The collaboration marks a significant moment for both artists, with the reveal coming just days before Rodrigo's forthcoming album hits release.

During her set at the festival, Rodrigo brought Smith onstage to perform the duet for the first time in front of an audience. The song represents a notable pairing between one of pop's biggest current stars and the legendary frontman of the influential rock band The Cure, whose career spans decades and has profoundly shaped alternative and gothic rock music.

The appearance generated considerable buzz across music publications, with coverage appearing in major outlets including Variety, Billboard, and Yahoo. Industry reports indicate that the collaboration breaks with an established pattern in Rodrigo's previous work, though specific details about how the duet represents a departure from her typical approach were not elaborated in available coverage.

This surprise premiere represents one of pop's most anticipated reveals heading into 2026. The news of the collaboration between Rodrigo and Smith had been kept under wraps until the Primavera Sound performance, making the duet one of the year's best-kept secrets in the music industry. The album on which "What's Wrong With Me" appears is scheduled to release within the coming week of the festival performance.

The partnership demonstrates the reach of Rodrigo's artistry across generations and genres. At a relatively early point in her career, she has already collaborated with major figures from different musical eras and styles. Smith's involvement in the project signals the respect Rodrigo commands among established artists and legends within the industry.

The Cure frontman brings decades of experience and iconic status to the collaboration. Known for his distinctive vocal style and the band's atmospheric, layered sound, Smith has remained a significant cultural figure since The Cure's emergence in the late 1970s. His willingness to participate in the project speaks to the appeal of working with Rodrigo during a period when her artistic influence continues to expand.

Festival audiences at Primavera Sound witnessed the public debut of what will likely become a standout track on Rodrigo's upcoming album. The surprise element of the performance, combined with the unexpected nature of the collaboration itself, created a memorable moment for attendees and generated significant conversation across social media and music journalism platforms.

As fans await the album's release, "What's Wrong With Me" has already established itself as one of the most discussed upcoming tracks, generating anticipation for how the duet will sound in its studio version and what other surprises might await on the full album.
