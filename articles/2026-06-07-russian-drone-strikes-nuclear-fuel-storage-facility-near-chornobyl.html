Russian drone strikes nuclear fuel storage facility near Chornobyl

A Russian Shahed drone struck a building at the spent nuclear fuel storage facility near the disused Chornobyl nuclear power plant. The reception building of the storage facility sustained substantial damage, though it was empty of fuel containers at the time of the attack.

President Zelensky condemned the strike as deliberate and "extremely vile," criticizing it ahead of scheduled talks in London. The targeting of the sensitive site appears intended as a message from Moscow amid an intensifying exchange of long-range aerial strikes between Russia and Ukraine, with both sides hitting high-profile locations.
