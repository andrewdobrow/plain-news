Russia Ran Coordinated Campaign to Undermine Moldova's Pro-Western Government

Russia conducted a wide-ranging campaign to undermine Moldova's pro-Western government, using methods that included paying Orthodox priests, organizing vote-buying schemes, and establishing training camps focused on election interference. The effort represents one of the most comprehensive documented attempts by Moscow to influence a European election.

The campaign targeted Moldova's Western-leaning leadership, which has sought closer ties with the European Union and distanced itself from Russian influence. Russian operatives used financial incentives to sway public opinion and disrupt democratic processes in the former Soviet republic. The training camps were specifically designed to teach tactics for interfering in electoral procedures.

Orthodox priests received payments from Russian sources as part of the influence operation. The religious dimension of the campaign reflects Russia's use of cultural and spiritual ties to advance political objectives in countries with significant Orthodox Christian populations. Moldova, a small nation bordering Ukraine and Romania, has been a focal point of competing geopolitical interests since Russia's invasion of Ukraine.

The documented interference comes as Moldova continues its push for European integration. The country applied for EU membership following Russia's military action in neighboring Ukraine, viewing closer Western alignment as essential to its security and economic future. Russian officials have previously criticized Moldova's government for what they characterize as anti-Russian policies.

The scale of the operation underscores the lengths to which Russia has gone to maintain influence in former Soviet states. Moldova's government has worked to counter Russian disinformation and economic pressure while managing tensions with the breakaway region of Transnistria, where Russian troops remain stationed. The revelations about the interference campaign are likely to fuel further debate in Moldova about the country's geopolitical orientation and vulnerability to external manipulation.
