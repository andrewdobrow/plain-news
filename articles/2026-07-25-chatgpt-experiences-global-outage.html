ChatGPT experiences global outage

OpenAI confirmed that ChatGPT experienced a worldwide outage, leaving users across multiple continents unable to access the AI chatbot service. Thousands of users reported errors, missing conversation history, and stalled prompts across both mobile and web platforms. The outage affected users in the United States, India, Israel, and multiple other regions.

The service disruption prevented users from logging in and accessing the conversational AI tool, which has become widely used for tasks ranging from writing assistance to coding help and general information queries. Users attempting to access ChatGPT encountered error messages and found themselves unable to load previous conversations.

ChatGPT has grown to serve millions of users globally since its public release. The platform allows users to interact with an AI model that can generate text responses, answer questions, and assist with various tasks. Outages of this scale are relatively uncommon for the service, though like all online platforms, it has experienced technical difficulties in the past.

OpenAI acknowledged the service disruption through official channels. The company operates ChatGPT as a cloud-based service, meaning technical issues on their infrastructure can affect all users simultaneously regardless of location. During the outage, users across different time zones found themselves unable to access the service during their respective business and personal hours.

The incident highlights the growing dependence on cloud-based AI services and the widespread impact when such platforms experience technical failures. As ChatGPT has integrated into daily workflows for many users, service interruptions can affect productivity across various sectors. OpenAI has not publicly detailed the technical cause of the outage or provided an estimated timeline for full service restoration at the time of the initial reports.
