Israeli Soldiers Kill Seven-Month-Old Palestinian Baby in West Bank

Israeli police and military forces shot and killed four members of a Palestinian family in the occupied West Bank, including two young children, according to Palestinian health officials and witness accounts.

Mohammed, five, and Othman, seven, who was blind and had special needs, were killed along with their parents, Waad Bani Odeh, 35, and Ali Bani Odeh, 37. The family was returning home to Tamoun after a Ramadan shopping trip late Saturday when undercover Israeli forces opened fire on their vehicle without warning. All four family members were shot in the head and face. Ali, who was driving, was also shot in the chest and left hand.

Two older brothers survived the attack. Khaled, 11, told Reuters that he heard his mother crying and his father praying before the gunfire stopped. He said Israeli forces then dragged him from the wreckage, beat and kicked him, and taunted him about his family's deaths. One of the Israeli forces told him "we killed dogs," Khaled said.

The family had traveled to nearby Nablus to buy clothes for Eid, the celebration marking the end of Ramadan. Both surviving boys sustained shrapnel wounds to the head and eye.

Israeli police said the forces opened fire after perceiving an immediate threat when the vehicle accelerated. The operation was aimed at arresting suspects believed involved in militant activity, according to a police statement. Israeli authorities declined to comment on what threat was posed by unarmed parents and children, or whether the shooting violated rules of engagement. The circumstances remain under review.

Palestinian health officials and the ministry of foreign affairs characterized the killings as an extrajudicial execution. The ministry said such incidents reflect a broader systematic campaign against Palestinians in the occupied West Bank.

The shooting came hours after Israeli settlers killed Amir Moatasem Odeh, 28, in another West Bank location. His father was stabbed and hospitalized with serious injuries.

The deaths are part of an escalating pattern of violence in the occupied West Bank. Since the last indictment of Israeli forces for a homicide in 2019, Israeli forces have killed more than 1,400 people, including over 320 children, according to UN figures compiled by Israeli human rights group Yesh Din. In recent weeks alone, Israeli settlers have killed six civilians during incursions into Palestinian villages and grazing land.

The incident has drawn international attention to Israel's military conduct in Palestinian territories. Human rights organizations have repeatedly called for accountability in cases involving civilian casualties.
