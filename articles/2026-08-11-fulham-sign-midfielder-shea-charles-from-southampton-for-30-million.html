Fulham sign midfielder Shea Charles from Southampton for £30 million

Fulham completed the signing of midfielder Shea Charles from Southampton in a transfer deal worth up to £30 million. The club confirmed the acquisition of the young midfielder as part of their squad reinforcement ahead of the new season.

Charles joined Southampton's academy system and developed through their youth ranks before breaking into the first team. The midfielder's performances at Southampton attracted interest from multiple Premier League clubs during the transfer window. Fulham moved decisively to secure his signature in what represents a significant investment in their midfield.

The £30 million fee includes performance-related add-ons that could increase the total value of the transfer. Charles signed a multi-year contract with Fulham, committing his future to the West London club. The transfer represents one of the more substantial deals of the current transfer window for both clubs involved.

Southampton will use the funds from the sale to reinvest in their own squad as they continue their rebuild. The club has been active in the transfer market, both buying and selling players as they reshape their roster. Charles becomes the latest player to depart St Mary's Stadium during this window.

Fulham manager will integrate Charles into the squad during the remaining pre-season preparations. The midfielder is expected to compete for a starting position in central midfield, adding depth and quality to Fulham's options in that area of the pitch. The club views Charles as a player who can contribute immediately while continuing his development at the Premier League level.
