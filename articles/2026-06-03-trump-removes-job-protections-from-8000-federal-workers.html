Trump removes civil service protections from 8,000 federal workers

President Trump signed an executive order removing civil service protections from approximately 8,000 senior federal workers earning up to nearly $200,000 annually. These employees, classified as policy-influencing positions across multiple agencies, can now be dismissed without the procedural protections traditionally afforded to career civil servants.

The reclassification allows the administration to fire these workers for any reason, representing a significant shift in federal workforce management at senior levels.
