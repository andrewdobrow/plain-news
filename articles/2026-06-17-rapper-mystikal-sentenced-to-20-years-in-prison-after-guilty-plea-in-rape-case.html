Rapper Mystikal Sentenced to 20 Years in Prison After Guilty Plea in Rape Case

Louisiana rapper Mystikal pleaded guilty to third-degree rape on Tuesday in connection with charges stemming from his 2022 arrest. The 55-year-old artist, whose legal name is Michael Tyler, now faces up to 20 years in prison following the plea deal entered in a state courthouse outside Baton Rouge.

Tyler was originally charged with more serious offenses including first-degree rape, simple robbery, domestic abuse battery and false imprisonment, to which he had pleaded not guilty. Had he been convicted on those initial charges, he would have faced mandatory life imprisonment. The guilty plea to the lesser third-degree rape charge was part of an agreement that significantly reduced the severity of the case against him.

According to reports from local news outlets, the alleged victim told investigators that Tyler raped her, beat her and choked her. She also claimed he took her phone and car keys to prevent her from leaving and then forced her to send him money through the Cash App payment platform. Tyler's sentencing hearing was tentatively scheduled for June.

Mystikal rose to prominence in 2000 with the global hit "Shake Ya Ass" and went on to receive two Grammy nominations. The track gained additional cultural prominence through its appearance in the film "About a Boy," which starred Hugh Grant, Toni Collette and Nicholas Hoult. His debut album reached the top of the Billboard 200 chart.

However, this latest case adds to a troubling history of criminal convictions. In 2004, Tyler was sentenced to six years in prison after being found guilty of sexual assault and extortion involving his hairstylist, a conviction that required him to register as a sex offender. In 2012, he was jailed for three months following an arrest on a misdemeanor domestic abuse charge. More recently, in 2017, authorities charged him with rape and kidnapping, though those charges were ultimately dropped.

The guilty plea on Tuesday came ahead of a scheduled trial date set for March 30. This case reflects broader discussions about accountability within the entertainment industry regarding sexual violence. Tyler had attempted a career comeback in recent years with new music releases and select performances, efforts that now come to an end with this lengthy prison sentence, which will extend well into his later years.
