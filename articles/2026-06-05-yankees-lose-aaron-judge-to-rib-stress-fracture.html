Yankees outfielder Aaron Judge out at least four weeks with rib stress fracture

New York Yankees outfielder Aaron Judge has been diagnosed with a stress fracture in his rib on his right side and will be reevaluated in four to six weeks. The injury represents a significant setback for the Yankees as they navigate the 2026 season without one of baseball's premier power hitters.

Judge's absence leaves a substantial void in the Yankees lineup. The team now faces at least a month without their star player, who has been a cornerstone of the franchise in recent years. The Yankees will need to adjust their roster and batting order to compensate for the loss during his recovery period.

Stress fractures in ribs typically require rest and limited physical activity to heal properly. The four to six week timeline means Judge will miss a considerable portion of the season, though the exact duration depends on how his body responds to treatment. The Yankees medical staff will monitor his progress closely before determining when he can resume baseball activities.

The injury comes at a time when the Yankees are trying to position themselves in the competitive American League standings. Judge's production both at the plate and in the outfield has been integral to the team's success. His power hitting and defensive capabilities make him one of the most valuable players in the league.

The Yankees organization now faces decisions about how to fill the roster spot and whether any roster moves or adjustments will be necessary during Judge's absence. The team will rely on other players to step up offensively while awaiting his return to full health.
