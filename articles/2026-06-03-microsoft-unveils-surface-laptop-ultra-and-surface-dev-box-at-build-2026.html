Microsoft Unveils Surface Laptop Ultra and Surface Dev Box at Build 2026

Microsoft introduced new Surface hardware during its Build 2026 developer conference, showcasing the Surface Laptop Ultra and Surface Dev Box as part of a broader push into AI-powered computing. The announcements came alongside updates to Copilot AI tools and the reveal of Project Solara, a new platform designed for devices that run AI agents instead of traditional applications.

The Surface Laptop Ultra and Surface Dev Box represent Microsoft's latest hardware offerings targeting developers and professionals. Nvidia announced it is partnering with Microsoft to integrate new PC chips into Windows machines, with Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang positioning the collaboration as part of his strategy to compete across every layer of the AI technology stack. The new Nvidia RTX Spark Laptops aim to bring enhanced AI capabilities to consumer devices.

Microsoft AI chief Mustafa Suleyman stated during the conference that three labs currently dominate AI research, and he wants Microsoft to become the fourth. The company emphasized Windows as a trusted platform for development and detailed how its AI infrastructure will support the next generation of developer tools and applications.

Project Solara emerged as one of the more significant platform announcements. The initiative represents a shift away from traditional app-based computing toward systems where AI agents handle tasks directly. Microsoft positioned this as a fundamental rethinking of how users interact with devices, though specific technical details about how the platform will function were not disclosed in the available coverage.

The Build 2026 keynote covered multiple products and services across Microsoft's developer ecosystem. The company continues to invest heavily in AI integration across its product lines, with Copilot features receiving updates aimed at improving developer productivity. The conference runs through the week, with additional sessions planned to detail implementation specifics for the announced technologies.
