Zinedine Zidane named France national team head coach

Zinedine Zidane has been confirmed as the new head coach of the France national football team. The former Real Madrid manager takes charge of the national side, marking his return to management after leaving the Spanish club.

Zidane enjoyed a storied playing career with France, winning the 1998 World Cup and Euro 2000. As a manager, he led Real Madrid to three consecutive Champions League titles between 2016 and 2018, establishing himself as one of the most successful coaches in European football. His appointment comes as France looks to build on its strong international record in recent years.

The 54-year-old brings significant experience to the role, having managed at the highest level of club football. During his two spells at Real Madrid, Zidane won 11 trophies including three Champions League titles, two La Liga championships, and two Spanish Super Cups. His tactical acumen and ability to manage star players made him one of the most respected figures in world football.

Zidane inherits a French squad that has remained competitive on the international stage. The team features a blend of experienced players and emerging talent, providing the new coach with strong resources as he takes over. His familiarity with many French players, both from his own era and through his work in Spanish football, is expected to facilitate his transition into the role.

The appointment represents a significant moment for French football. Zidane's status as one of the nation's greatest ever players, combined with his proven success as a manager, has generated considerable anticipation about what he can achieve with the national team. His first matches in charge will be closely watched as he begins to implement his vision for French football on the international stage.
