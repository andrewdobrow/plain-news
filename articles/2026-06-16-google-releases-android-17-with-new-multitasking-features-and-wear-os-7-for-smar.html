Google releases Android 17 with new multitasking features and Wear OS 7 for smartwatches

Google has released Android 17 and Wear OS 7, introducing a range of productivity and AI-focused upgrades across its Pixel device lineup. The operating system updates are rolling out through over-the-air updates to Pixel phones and watches, with broader availability expanding in the coming weeks.

Android 17 brings several new multitasking capabilities designed to improve user productivity. The update includes floating "Bubble" app windows that make it easier to manage multiple applications simultaneously. A new Screen Reaction recording mode allows users to capture video of themselves and their screen at the same time, enabling them to create reaction videos popular on social media. The phone app has also been expanded with new functionality. Additionally, Android 17 introduces a 50/50 split gaming mode designed specifically for foldable phones.

The update incorporates enhanced parental controls and security tools to help families manage device usage more effectively. Google has indicated that major AI features will arrive later this summer through the accompanying Pixel Drop update, which brings the company's latest AI models to its devices.

Wear OS 7 targets smartwatch users with performance improvements and new capabilities. The update introduces Live Updates, a feature that tracks live events on Wear OS devices. This means users can monitor ongoing information like sports scores or meal delivery status directly on their smartwatches. The smartwatch operating system also delivers improved battery life, helping devices keep up with user activity throughout the day.

Wear OS 7 incorporates Gemini AI integration, extending Google's artificial intelligence capabilities to wearable devices. The update is rolling out to Pixel Watch 2, 3, and 4 models.

The releases continue Google's annual update cycle for its mobile and wearable platforms. Pixel devices receive the updates first, with other manufacturers expected to release Android 17 for their devices in the following months after completing customization and testing processes.

Google has not provided specific details about which individual Pixel models will receive the updates or confirmed exact timelines for global availability. The company noted that users should expect the over-the-air updates to arrive gradually as the rollout expands beyond initial Pixel device availability.

These updates represent Google's broader push to integrate artificial intelligence across its hardware ecosystem. The company is positioning the Android 17 and Wear OS 7 releases as improving both productivity and device longevity, emphasizing how new features can help users work more efficiently while maintaining the long-term health and performance of their devices.
