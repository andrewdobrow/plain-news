Microsoft unveils new AI-focused hardware and development tools at Build 2026

Microsoft introduced new hardware and software products at its Build 2026 developer conference, including the Surface Laptop Ultra and Surface Dev Box. The company also announced Project Solara, a platform designed for devices that run AI agents instead of traditional applications.

The Surface Laptop Ultra and Surface Dev Box represent Microsoft's latest hardware offerings for developers. Nvidia partnered with Microsoft to introduce new PC chips designed for personal AI applications, part of Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang's strategy to compete across all layers of the AI technology stack. Microsoft also announced plans to bring Linux tools and an RTX Spark desktop environment to Windows developers.

Project Solara marks a shift in how Microsoft envisions computing devices. The platform is built for running AI agents rather than conventional software applications, suggesting a fundamental change in how users might interact with future devices. The announcement signals Microsoft's bet that AI agents will become the primary interface for computing tasks.

The conference highlighted Microsoft's continued investment in AI infrastructure and developer tools. The company positioned Windows as a trusted platform for development while simultaneously introducing tools that expand beyond the traditional Windows interface. The addition of Linux tools and RTX Spark desktop capabilities indicates Microsoft's effort to accommodate diverse developer preferences and workflows.

The announcements come as major technology companies compete to dominate the AI hardware and software markets. Nvidia's involvement in the new PC chips underscores the hardware demands of AI applications and the importance of specialized processors for personal AI computing. Microsoft's focus on AI agents and specialized development environments reflects broader industry trends toward AI-integrated computing experiences.
