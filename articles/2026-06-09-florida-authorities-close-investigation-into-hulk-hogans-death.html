Florida Police Close Hulk Hogan Investigation, Reveal Cause of Death

Florida authorities have closed their investigation into the death of wrestling legend Hulk Hogan and revealed the cause of death, more than 10 months after the iconic performer died at age 71. Hogan, whose real name was Terry Gene Bollea, died on July 24, 2025. The specific cause of death released by investigators was not detailed in available reports.

Hogan became one of the most recognizable figures in professional wrestling history during a career spanning four decades. He headlined multiple WrestleMania events and became a cultural phenomenon in the 1980s and 1990s, transcending the sport to become a mainstream celebrity. His signature moves and catchphrases made him a household name around the world.

The wrestling icon's career included championship reigns with both WWE and WCW, where he became a central figure in the Monday Night Wars that defined professional wrestling in the late 1990s. Beyond wrestling, Hogan appeared in films and television shows, further cementing his status as an entertainment figure. His influence on professional wrestling and popular culture extended well beyond the ring.

The Florida police investigation began following his death last summer. Authorities have now concluded their work and released their findings. The closure of the investigation provides official confirmation of the circumstances surrounding Hogan's death, though the investigation timeline extended nearly a year after his passing.

Hogan's death marked the end of an era in professional wrestling. He remained connected to the wrestling world throughout his later years, making occasional appearances and maintaining his status as one of the industry's most recognizable legends. His impact on the sport and entertainment industry continues to be felt by fans and performers alike.
