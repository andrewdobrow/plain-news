Meta signs deal with Reliance to build first AI data center in India

Meta has signed its first AI data center agreement in India, partnering with Reliance to establish a 168-megawatt facility that will support the company's global artificial intelligence computing operations. The deal marks a significant expansion of Meta's infrastructure footprint in one of the world's largest internet markets.

The data center will provide computing power for Meta's AI workloads across its global network. The facility has been designed with expansion capabilities, allowing for additional capacity to be added as Meta's computational needs grow over time. The partnership with Reliance, one of India's largest conglomerates, gives Meta access to local infrastructure expertise and established operational capabilities in the country.

India represents a strategic location for tech companies building out AI infrastructure. The country offers a combination of growing technical talent, expanding digital infrastructure, and increasing government support for technology investments. Meta joins other major technology firms that have established or expanded data center operations in India in recent years to support both regional services and global computing demands.

The 168-megawatt capacity positions this facility as a substantial addition to Meta's global data center network. Modern AI training and inference operations require significant electrical power and cooling systems, making energy capacity a key factor in data center planning. The ability to expand the facility suggests Meta anticipates growing computational requirements as it continues developing and deploying AI models across its platforms.

Meta has been investing heavily in AI infrastructure to support its development of large language models and AI features across Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, and other services. The company has previously built data centers in the United States, Europe, and other regions, but this represents its first dedicated AI computing facility in India. The partnership with Reliance may also provide Meta with regulatory and operational advantages as it navigates India's technology landscape and compliance requirements.
