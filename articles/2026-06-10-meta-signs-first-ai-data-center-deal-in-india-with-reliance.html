Meta signs first AI data center deal in India with Reliance

Meta has signed its first data center agreement in India with Reliance to support its global artificial intelligence computing needs. The facility will provide 168 megawatts of capacity and can be expanded over time.

The deal marks Meta's entry into India's data center infrastructure market as the company scales its AI operations worldwide. Reliance, one of India's largest conglomerates, will provide the computing infrastructure for Meta's AI workloads.

The 168-megawatt capacity represents substantial computing power dedicated to AI applications. Data centers of this scale typically house thousands of servers and require significant cooling and power infrastructure. The agreement includes provisions for future expansion, suggesting Meta anticipates growing computing demands in the region.

India has emerged as a strategic location for technology companies building AI infrastructure. The country offers a combination of technical talent, growing digital markets, and competitive costs for data center operations. Major technology companies have increasingly invested in Indian data center capacity to serve both local and global computing needs.

Meta has been rapidly expanding its AI computing infrastructure as it develops and deploys large language models and other AI technologies across its platforms. The company has invested billions in AI research and development, requiring substantial computing resources for training and running AI models. This India partnership follows similar data center investments Meta has made in other regions to support its AI ambitions.
