Von Miller signs one-year deal with Dallas Cowboys

Eight-time Pro Bowl pass rusher Von Miller has agreed to a one-year contract with the Dallas Cowboys, the team announced. The 37-year-old defensive end joins Dallas after spending the previous three seasons with the Buffalo Bills.

Miller expressed his excitement about joining the Cowboys, calling the signing "a dream within a dream." The veteran defender brings significant playoff experience to a Dallas defense looking to strengthen its pass rush heading into the season. Over 13 NFL seasons, Miller has accumulated 126.5 career sacks and earned widespread recognition as one of the league's premier edge rushers.

The Cowboys made this move to bolster their defensive front before the regular season begins. Miller's addition provides Dallas with a proven pass rusher who has won two Super Bowl championships during his NFL career. He earned Super Bowl 50 MVP honors during his time with the Denver Broncos, demonstrating his ability to perform at the highest level when it matters most.

Miller's veteran presence is expected to integrate well with the Cowboys' existing defensive lineup. His experience and leadership can benefit younger players on the roster while providing Dallas with an immediate impact on the field. The one-year contract structure offers flexibility to both parties while allowing Miller to compete for another championship.

The Cowboys have not disclosed specific financial terms of the agreement. Miller's signing addresses a key defensive need for Dallas as the team prepares to compete in a challenging division featuring high-powered offenses. The veteran pass rusher is expected to participate in team activities right away as the Cowboys finalize their roster before the season opener.

Despite being in the later stages of his career, Miller remains capable of producing at an elite level. His ability to pressure opposing quarterbacks and mentor younger defensive players makes him a valuable addition to the roster. The signing demonstrates Dallas's commitment to strengthening their defensive capabilities for the upcoming campaign.

Miller's track record of success in the playoffs and his championship experience provide the Cowboys with valuable assets as they pursue their Super Bowl aspirations. His arrival sends a message about the team's intentions to compete for a division title and make a deep playoff run.

The veteran defender joins a Cowboys team looking to make significant defensive improvements. With Miller now in the fold, Dallas has addressed one of their primary roster needs heading into the new season. His signing represents a significant effort by the organization to enhance their pass rush capabilities and strengthen their overall defensive performance.
