Mbappé breaks France's all-time scoring record in World Cup opener against Senegal

Kylian Mbappé scored twice in France's World Cup opener against Senegal, becoming his country's all-time leading goalscorer at just 27 years old. The striker netted his 13th and 14th World Cup goals during the match, moving into a tie for third place on the tournament's all-time scoring list. France secured a 3-1 victory in their opening group stage fixture.

Mbappé's two goals pushed his total for France past the previous national record, a remarkable achievement in his second World Cup appearance for Les Bleus. The forward's 14 World Cup goals place him alongside historic names in tournament history, with only two players having scored more goals across all World Cup editions combined.

France's performance improved significantly after a sluggish first half. The team delivered a second-half display that combined physical intensity with technical excellence, showcasing the kind of football that separates top-tier nations from the rest. Mbappé's first goal came just after the hour mark when he tucked away a superb pass from Michael Olise, transforming what had been a closely contested match into a procession.

The match included a controversial moment when Mbappé went down under a tackle from Senegal's Sadio Mane inside the penalty area. After VAR review, referees decided against awarding France a penalty, a decision that left the French team and observers bewildered. The call generated significant attention from match officials and analysts.

Mbappé's rise to become France's record scorer has been both methodical and remarkably swift. He has surpassed legendary French forwards who required entire careers to accumulate comparable goal tallies for the national team. His scoring rate for France has significantly outpaced his predecessors, with his World Cup performances forming a substantial portion of his international goal total.

Olise's performance was particularly noteworthy, with his playmaking abilities helping orchestrate France's dominant second-half display. His creativity from midfield provided the platform for much of France's attacking success and set the stage for Mbappé's record-breaking performance.

France now looks ahead to their remaining group stage matches as they pursue another deep tournament run. The team will rely on Mbappé to maintain his impressive scoring form as they navigate their way through the competition. Senegal, despite the loss, demonstrated strong play throughout and will seek to regroup for their next group stage fixture. The opening match suggested that the World Cup favourites have the quality to deliver on their potential, though their first-half struggles hint that consistency will be crucial in their pursuit of the tournament.
