Daveigh Chase, Voice of Lilo and Star of The Ring, Dies at 35

Actress Daveigh Chase has died at age 35 from sepsis following meningitis and blood infections. Her boyfriend, Roy Hernandez, confirmed the news after Chase was admitted to a Los Angeles hospital earlier this month.

Chase was best known for voicing the character Lilo in Disney's 2002 animated film Lilo and Stitch. That same year, she appeared in the horror film The Ring, playing Samara Morgan, the ghost at the center of the story. The Ring became a commercial success and established Chase as a recognizable talent in Hollywood during the early 2000s.

Her voice acting work extended beyond Disney. Chase also voiced Chihiro Ogino in the English dub of Studio Ghibli's acclaimed animated film Spirited Away. On screen, she had roles in the films Donnie Darko and appeared in the television series Sabrina the Teenage Witch. She also starred in the HBO drama Big Love, which ran from 2006 to 2011, where she played Rhonda Volmer in the series about a polygamous family in Utah.

Born on July 24, 1990, in Las Vegas, Nevada, Chase built her career across voice acting, film, and television from childhood into adulthood. Her work in Lilo and Stitch introduced her voice to a generation of children, while The Ring showcased her ability to handle darker, more challenging roles.

According to Hernandez, Chase had been struggling with serious health issues and personal difficulties. In a GoFundMe page set up before her death, he described how Chase had faced "a difficult childhood and a painful falling out with her family" and had been "bullied and struggled to find safety and happiness in downtown LA." Hernandez wrote that when they met, he promised to protect her and provide the love and comfort she deserved.

Meningitis is an inflammation of the protective membranes surrounding the brain and spinal cord. When combined with blood infections, it can lead to sepsis, a life-threatening condition where the body's response to infection causes tissue damage and organ failure. The condition can progress rapidly and requires immediate medical treatment.

Chase's death represents a significant loss to the entertainment industry. She contributed to both family entertainment through her Disney voice work and horror cinema through The Ring. Her career demonstrated her versatility as a performer across multiple genres and mediums, from animated films to live-action television dramas. Those who knew her remember her contributions to popular culture during the early 2000s, when she was among the most recognizable young talents working in Hollywood.
