Daveigh Chase, Star of The Ring and Voice of Lilo, Dies at 35

Daveigh Chase, the actress known for her role as Samara Morgan in the 2002 horror film The Ring and as the voice of Lilo in Disney's Lilo & Stitch, has died at age 35. Multiple entertainment outlets confirmed her death on Wednesday.

Chase gained widespread recognition at age 12 for her portrayal of the ghostly Samara in The Ring, the American remake of the Japanese horror film Ringu. That same year, she voiced the character Lilo Pelekai in the animated feature Lilo & Stitch, a role she reprised in the film's sequels and the subsequent television series. Her dual breakthrough performances in 2002 established her as a prominent young actress in both live-action and voice acting.

Beyond her most famous roles, Chase appeared in various television series and films throughout her career. She had roles in shows including Charmed, ER, and Big Love. Her film work extended beyond The Ring franchise to include appearances in Donnie Darko and other independent productions. She also provided voice work for video games, including The Kingdom Hearts series.

Chase began her entertainment career at a young age, appearing in commercials and minor television roles before landing her breakout parts in 2002. Her performance in The Ring helped define a generation of American horror cinema, while her work as Lilo brought warmth and authenticity to one of Disney's most beloved modern characters. The contrast between these two roles demonstrated her range as a performer.

No cause of death has been publicly disclosed. Chase's work continues to reach audiences through streaming platforms and home media, with both The Ring and Lilo & Stitch remaining popular titles more than two decades after their initial releases.
