Patriots fear quarterback Drake Maye suffered fractured ankle that could end his season

The New England Patriots believe quarterback Drake Maye may have fractured his ankle during Sunday's 35-10 loss to the Detroit Lions in Munich, sources told ESPN. Maye could miss the remainder of the season after being sacked in the first quarter by Lions defensive end Aidan Hutchinson, who suffered a torn ACL in his right knee while celebrating the play and will miss the rest of the year, sources told NFL Network.

Patriots coach Mike Vrabel told reporters Monday he did not expect Maye to play when New England returns from its bye week to face the Los Angeles Chargers, but declined to discuss specifics about the injury or timeline. Maye remained in Germany and did not travel back with the team as a precaution, Vrabel said during a radio interview. The coach added he does not anticipate seeking a quarterback replacement from outside the organization.

Maye was replaced Sunday by backup Tommy DeVito. The Patriots also have rookie Behren Morton, a seventh-round draft pick from Texas Tech, on the roster. Maye's streak of 43 consecutive starts, including playoffs, is the third longest among active NFL quarterbacks behind Buffalo's Josh Allen and Detroit's Jared Goff.

Hutchinson brought Maye down from behind, landing on the quarterback's lower leg. After leaping to celebrate the sack, Hutchinson landed awkwardly and immediately clutched his knee. Fox rules analyst Mike Pereira and the NFL's officiating rules analyst Walt Anderson both confirmed the tackle was not a hip-drop, which requires unweighting, swiveling, and landing on the back of the leg below the knee. No penalty was called on the play. Vrabel said he did not believe there was any ill intent.
