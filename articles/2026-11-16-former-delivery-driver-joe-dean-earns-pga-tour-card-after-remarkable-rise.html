Former delivery driver Joe Dean earns PGA Tour card after remarkable rise

Former supermarket delivery driver Joe Dean earned a place on the PGA Tour after finishing the season-ending World Tour Championship on Sunday, capping a whirlwind year for the English golfer. Dean, 32, was ranked 2,930th in the world two years ago while working part time to fund his golf career. He secured the 10th and final qualifying spot in the Race to Dubai rankings after a last-hole bogey by Mikael Lindberg moved him back into position, having briefly dropped out after shooting two over par in his final round.

Dean's breakthrough came in August when he won the British Masters, his first DP World Tour victory in his 70th event. That win earned him £440,000 in prize money, more than he had made in total from his previous 19 tournaments in the 2026 season. In July, he had earned a place at The Open after winning the inaugural Last-Chance Qualifier. His PGA Tour card will allow him to compete for more lucrative prize money on the American circuit.

Despite his impressive rise, Dean recently told Today's Golfer that he finds the sport stressful. He said that if he could earn a better living doing something else, he probably would, adding that even when playing well, he does not find it fun due to the pressure. He said he finds relaxing activities like sitting by a fishing lake or cleaning his car more enjoyable.

Patrick Reed also secured his return to the PGA Tour after a four-year absence. The 36-year-old American and 2018 U.S. Masters champion left LIV Golf at the start of this year to play on the DP World Tour, then won two tournaments early in the season to earn one of the 10 PGA Tour cards available through the European circuit.
