Wave of bomb threats and cyberattacks across Europe raises questions about coordination

A series of bomb threats targeting airlines and cyberattacks on critical infrastructure across Europe has disrupted everyday life and heightened security concerns, though the perpetrators remain unidentified. The incidents have affected multiple sectors, including aviation, healthcare, and food supply systems.

Cyberattacks targeted European refrigeration systems and critical health infrastructure, including a French blood bank. Security researchers reported that hackers had probed Europe's commercial refrigeration networks weeks before the attacks occurred. A health intelligence-sharing group issued warnings to the healthcare industry following the breach of the French blood bank.

The airline industry faced a wave of bomb hoaxes that forced flight disruptions. The attacks on freezer systems caused meltdowns at facilities across the continent, threatening stored medical supplies and perishable goods.

Authorities have not publicly identified who is responsible for the coordinated disruptions. The nature and timing of the incidents have raised questions about whether the attacks are linked and whether they represent a broader campaign targeting European infrastructure.
