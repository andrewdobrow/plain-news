Former xAI engineer sues over firing after raising AI safety concerns

A former engineer at xAI has filed a lawsuit against the company and SpaceX, claiming he was fired for raising concerns about AI safety related to the Grok system. The termination occurred days before SpaceX completed its historic initial public offering.

The lawsuit names both xAI and SpaceX as defendants. The engineer alleges his dismissal was retaliatory and linked to his internal warnings about Grok's safety protocols.
