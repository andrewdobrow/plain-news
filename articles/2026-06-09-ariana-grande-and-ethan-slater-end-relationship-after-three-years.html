Ariana Grande and Ethan Slater Split After Three Years Together

Ariana Grande and Ethan Slater have ended their relationship after three years of dating, according to multiple reports. The pair met while filming the first Wicked movie, where Grande played Glinda and Slater portrayed Boq.

The separation happened several months ago, though news of the split only recently became public. Grande is currently launching her Eternal Sunshine tour as a single woman. The relationship began on set and drew significant media attention from the start, as both actors were in previous relationships when they met during filming. Grande was married to Dalton Gomez at the time, while Slater was married to Lilly Jay, with whom he shares a child. Both marriages ended before Grande and Slater began dating publicly.

The first Wicked film, which brought the two together, became a major success, earning Grande critical acclaim and an Oscar nomination for her performance. The movie adaptation of the beloved Broadway musical introduced both performers to new audiences and performed exceptionally well at the box office. Their relationship lasted through the film's production, release, and awards season.

Slater, who gained wider recognition for his role in Wicked after previously appearing in SpongeBob SquarePants: The Broadway Musical, has continued his acting work. Grande's career has similarly moved forward following the split. The singer has maintained her professional commitments throughout the relationship and its ending, with her Eternal Sunshine tour marking a new chapter as she returns to live performances.

The split comes during a significant time for the Wicked franchise. The sequel, Wicked: For Good, was released recently and has received considerable attention. However, the film's release has also been marked by other notable incidents involving the cast. During the Singapore premiere, co-star Cynthia Erivo intervened when an individual jumped a barrier and rushed toward Grande on the red carpet. Erivo described the moment as frightening for both performers, noting that the person would not release their grip on Grande. The individual was subsequently arrested and sentenced to nine days in jail.

Erivo later spoke about the incident's aftermath and how public reactions affected her. She expressed frustration with how her actions were interpreted, particularly regarding assumptions made based on her appearance. These experiences influenced her decision regarding her own involvement in awards campaigns for the sequel.

The Wicked franchise continues to be a significant part of both Grande's and Slater's careers, though their romantic relationship has now concluded. As both actors move forward separately, they continue to be involved in projects stemming from their work on the musical adaptation.
