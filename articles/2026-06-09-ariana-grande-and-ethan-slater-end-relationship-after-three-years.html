Ariana Grande and Ethan Slater Split After Three Years

Pop star Ariana Grande and actor Ethan Slater have ended their relationship after nearly three years together, according to multiple reports. The couple met while working together on the first Wicked film, where Grande played Glinda and Slater portrayed Boq.

Grande's performance in the musical adaptation earned her significant recognition, including an Oscar nomination. The film became a major commercial success, collecting $765 million at the worldwide box office and winning two Academy Awards.

Grande and Slater made their relationship public in 2023, following the end of their previous relationships. Their romance generated considerable media attention at the time due to the circumstances surrounding their coupling and their high-profile status in entertainment.

Slater is known for his theatrical work, including a Tony-nominated performance in SpongeBob SquarePants: The Musical. He joined the Wicked cast in a supporting role alongside Grande, who is an acclaimed Grammy-winning musician with a large global following.

Neither Grande nor Slater has issued a public statement regarding the split. The breakup comes as both continue advancing their careers in their respective fields. Grande maintains an active presence in music while Slater pursues acting work.

Sources close to the situation have not revealed specific details about what led to the separation or how it might affect their professional obligations to the Wicked franchise, given that both starred in the first film and appear set to continue with upcoming sequels.

The couple had maintained a relatively private relationship throughout their time together, despite their celebrity status. After the initial media coverage when they first became public, they generally avoided extensive public displays of their romance.

The Wicked film series has been significant in Grande's career, marking a major transition from her work as a recording artist to the film industry. The first installment's critical and commercial success established her as a serious actress, though subsequent projects in the franchise have performed differently at the box office.

Slater's involvement in the Wicked adaptations represents a continuation of his work across both stage and screen productions. His casting in the musical film series built upon his established reputation in theatrical performances.

As both entertainers navigate life following the separation, they will likely continue to engage with professional commitments that may require them to interact, particularly regarding the Wicked franchise that brought them together initially.
