Ariana Grande and Ethan Slater end relationship after three years

Ariana Grande and Ethan Slater have separated after three years together, ending a relationship that began on the set of the first Wicked film. The pair met while working on the movie adaptation of the popular Broadway musical, which led to Grande receiving an Oscar nomination for her performance.

The relationship attracted significant public attention when it began in 2023, as both Grande and Slater were ending previous relationships at the time. Grande was married to real estate agent Dalton Gomez, while Slater was married to his high school sweetheart Lilly Jay, with whom he shares a son. The timing of their romance sparked widespread media coverage and public discussion.

Grande's role in Wicked marked a significant moment in her career transition from pop music to film acting. She portrayed Glinda the Good Witch in the adaptation directed by Jon M. Chu. The first Wicked film became one of the highest-grossing musical films, earning $765 million in worldwide box office receipts and winning two Academy Awards. Her performance earned critical acclaim and led to her first Oscar nomination, cementing her status as a multifaceted entertainer capable of success across different mediums.

Slater, a Broadway veteran who originated the role of SpongeBob SquarePants in the musical adaptation, played Boq in the Wicked film. He had built a respected career in theater before transitioning to film work. The couple had largely kept their relationship private in recent months, making few public appearances together.

The end of their relationship comes as the Wicked sequel has performed markedly differently from its predecessor. Wicked: For Good took $541 million at the worldwide box office and received no Oscar nominations, a significant decrease from the first film's performance and accolades.

Recent weeks have seen notable developments surrounding the Wicked cast. During the Singapore premiere of Wicked: For Good, co-star Cynthia Erivo stepped in when a red-carpet invader rushed towards Grande. Erivo described the moment as frightening for both herself and Grande, noting that the intruder would not let go of Grande. The individual was sentenced to nine days in jail.

Erivo later discussed how social media reactions to the incident revealed problematic perceptions of Black women. She explained that comments suggesting she was Grande's bodyguard reflected assumptions about her appearance and role. Erivo said she felt her instinctive protective action had been mischaracterized, noting that the experience discouraged her from campaigning for Oscar recognition for the sequel.

Neither Grande nor Slater has publicly commented on their separation. Grande continues to balance her music career with acting projects, while Slater remains active in both film and theater work.
