Mexico defeats South Africa 2-0 in World Cup opening match

Mexico launched the 2026 World Cup with a 2-0 victory over South Africa at Azteca Stadium. Goals from Quinones and Jimenez secured the win for the host nation in the tournament's opening match. South Africa finished the game with nine men after three red cards were issued during the contest.

The match followed a star-studded opening ceremony featuring Shakira and took place amid sporadic clashes between police and protesters around the stadium. Mexico's president had ordered schools closed and implemented remote work policies due to the World Cup. The victory provided a moment of celebration for the host country amid broader tensions in Mexico City.
