SpaceX Sets $1.77 Trillion Valuation Ahead of Record-Breaking IPO

SpaceX has acquired Elon Musk's artificial intelligence company xAI in a major merger that consolidates parts of his business empire as the aerospace company prepares for a public listing. The deal, announced Monday on SpaceX's website, values SpaceX at $1 trillion and xAI at $250 billion, setting the combined entity on course for a stock market valuation exceeding $1 trillion.

The merger creates what the companies described as "the most ambitious, vertically-integrated innovation engine on (and off) Earth, with AI, rockets, space-based internet, direct-to-mobile device communications and the world's foremost real-time information and free speech platform." Through the acquisition, SpaceX gains xAI's Grok chatbot and the social media platform X.

The consolidation reflects Musk's strategy to combine space technology with artificial intelligence infrastructure. Both companies cited plans for space-based datacenters powered by solar-powered satellites as central to the merger's rationale. According to the announcement, current AI advances depend on large terrestrial datacenters that consume enormous amounts of power and cooling. The companies stated that "global electricity demand for AI simply cannot be met with terrestrial solutions, even in the near term, without imposing hardship on communities and the environment" and that "in the long term, space-based AI is obviously the only way to scale."

The IPO is expected to occur in early summer, timed to coincide with a planetary alignment and Musk's birthday on June 28. Musk confirmed the deal minimally on X, responding with "yes" to posts referencing merger reports.

Both companies have experienced significant valuation growth recently. SpaceX revealed an expected value of $800 billion in a December letter to investors as it continues dominating satellite launches and securing contracts with the U.S. federal government. xAI raised $20 billion in a Series E funding round last month that valued the company at $230 billion, despite controversy surrounding its Grok chatbot over promotion of racist content and nonconsensual deepfake images.

The merger comes as Musk has increasingly intertwined his various business interests. xAI acquired the platform X in an all-stock transaction in early 2025, and Tesla announced plans last month to invest $2 billion in xAI.

The announcement provides Musk with positive momentum after Tesla released disappointing earnings showing declining revenues and a struggling car business. The news also comes days after the Department of Justice released files related to Jeffrey Epstein that showed Musk exchanging friendly emails with the disgraced financier. Musk called the email release a "distraction" and stated he was "well aware that some email correspondence with him could be misinterpreted and used by detractors to smear my name."
