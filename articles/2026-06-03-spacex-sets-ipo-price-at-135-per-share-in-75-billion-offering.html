SpaceX sets share price at $135 for largest IPO in history

SpaceX set its initial public offering price at $135 per share, valuing the company at $1.75 trillion to $1.77 trillion. The offering will raise $75 billion to $85 billion, making it the largest IPO ever and surpassing Saudi Aramco's 2019 debut in both valuation and capital raised.

The space exploration company plans to list on the Nasdaq next week. Elon Musk's rocket maker announced the target price earlier than market observers expected. The capital raise reflects SpaceX's growing financing needs as it expands operations.
