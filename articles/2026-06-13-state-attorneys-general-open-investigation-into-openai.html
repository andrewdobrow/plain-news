State attorneys general investigate OpenAI over data practices

OpenAI is under investigation by state attorneys general who are examining the company's advertising policies and how it handles health data. The specific states involved in the probe have not been publicly identified.

The investigation focuses on multiple aspects of OpenAI's operations, including its advertising practices and the management of health information collected through its services. State-level investigations of this kind can proceed independently of federal oversight and may ultimately result in enforcement actions, financial settlements, or requirements for the company to change how it operates.

OpenAI has increasingly drawn regulatory attention as its products have gained widespread adoption. The company operates ChatGPT and develops large language models that millions of people use globally. Regulators examining AI companies have placed particular emphasis on how these firms handle user data, especially sensitive categories like health information.

State attorneys general have become more engaged in regulating technology companies in recent years. Their focus has centered on consumer protection, data privacy, and advertising practices. These officials have the power to enforce state consumer protection laws and can pursue remedies ranging from financial penalties to mandatory changes in business practices.

The investigation reflects broader questions about how AI companies collect, store, and utilize user data. As these systems become more integrated into daily life and handle increasingly sensitive information, regulators have stepped up scrutiny of the industry's data management practices.

The full scope of the investigation and its expected timeline remain unclear. OpenAI has not made any public statements about the probe or confirmed whether it has received formal information requests from state officials.

This marks the latest regulatory challenge for OpenAI, which has faced mounting scrutiny from government agencies as artificial intelligence technology has become more prominent. The company's practices around data handling and advertising are now subjects of examination at the state level, where attorneys general have demonstrated a growing willingness to challenge major technology firms over consumer protection concerns.

The outcome of such investigations can vary considerably. Some result in quick settlements, while others lead to protracted legal disputes and significant changes to company operations. State-level actions can also prompt investigations by other state officials, potentially creating a coordinated multi-state effort to address shared concerns about a company's practices.

For OpenAI, this investigation adds to an already complex regulatory environment. The company faces questions about its data practices, business model, and the societal impact of its technology from multiple levels of government and regulatory bodies.
