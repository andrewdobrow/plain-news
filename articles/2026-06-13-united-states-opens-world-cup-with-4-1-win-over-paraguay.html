Mbappe scores twice as France opens World Cup with win over Senegal

Kylian Mbappé scored twice as France defeated Senegal 3-1 in their opening Group I match of the 2026 World Cup, delivering an impressive performance that showcased the attacking prowess of the tournament favorites.

Mbappé's brace proved decisive in securing the victory, with his goals coming at crucial moments during the match. After a sluggish first-half display, France transformed their performance in the second period, with Mbappé breaking through just after the hour mark after receiving a superb pass from Michael Olise. He added his second goal in the closing stages of added time, cementing France's dominance as the match wore on.

The 27-year-old's two-goal haul holds particular significance for French football. His 58th international goal moves him ahead of Olivier Giroud as France's all-time leading scorer, a milestone achieved during what many regard as the prime years of his career. On the World Cup stage specifically, Mbappé has now scored 14 goals across his tournament appearances.

Olise's playmaking abilities also shone throughout the contest, with the midfielder proving instrumental in France's attacking flow. The performance demonstrated the quality that has led defender Lucas Hernandez to claim France possess "the best attack in the world," featuring Mbappé as captain alongside Ballon d'Or winner Ousmane Dembélé and other attacking talent.

France's victory came after a concerning first half that raised questions about their approach under manager Didier Deschamps. The coach has long been criticized for his conservative tactics, though his track record of reaching back-to-back World Cup finals and a Euros final during his 12-year tenure validates his pragmatic philosophy. Recent friendlies against Brazil and Colombia suggested Deschamps might be willing to embrace a more expansive style, and this performance against Senegal indicated that balance may have been found.

While Senegal mounted a late challenge and managed to score, they ultimately could not breach France's defensive organization. France's defense remains a cornerstone of their setup, with only four European nations conceding fewer qualifying goals than Les Bleus.

The opening victory places France in a strong position to advance from their group as they pursue another World Cup title. Mbappé's record-breaking performance sets the tone for what many expect to be a formidable tournament campaign from the favorites, combining the attacking flair that has defined their recent matches with the defensive solidity that has underpinned their success under Deschamps.
