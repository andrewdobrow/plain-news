Balogun scores twice as United States opens World Cup with 4-1 win over Paraguay

United States striker Folarin Balogun described his performance in Friday's 4-1 World Cup victory over Paraguay as a "dreamy night" after scoring twice in his first World Cup appearance for the Americans.

Balogun, who had the option to represent England or Nigeria at international level, chose to play for the United States and made an immediate impact in the team's opening match of the tournament. His two goals helped secure a convincing victory that gave the USMNT a strong foundation as they begin their World Cup campaign.

The striker's decision to commit his international future to the United States proved worthwhile as he delivered a standout performance when it mattered most. With two goals in the opening fixture, Balogun demonstrated why he was such a significant addition to the American squad heading into the competition.
