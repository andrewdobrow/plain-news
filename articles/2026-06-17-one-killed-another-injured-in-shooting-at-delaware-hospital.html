One killed, another injured in shooting at Delaware hospital

A 23-year-old man was taken into custody in Philadelphia following a shooting at Wilmington Hospital that left one person dead and another wounded. The suspect was apprehended after a manhunt prompted the hospital to go into lockdown, with employees barricading themselves inside rooms for safety.

The shooting occurred around 3:30 p.m. on Tuesday at the hospital in Wilmington, Delaware's largest city. Wilmington Police Chief Wilfredo Campos confirmed the incident but declined to release identifying information about the victims or details about the condition of the person who survived.

Hospital officials determined the shooting was a "targeted, isolated incident," meaning it was not a random act of violence. The hospital's operator, ChristianaCare, said it was redirecting patients away from its emergency department and "taking all appropriate steps to ensure the safety of our patients, caregivers and visitors."

The lockdown was lifted later in the day as police completed their initial investigation. No further details were provided about any relationship between the suspect and the victims or what motivated the shooting.

The incident adds to ongoing concerns about violence in healthcare settings. Hospitals across the United States have faced increasing security challenges in recent years as workplace violence remains a persistent problem at medical facilities nationwide.

Wilmington, which has approximately 71,000 residents, is located about 25 miles south of Philadelphia. The hospital serves as a major medical center for the northern Delaware region, and the disruption affected normal operations during the police investigation and sweep of the facility.

The shooting prompted a significant law enforcement response as officers worked to secure the area, locate the suspect, and ensure no additional threats remained on the premises. Emergency medical personnel attended to both victims immediately after the incident was reported.

Your original article stated the suspect was arrested, and the source material confirms this took place in Philadelphia following the incident. The source also clarifies that the shooting was determined to be targeted rather than random, and provides additional context about the hospital's response and the broader issue of violence in healthcare settings.
