24 Jump Street in Development With Original Stars in Talks to Return

A third installment in the Jump Street comedy franchise is in development, with Jonah Hill, Channing Tatum, and Ice Cube in talks to return for 24 Jump Street. The project marks a return to the series that began with 21 Jump Street in 2012 and continued with 22 Jump Street in 2014.

The original films followed two undercover cops, played by Hill and Tatum, as they infiltrated high school and college to investigate drug rings. Ice Cube portrayed their commanding officer. The movies became critical and commercial successes, with 21 Jump Street earning over $200 million worldwide and 22 Jump Street surpassing $330 million globally. Both films were praised for their chemistry between the leads and their blend of action and comedy.

Tatum has been publicly pushing for a third film for years. The actor previously expressed his desire to bring the franchise back, and his persistence appears to have paid off. The development of 24 Jump Street comes more than a decade after the last installment, making it one of the longest gaps between sequels in the series.

No director has been announced for the project, though Phil Lord and Christopher Miller directed the first two films. The previous installments were produced by Columbia Pictures and distributed by Sony Pictures. Details about the plot and production timeline have not been disclosed.

The Jump Street franchise originated as a reboot of the 1987 television series of the same name, which launched Johnny Depp's career. The films successfully transformed the source material into a comedic take on the buddy cop genre, earning praise for self-awareness and humor. The return of the original cast would continue the story of the partnership between Hill and Tatum's characters, whose dynamic anchored both previous films.
