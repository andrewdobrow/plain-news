White Sox Rookie Braden Montgomery Hits Walk-Off Home Run in Major League Debut

Braden Montgomery made a memorable entrance to major league baseball, hitting a two-run home run in the 10th inning of his debut to lift the Chicago White Sox over the Atlanta Braves 6-5. The dramatic finish marked one of the most impressive debut performances in recent baseball history.

The rookie's game-winning blast in extra innings gave the White Sox a comeback victory and provided an immediate impact from their new call-up. Montgomery described the moment as surreal, delivering in the highest-pressure situation possible during his first appearance in a major league uniform.
