Knicks win first NBA championship in 53 years behind Brunson's 45 points

The New York Knicks claimed their first NBA championship since 1973 by defeating the San Antonio Spurs 94-90 in Game 5 of the Finals series. The victory ended a 53-year drought and delivered the franchise its third title overall.

Jalen Brunson led the Knicks to victory with a dominant 45-point performance in the decisive game. The point guard's scoring outburst proved essential down the stretch as New York secured the championship on the road. Brunson's Finals performance cemented his status as a crucial figure in franchise history and demonstrated his ability to elevate his game when it mattered most.

The Knicks' path to the championship showcased remarkable resilience throughout the playoffs. Before facing San Antonio, New York had won 11 consecutive games while dominating opponents. The team also came back from a 22-point deficit against the Cleveland Cavaliers in the Eastern Conference Finals, showing a pattern of overcoming adversity.

The Finals series against the Spurs was competitive throughout. San Antonio entered the matchup as a formidable opponent, having eliminated the defending champion Oklahoma City Thunder in the previous round. The Spurs were led by Victor Wembanyama, who many considered the most exciting player in the world.

In Game 5, the Knicks trailed by 14 points at one stage during the third quarter but mounted a comeback to take control. New York's defense and efficiency in the final moments proved decisive, with the team executing crucial plays when needed.

Beyond Brunson's scoring, other Knicks players contributed significantly to the championship run. The team's depth and ability to maintain composure in tight moments reflected strong chemistry and preparation throughout the season.

The Spurs' Victor Wembanyama struggled in the Finals series and was unable to carry San Antonio to victory. His performance fell short of expectations as the Knicks' defense effectively limited his impact at times during the series.

The championship ends a long period of frustration for the Knicks franchise and their passionate fan base. New York fans poured into the streets to celebrate the historic victory, marking one of the most significant moments in the team's history. The celebration reflected the relief and joy of a city that had waited over five decades for an NBA title.

The 2026 championship represents a major turnaround for a franchise that struggled through much of the previous 50 years. The Knicks' Finals victory over San Antonio culminated a remarkable season and delivered the city its first NBA championship since the early 1970s. Brunson's outstanding Finals performance will be remembered alongside the greatest moments in New York basketball history.
