Tarik Skubal returns to Tigers rotation six weeks after elbow surgery

Tarik Skubal will take the mound Saturday when the Detroit Tigers face the Cleveland Guardians, marking the left-hander's return to action. The Tigers ace underwent elbow surgery less than six weeks ago, making his recovery timeline notably swift.

Skubal's return provides a significant boost to Detroit's rotation as they continue their season. The left-hander has been a cornerstone of the Tigers pitching staff, and his absence left a void that the team struggled to fill during his recovery period.
