12 people shot near street festival in Toledo, Ohio

At least 12 people were wounded in a shooting near a street festival in Toledo, Ohio, according to authorities. Police say the incident appears to have involved two people firing weapons at each other rather than targeting the crowd indiscriminately.

The shooting took place on Saturday near the community festival location. Victims were transported to nearby hospitals following the incident. Two of the wounded were reported to be in critical condition, according to Toledo deputy police chief Joe Heffernan.

"It appeared there were at least two people firing weapons who were probably shooting at each other," Heffernan said.

Law enforcement launched a search for the suspects involved in the shooting. Police are working to identify and locate the individuals responsible for opening fire near the festival grounds. The investigation into what prompted the gunfire is ongoing.

The victims range in age from 14 to 61 years old, demonstrating the broad impact of the shooting on the community. Details about the specific injuries sustained by those shot or their current medical conditions were not immediately released by authorities.

The shooting represents another instance of gun violence occurring at a public gathering in an American community. The incident disrupted what was intended to be a community celebration, forcing emergency responders to the scene.

Police have continued their investigation to determine the full circumstances surrounding the shooting and to apprehend those responsible for the gunfire. Authorities are urging anyone with information about the incident to come forward.
