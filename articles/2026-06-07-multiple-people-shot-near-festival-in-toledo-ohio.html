At least 12 people shot near festival in Toledo, Ohio

At least 12 people were wounded in a shooting near a community festival in Toledo, Ohio on Saturday, with police continuing to search for suspects.

The incident occurred near the Old West End Festival, according to authorities. The Toledo Police Department confirmed that multiple victims were transported to nearby hospitals for treatment.

Toledo Deputy Police Chief Joe Heffernan said that two of the wounded were in critical condition. He indicated that the shooting appeared to involve at least two people firing weapons at each other, rather than a single shooter targeting the crowd.

The search for suspects remains active, with law enforcement working to locate those responsible. Police have not announced any arrests related to the incident.

The Old West End Festival is an annual community event held in Toledo's historic neighborhood. The festival typically features food vendors, music, and entertainment that draw crowds to the area. The exact circumstances of how the shooting related to the festival activities have not been fully detailed by authorities.

Law enforcement responded quickly to reports of gunfire and secured the scene. The Toledo Police Department released statements indicating that "many victims" had been taken for medical treatment following the shooting.

Details about the specific conditions of all victims or whether any fatalities occurred have not been released by police. Authorities have also not provided descriptions of the suspects they are seeking in connection with the incident.

The investigation is ongoing as police work to gather more information about what led to the shooting and to apprehend those involved.
