Multiple people shot near festival in Toledo, Ohio

Multiple people have been shot near the Old West End Festival in Toledo, Ohio, with authorities still searching for suspects, according to the Toledo Police Department.

The shooting took place Saturday afternoon near the festival, a community event typically held in the neighborhood. Police and medical personnel responded to the scene in large numbers following the incident.

"Many victims" have been taken for medical treatment, the Toledo Police Department said in a statement. The department is actively investigating the shooting and has asked the public to avoid the area while the search for suspects continues.

The Old West End Festival is an annual gathering in Toledo that usually serves as a family-oriented community event. The shooting disrupted what is typically a peaceful celebration in the neighborhood.

Authorities have not yet disclosed the total number of people injured or specific details about the condition of the victims. The investigation is ongoing as law enforcement works to identify and locate those responsible for the attack.

Police have urged anyone with information about the incident to contact the Toledo Police Department as the investigation develops.
