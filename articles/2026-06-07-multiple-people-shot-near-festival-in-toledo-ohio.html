12 injured in shooting near Ohio festival, suspects sought

Twelve people were wounded when gunfire erupted near the Old West End festival in Toledo, Ohio on Saturday evening, prompting an ongoing manhunt for at least two shooters.

The shooting occurred just after 5:30 p.m. near the festival, which features live music and architectural home tours. Nine adults and three teenagers were injured in the incident. According to Toledo police deputy chief Joseph Heffernan, the shooters were "probably shooting at each other" when the gunfire broke out.

Two of the victims were reported to be in critical condition, though complete details about all the injured have not been disclosed. All twelve victims survived and were transported to area hospitals for treatment.

The search for the suspects entered its second day as law enforcement intensified efforts to locate those responsible. The incident prompted festival organizers to cancel planned events on Sunday as the investigation continued.

Local prosecutors indicated they are pursuing swift and strong justice in the case. Police are urging anyone with information about the suspects or the shooting to come forward. Investigators are working to determine additional details about those involved and the circumstances that led to the gunfire.

The festival area remained closed as authorities collected evidence and conducted interviews with witnesses present during the shooting. Law enforcement has increased patrols in the area while searching for the suspects.

This incident is part of a troubling pattern of violence at public gatherings across the United States. The shooting has renewed concerns about safety measures at festivals and community events, particularly those that draw large crowds.

Police have not yet classified whether the shooting was targeted or random. The investigation is ongoing as authorities work to apprehend those responsible for the violence.
