Multiple people shot near street festival in Toledo, Ohio

At least 12 people were wounded in a shooting near the Old West End Festival in Toledo, Ohio on Saturday, according to authorities. The Toledo Police Department confirmed that multiple victims were transported to nearby hospitals for medical treatment.

Two of the injured remain in critical condition, Toledo deputy police chief Joe Heffernan said. He indicated that at least two people were firing weapons, and it appeared they were "probably shooting at each other."

The incident occurred in proximity to the community festival, creating a chaotic scene as attendees sought safety. Police say a search for the suspects remains ongoing, with no arrests announced at this time. Authorities have not yet released descriptions of the suspected shooters.

The Old West End Festival is an annual community event in Toledo. The shooting represents one of several mass shooting incidents occurring across the United States in recent months, raising ongoing concerns about public safety at public gatherings and outdoor events.

Police have asked anyone with information about the shooting to contact local authorities. Investigators are working to determine the sequence of events and identify those responsible for the attack. It remains unclear whether the festival will continue or be canceled in light of the incident.

The Toledo Police Department stated that "many victims" have been taken for medical treatment, though the exact circumstances surrounding all injuries are still being investigated.
