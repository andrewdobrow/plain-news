Golden Tempo Wins Belmont Stakes Month After Kentucky Derby Victory

Golden Tempo captured the 2026 Belmont Stakes on Saturday at Saratoga Race Course, five weeks after winning the Kentucky Derby. The victory keeps alive the horse's pursuit of a rare Triple Crown championship and adds another historic achievement to trainer Cherie DeVaux's career.

With jockey Jose Ortiz in the saddle, Golden Tempo surged down the stretch at the historic upstate New York track to secure the win at odds of 6-1. Commandment finished second while Renegade, the race favorite, came in third.

The Belmont Stakes represents the third leg of thoroughbred racing's Triple Crown. Golden Tempo's back-to-back victories in two of the sport's most prestigious races demonstrate exceptional performance across demanding competitions. The horse remains on track to become the first Triple Crown winner since 2018, when Justify completed the sweep.

DeVaux has continued to make history throughout her training career with Golden Tempo. The trainer's success with the horse underscores her prominence in a sport traditionally dominated by men. Her achievements have drawn significant attention from major sports outlets covering the racing season.

To complete the Triple Crown, Golden Tempo must win the Preakness Stakes, the final leg of racing's most celebrated achievement. Only thirteen horses have accomplished this feat in the modern era since Sir Barton became the first Triple Crown winner in 1919. The rarity of the achievement reflects the difficulty of maintaining peak performance across three demanding races over several weeks.

The Belmont Stakes has been held at various locations in recent years due to ongoing renovations at Belmont Park in Elmont, New York. Saratoga Race Course has served as an alternate venue during this period of construction. The Saratoga track is renowned for its historic grandstand and its traditional summer racing meet, which typically operates from July through Labor Day.

Golden Tempo's performance at Saratoga continues a remarkable run in 2026. The horse's ability to win at different tracks and under varying conditions suggests strong preparation and consistent quality. Racing analysts and enthusiasts will be watching closely to see whether the horse can maintain this momentum through the Preakness.

The pursuit of a Triple Crown remains one of thoroughbred racing's greatest challenges. Golden Tempo's current position in this historic quest has captured the attention of casual fans and dedicated racing followers alike. The upcoming Preakness will determine whether the horse joins an elite group of champions who have swept all three races.
