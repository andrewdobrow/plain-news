Tottenham agree £75m deal to sign Manchester City winger Savinho

Tottenham Hotspur have secured a transfer agreement with Manchester City for Brazilian winger Savinho, with the deal worth £75 million plus £10 million in potential add-ons.

The 22-year-old joined Manchester City through the City Football Group in summer 2022 for approximately £30.8 million. After loan spells at Troyes, PSV and Girona, he moved to the Etihad Stadium two years ago. However, he failed to establish himself as a regular starter under former manager Pep Guardiola, prompting City to sanction his departure.

The transfer represents a significant profit for Manchester City's owners, who stand to gain around £55 million on their initial investment in the player. For Tottenham, the signing marks a major statement of intent as the club looks to strengthen its attacking options ahead of the new season.

The deal comes as part of a busy summer for Tottenham under their current management. The north London club is simultaneously working on a separate agreement with Eintracht Frankfurt for forward Omar Marmoush, worth approximately £75 million. Combined, the two transfers would represent spending of around £160 million on attacking talent alone.

Tottenham's overall summer spending is approaching £400 million as they continue their squad rebuild. The club's willingness to commit substantial resources reflects their ambitions for both domestic and European competition in the coming campaign.

The agreement with Manchester City includes performance-related add-ons that could push the total value to £85 million. The transfer remains subject to standard medical examinations and the agreement of personal terms between the player and Tottenham.

Savinho's move to Spurs represents one of the most significant transfer moves of the summer window. The Brazilian's versatility and potential have made him an attractive prospect despite his limited opportunities at Manchester City. At 22 years old, he represents an investment in youth and development alongside more established signings.

The timing of the transfer allows Savinho to integrate into Tottenham's squad during pre-season preparation for the new campaign. His arrival, combined with the expected signing of Marmoush, would significantly enhance Tottenham's forward line and provide manager with increased attacking options.

For Manchester City, the sale represents smart business in terms of financial return on investment and squad management. The club has pursued a policy of moving players on who have not secured regular first-team football, and Savinho's departure follows that pattern.

The transfer window remains active, with the deadline approaching. Tottenham's activity in the market demonstrates the club's determination to build a competitive squad capable of challenging for honours across multiple competitions.
