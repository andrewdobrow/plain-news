Russia Launches Large-Scale Strikes on Kyiv, Multiple Casualties Reported

Russia carried out its largest missile and drone attacks on Kyiv in weeks, killing and injuring multiple people including children. Ukrainian officials described a significant number of casualties as explosions struck residential buildings across the capital. The strikes represented a major escalation in attacks on the city after a period of relative calm.

The assault involved coordinated waves of missiles and drones targeting various areas of Kyiv. Residential buildings sustained damage from the strikes, and emergency services responded to multiple locations across the city. Ukrainian air defense systems engaged the incoming threats, though many projectiles reached their targets. The attacks lasted several hours, forcing residents into shelters and disrupting daily life in the capital.

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy had warned just hours before the strikes that intelligence indicated Russia was preparing a massive attack. His warning proved accurate as the bombardment unfolded overnight and into the early morning hours. At least five people were injured in the attacks, according to initial reports from Ukrainian authorities.

The strikes come amid ongoing fuel shortages inside Russia caused by Ukrainian drone attacks on oil refineries. Those Ukrainian operations have disrupted Russian fuel supplies and caused gasoline prices to rise, creating lines at gas stations. The timing of the heavy strikes on Kyiv may represent an attempt by Moscow to demonstrate military capacity despite the domestic economic pressures.

Kyiv has experienced periodic attacks throughout the conflict, but the intensity of this bombardment marked a notable increase in Russian offensive operations against the capital. The use of both missiles and drones in large numbers reflects Russia's strategy of overwhelming Ukrainian air defenses through volume. The residential nature of the damage and the inclusion of children among the casualties underscores the civilian toll of the strikes.
