Russia Launches Large-Scale Strikes on Kyiv, Killing at Least 13

Russia carried out its largest missile and drone attacks on Kyiv in weeks, killing at least 13 people and injuring scores more across the Ukrainian capital. Ukrainian officials reported a significant number of casualties, including children, as the strikes caused widespread damage throughout the city.

The attacks represented a major escalation in Russian bombardment of the capital after a relative lull in recent weeks. Multiple neighborhoods sustained damage as missiles and drones struck residential and infrastructure targets. Emergency services worked through the morning to search damaged buildings and treat the wounded.

The assault on Kyiv was part of a broader wave of strikes across Ukraine. Poland scrambled fighter jets in response to the attack, while Finland imposed temporary airspace restrictions as a precautionary measure. The coordinated nature of the strikes and their intensity marked a shift in Russia's tactical approach to targeting the Ukrainian capital.

Ukrainian air defense systems intercepted some of the incoming missiles and drones, but the scale of the attack overwhelmed defenses in several areas. Officials described the operation as one of the most intensive Russian aerial campaigns against Kyiv since the early months of the conflict. The strikes hit during morning hours when many residents were beginning their daily routines.

The attack comes as international diplomatic efforts continue on multiple fronts related to regional conflicts. Ukrainian authorities have launched investigations into the strikes and are assessing the full extent of damage to civilian infrastructure. Medical facilities across Kyiv received casualties throughout the morning, with hospitals reporting they were treating both serious and minor injuries from the bombardment.
