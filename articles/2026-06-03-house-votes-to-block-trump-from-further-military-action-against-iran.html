U.S. 2026-06-04

House votes to block further military strikes on Iran

The House of Representatives passed a measure seeking to halt additional military action against Iran, delivering a rebuke to President Trump. The vote included four Republicans who broke with the president, prompting his public criticism.

Trump responded to the vote by calling it "unpatriotic" and singling out the Republicans who voted against him. The lower chamber of Congress passed the measure in what observers described as a largely symbolic vote. The president has faced what multiple sources characterized as a legislative losing streak, with his influence over Congress appearing to weaken.

The four Republicans who opposed Trump on the Iran vote now face what reports described as his wrath. Trump publicly criticized these lawmakers, referring to them as "bad Republicans" for their decision to support ending the war in Iran. The vote represents a notable break within the Republican party on foreign policy.

The measure comes as Israel and Lebanon have reached an agreement that could help end the war in Iran, though implementation of the ceasefire has stalled. The U.N. peacekeeping mission for Lebanon reported that one peacekeeper was killed and others were wounded when they came under mortar fire in southeastern Lebanon.

Senate Republicans are separately moving forward on a bill to fund immigration enforcement through the end of Trump's term. The Senate dropped Trump ballroom funding from the immigration bill and is set to begin debate on ICE funding. The chamber is preparing for what lawmakers called a vote-a-rama on the budget bill.
