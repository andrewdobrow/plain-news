Antonelli wins Monaco Grand Prix after chaotic finish

Kimi Antonelli secured his fifth consecutive victory at the Monaco Grand Prix, claiming the win from pole position in a dramatic race that featured multiple restarts and track damage issues.

The 19-year-old Mercedes driver dominated the streets of Monte Carlo from start to finish, though he faced intense pressure during the closing stages. A red flag was deployed late in the race after the track surface began breaking up at the final corner, forcing officials to halt racing and restart the event.

Antonelli held his nerve through two restarts under challenging circumstances, maintaining his composure as he faced pressure from Lewis Hamilton and others around him. The German team's other driver, George Russell, had a more difficult day, starting from sixth on the grid after struggling with grip during qualifying.

The qualifying session on Saturday proved to be the decisive part of the weekend, as overtaking remains extremely difficult on Monaco's narrow streets. Antonelli had secured pole position with a brilliant final lap that just edged out Max Verstappen by 0.043 seconds. Hamilton qualified third in his Ferrari.

Both Verstappen and Hamilton praised Antonelli's performance after qualifying. Verstappen said the teenager showed the special qualities that mark out future champions. "When you get to Formula One, sometimes it's not only talent, things have to come together, you need to become a more all-round driver and then, of course, you need to have the package to be able to do it. This year he's showing that once he has a package that can do it, he's delivering as a driver," the Red Bull driver said.

Antonelli's qualifying pace demonstrated Mercedes' strength this season. Despite concerns that the team would struggle on Monaco's twisting layout, they found significant improvements during final practice and delivered a commanding qualifying performance.

The Italian's victory extends his remarkable winning streak and increases his championship lead over George Russell by 43 points. At just 19 years old, Antonelli has become the youngest winner of the Monaco Grand Prix in his only second race at the circuit. His achievement underscores his status as the dominant force in Formula 1 this season.

The chaotic nature of Sunday's race, with its safety car periods and red flag interruption, tested Antonelli's mental strength and racecraft. His ability to manage the pressure during the restarts and maintain his position at the front highlighted the qualities that have made him the championship favourite.

Hamilton finished second, while Isack Hadjar claimed the final podium position for Red Bull. Russell's struggles from sixth place mean he faces a significant challenge to close the championship gap in the remaining races.
