Apple unveils Siri AI overhaul at WWDC 2026

Apple introduced a redesigned Siri at its annual Worldwide Developers Conference, transforming the voice assistant into what the company describes as an AI companion with expanded capabilities. The new system, called Siri AI, represents a shift from simple voice commands to a more comprehensive artificial intelligence platform designed to handle a broader range of tasks.

The announcement came at the start of a weeklong event at Apple Park that includes developer sessions and product demonstrations. WWDC 2026 marks a significant moment for the company as it is CEO Tim Cook's final conference with Apple. The event showcases iOS 27, updates to Apple Intelligence, and other software improvements across the company's product line.

Apple also announced a rebuilt search function addressing longstanding user complaints about finding emails, photos, and other content. The company says the new search system will deliver more accurate results across its platforms. Additionally, Apple is adjusting its Liquid Glass design interface, which received mixed reactions when introduced last year. Some users appreciated the transparent aesthetic, while others found the interface difficult to read.

The iOS 27 update includes speed improvements and refinements to the Liquid Glass design to improve usability. Apple's beta testing website temporarily went offline just before the conference began, suggesting high demand for the new software releases. The company has not specified when the Siri AI features will become available to the general public beyond the developer preview.

The moves come as Apple faces pressure to compete in the rapidly evolving artificial intelligence sector. Major technology companies have been racing to integrate advanced AI capabilities into their products, and Apple's approach focuses on embedding intelligence throughout its existing ecosystem rather than launching standalone AI products. The Siri overhaul represents the company's most significant update to its voice assistant since its introduction over a decade ago.
