Apple introduces third-generation AI models with new Siri features

Apple announced a major overhaul of Siri at its annual developer conference, integrating the voice assistant with Apple Intelligence and rebranding it as "Siri AI." The updated tool will become widely available in the fall and will function more like modern AI chatbots such as ChatGPT or Google Gemini rather than a traditional search-based question-and-answer system.

The revamped Siri represents a significant shift in Apple's artificial intelligence strategy. Mike Rockwell, Apple's vice-president of Siri engineering, explained that the company rebuilt the assistant with generative AI at its core. The new Siri will have its own dedicated app and offer more conversational capabilities while integrating with Apple's ecosystem of native applications on both mobile devices and laptops.

During a product demonstration, Rockwell showed how Siri could identify a sunset beach photograph, retrieve a friend's address from stored contacts, and provide directions to that location with a stop at the friend's house all within a single interface. The updated assistant will also be able to proofread documents, help with shopping, add calendar events, and extract information from photos, such as nutrition details from food images. Initially, the tool will only support English.

Siri's rejuvenation comes powered by Google's Gemini AI model through a partnership worth approximately one billion dollars between the two technology companies. The collaboration addresses Apple's historical lag in AI development compared to competitors like Google, which has introduced advanced AI features more rapidly in recent years.

Apple executives acknowledged the company's slower approach to the AI boom. Craig Federighi, the company's senior vice-president of software engineering, noted that some competitors appear focused on advancing AI without clear consideration for how the technology serves users. Apple has repeatedly postponed Siri updates initially announced in 2024, and the company previously agreed to pay $250 million to settle a class-action lawsuit alleging it had falsely advertised Siri's capabilities.

Beyond Siri improvements, Apple unveiled additional AI features across its products. The company integrated AI tools into Safari, messaging applications, and its Home app. New camera and photo tools powered by AI will allow users to create, alter, and enhance images.

The conference also marked a significant leadership transition. Tim Cook, who has led Apple for 15 years and transformed it into one of the world's most profitable publicly traded companies, announced he will step down as CEO in the fall. During his opening remarks to the developer audience, Cook joked about the concentration of iPhones in the room.

Apple simultaneously announced child safety enhancements designed to give parents greater control over their children's device access. New features limit content visibility, restrict communications, and allow parents to manage app usage through time allowances and permission requirements for website browsing.
