Apple unveils redesigned Siri AI and iOS 27 at WWDC 2026

Apple introduced a revamped voice assistant and operating system updates at its annual Worldwide Developers Conference on Monday morning at Apple Park. The event, which marks CEO Tim Cook's final WWDC with the company, featured announcements spanning Siri AI, iOS 27, and expanded Apple Intelligence features.

The company debuted Siri AI, a more conversational version of its voice assistant that incorporates a two-tiered AI model powered by Google technology. The new assistant includes features designed to make interactions feel more natural and contextual. Apple also announced AI-powered additions to several core apps, including Safari, Shortcuts, and its password management tools.

The updated Shortcuts app now allows users to describe desired workflows in text prompts rather than manually building automation sequences. Apple's Image Playground, the company's AI image generator, received a visual overhaul intended to improve its competitiveness with other generative AI tools. The Photos app will gain new AI editing capabilities, including a spatial feature called Reframe that lets users adjust image perspectives using artificial intelligence.

Apple announced that watchOS 27 will drop support for several older devices, including the Apple Watch Series 9, Ultra 1, and SE 2. The decision renders multiple generations of Apple Watches unable to run the latest operating system. The new watchOS includes Siri upgrades and health features focused on perimenopause tracking.

The weeklong event will continue with developer sessions and product demonstrations. The AI features announced are scheduled to roll out to users this fall alongside the new operating system releases. The conference comes as Apple works to integrate artificial intelligence capabilities across its product line while maintaining its focus on user privacy and on-device processing.
