Apple unveils third-generation foundation models for Siri at WWDC 2026

Apple announced significant updates to Siri and iOS 27 at its annual Worldwide Developers Conference, emphasizing artificial intelligence capabilities powered by third-generation foundation models. The company presented what it characterized as a substantially improved experience with its assistant, which has faced criticism for lagging behind competing products in recent years.

The updated Siri represents Apple's competitive response in the rapidly evolving AI assistant market. The company centered its presentation on demonstrating practical improvements to the assistant's functionality, directly addressing longstanding complaints that Siri has fallen behind alternatives like Google Assistant and Amazon Alexa. The third-generation foundation models signal a technical advancement from Apple's previous AI infrastructure.

Apple's collaboration with Google and Nvidia marks a notable shift in strategy for a company that traditionally develops its technology internally. The partnership aims to combine Apple's user interface expertise with Google's AI research capabilities and Nvidia's computing infrastructure. This approach indicates Apple recognizes the substantial investment required to compete effectively in modern AI development.

The updated Siri will integrate with Apple Intelligence, the company's artificial intelligence tool, and has been renamed "Siri AI." The new assistant will function more similarly to AI chatbots like ChatGPT or Google Gemini than to a traditional question-and-answer tool that draws from web searches. The voice assistant will be widely released in the fall.

iOS 27 will incorporate these AI improvements across the platform, with Apple emphasizing privacy protections alongside enhanced capabilities. The company has historically positioned itself as more privacy-conscious than competitors, and executives stressed this distinction during the presentation.

The announcement also included new child safety features for iPhones and iPads, coming amid broader scrutiny over technology platforms and online safety concerns. These features will roll out to compatible devices when iOS 27 launches later this year.

The announcement comes as technology companies race to integrate AI capabilities into consumer products. Apple's presentation follows recent releases from competitors including Google's Gemini updates and other AI developments. Industry observers noted that Apple's timeline positions it as a significant player in deploying comparable AI features across its ecosystem.

Tim Cook highlighted the AI-forward upgrade to the voice assistant at what was noted as his final WWDC keynote address. After years of user frustration and repeated development cycles, Apple presented this major upgrade to Siri as addressing longstanding demands for improvement. The company emphasized that this represents a fundamental shift in how the assistant operates and delivers value to users.
