K-pop Group BIGBANG Announces 31-Show World Tour for 20th Anniversary

South Korean group BIGBANG announced a 31-show world stadium tour bringing together members G-Dragon, Taeyang, and Daesung for their 20th anniversary. The tour will span multiple continents including Asia, North America, Europe, and Australia.

The announcement marks a significant reunion for the group, which has maintained a devoted global following since its formation. BIGBANG rose to prominence in the mid-2000s and became one of the most influential acts in the Korean Wave, helping establish K-pop as a mainstream global genre.

The stadium tour format reflects the group's continued draw as a live act. Playing venues of that scale across four continents demonstrates both the enduring appeal of the group's catalog and the maturation of K-pop as a touring market. The 20th anniversary milestone positions the tour as a career retrospective for a group that helped define the sound and visual style of modern K-pop.

G-Dragon, Taeyang, and Daesung have each pursued solo projects during periods when BIGBANG was not actively promoting as a group. The trio's decision to reunite for an extensive world tour suggests a commitment to marking the anniversary with a substantial campaign rather than a limited reunion. The tour's global routing indicates strong ticket demand across multiple markets.

The group's influence extends beyond music into fashion, with members frequently appearing at major fashion weeks and collaborating with luxury brands. Their 20th anniversary tour arrives as K-pop continues to expand its global footprint, with multiple acts now routinely selling out arenas and stadiums worldwide. BIGBANG's tour will test whether legacy acts can command the same scale as the genre's current generation of stars.
