BIGBANG Announces 31-Show World Stadium Tour for 20th Anniversary

K-pop group BIGBANG has announced a 31-show world stadium tour celebrating their 20th anniversary. The tour will bring together members G-Dragon, Taeyang, and Daesung across multiple continents including Asia, North America, Europe, and Australia.

The announcement marks a major reunion for the influential Korean group, which has shaped the global K-pop landscape since their debut. The stadium-scale tour represents one of the most extensive schedules undertaken by a K-pop act, spanning four continents over the course of the anniversary celebration.

BIGBANG has been credited with helping establish K-pop as a global phenomenon, pioneering the genre's expansion into Western markets. The group's members have pursued solo careers in recent years, making this reunion tour a notable event for fans who have followed the act over two decades. G-Dragon, known for his work as a rapper and producer, Taeyang, recognized for his vocal performances, and Daesung will perform together across the 31 scheduled dates.

The tour announcement comes as stadium concerts by K-pop acts have become increasingly common in international markets. BIGBANG's decision to book stadium venues rather than arenas reflects both their sustained popularity and the growth of K-pop audiences worldwide. The group's influence on subsequent K-pop acts and the genre's global reach has been widely documented by music industry observers.

Ticketing information and specific dates for each city have not yet been released. The tour is expected to begin in Asia before moving to other continents, following typical routing patterns for major international concert tours. Fans across the announced regions will have opportunities to see the reunited lineup perform together as part of the 20th anniversary celebration.
