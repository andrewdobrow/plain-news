BIGBANG Announces 31-Show World Stadium Tour for 20th Anniversary

Korean pop group BIGBANG announced a 31-show world stadium tour bringing together members G-Dragon, Taeyang, and Daesung for the group's 20th anniversary. The tour will span four continents including Asia, North America, Europe, and Australia.

The announcement marks a significant reunion for the influential K-pop act, which has shaped the global music landscape since its formation. The group's decision to mount a stadium tour rather than smaller venues reflects their sustained popularity and cultural reach two decades into their career.

BIGBANG's upcoming tour comes at a time when K-pop continues to expand its global footprint, with acts performing in increasingly large venues across multiple continents. The 31-date schedule positions the tour as one of the more extensive anniversary celebrations in recent K-pop history, with stadium bookings indicating strong anticipated demand across all planned markets.

The tour will feature the three-member lineup of G-Dragon, Taeyang, and Daesung. Specific cities, dates, and ticket sale information were not included in the announcement. The group originally debuted in 2006 and became known for helping establish K-pop's international presence before the genre's current mainstream success in Western markets.

The anniversary tour represents a homecoming of sorts for fans who have followed the group through various lineup changes and individual projects over the past two decades. Stadium tours require significant logistical coordination and production investment, suggesting the group and its management anticipate strong ticket sales across all four continents on the itinerary.
