BigBang Announces 31-Show Stadium Tour for 20th Anniversary

K-pop group BigBang will reunite for a 31-show stadium world tour celebrating the group's 20th anniversary, marking its first major trek since 2017. The three remaining members G-Dragon, Taeyang, and Daesung announced the tour plans, which will span multiple continents over more than six months.

The tour will launch Aug. 21 at Goyang Stadium in Goyang, South Korea, and continue through Feb. 28, 2027. The run will include dates across Asia, North America, Europe, and Australia, bringing the group to major stadium venues worldwide. The announcement marks a significant reunion for the South Korean act, which has been on an extended hiatus from large-scale touring.

BigBang originally debuted in 2006 and rose to become one of the most successful K-pop acts globally. The group consists of three active members following previous departures from the lineup. G-Dragon, Taeyang, and Daesung have pursued solo projects in recent years while maintaining their connection as a group.

The 2017 tour marked the group's last major world tour before members began fulfilling mandatory South Korean military service requirements and focusing on individual careers. The upcoming anniversary tour represents a full-scale return to the stage for the trio, who have maintained devoted fanbases across multiple continents despite the touring gap.

The 31-date stadium tour will be one of the more extensive K-pop touring schedules announced for 2026 and early 2027. The seven-month run reflects both the group's continued drawing power and the global expansion of K-pop audiences since their last tour. Ticket sales information and specific venue details for cities outside South Korea have not yet been announced.
