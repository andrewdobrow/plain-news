Pentagon adds BYD, Alibaba and Baidu to list of firms aiding Chinese military

The Pentagon added several major Chinese technology companies to its list of firms deemed to be supporting China's military, including electric vehicle maker BYD, e-commerce giant Alibaba, and search engine company Baidu. The designation warns American companies of potential risks associated with doing business with the flagged entities.

The updated list also includes biotech firm WuXi AppTec, expanding the scope of industries covered by the military designation. The Pentagon maintains this list under a law requiring the Department of Defense to identify commercial entities that contribute to China's military capabilities. Companies on the list face potential restrictions on U.S. investment and procurement contracts.

BYD has become one of the world's largest electric vehicle manufacturers, competing directly with American automakers in global markets. Alibaba operates one of China's dominant online retail platforms and cloud computing services, while Baidu leads in search engine technology and artificial intelligence development. WuXi AppTec provides drug development and manufacturing services to pharmaceutical companies worldwide.

The designations arrive as tensions between Washington and Beijing continue over technology transfer and national security concerns. U.S. officials have increasingly scrutinized Chinese technology firms for potential ties to military and intelligence operations. The list does not constitute sanctions but serves as a warning mechanism for American businesses and investors.

Chinese technology companies have faced growing restrictions in the United States over the past several years. Previous Pentagon lists have included telecommunications equipment makers and surveillance technology firms. The expansion to include major consumer-facing companies like BYD and Alibaba represents a broadening of U.S. efforts to limit what officials describe as the flow of technology and resources to China's military establishment.
