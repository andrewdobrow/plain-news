China's Xi Jinping Visits North Korea Amid Shifting Alliance Dynamics

Chinese President Xi Jinping is visiting Pyongyang for talks with North Korean leader Kim Jong Un. The trip comes as North Korea has strengthened its alliance with Russia, altering the traditional dynamic in which China served as the isolated nation's primary patron and diplomatic lifeline.

Xi faces a more emboldened North Korean dictator whose reduced dependence on China has shifted the regional balance. The enhanced Russia-North Korea partnership has provided Kim with additional strategic options and economic support, potentially diminishing Beijing's leverage over its neighbor.
