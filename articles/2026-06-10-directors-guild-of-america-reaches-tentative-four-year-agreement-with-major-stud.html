Directors Guild Reaches Tentative Four-Year Contract with Studios

The Directors Guild of America announced it has reached a tentative four-year deal with studios. The agreement comes as part of ongoing labor negotiations between Hollywood guilds and production companies.

The details of the tentative contract were not immediately disclosed. The agreement still requires ratification by DGA members before taking effect. The four-year term would govern working conditions and compensation for directors through 2030.
