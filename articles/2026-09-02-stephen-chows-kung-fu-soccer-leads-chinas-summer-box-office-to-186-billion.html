Stephen Chow's Kung Fu Soccer Leads China's Summer Box Office to $1.86 Billion

Stephen Chow's Kung Fu Soccer topped China's 2026 summer box office, earning 2.315 billion yuan, equivalent to $344.4 million, according to a report from Maoyan Research Institute. The film led what proved to be a strong season for Chinese cinema, with the overall summer box office reaching 12.498 billion yuan, or $1.86 billion.

The summer 2026 total represented a 4.45% increase compared to the previous year, marking the second consecutive year of growth for the Chinese film market during the crucial summer period. The figures come from Maoyan Entertainment's research division, which tracks box office performance across the country.

Kung Fu Soccer's performance solidified its position as the season's biggest draw, significantly outpacing other releases during the competitive summer window. The film's success contributed substantially to the overall market growth, though specific breakdowns of other top-performing titles were not detailed in the report.

The continued growth trajectory signals resilience in China's theatrical market, one of the world's largest. Summer remains a critical period for the industry, with studios releasing major tentpole films to capitalize on vacation schedules and increased moviegoing. The back-to-back years of growth suggest audience appetite for theatrical experiences remains strong despite competition from streaming platforms.

The Maoyan Research Institute regularly publishes analyses of China's box office performance, providing insights into one of the global film industry's most important markets. The summer season traditionally runs from June through August, encompassing the period when schools are closed and families have more leisure time for entertainment.
