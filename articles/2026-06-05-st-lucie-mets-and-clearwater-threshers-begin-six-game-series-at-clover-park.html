St. Lucie Mets and Clearwater Threshers begin six-game series at Clover Park

The St. Lucie Mets are facing the Clearwater Threshers in a six-game series at Clover Park in Port St. Lucie. The matchup brings Minor League Baseball action to Treasure Coast fans over multiple days of competition.

Clover Park serves as home field for the Mets, the Class A affiliate that draws baseball fans from across St. Lucie County and surrounding areas. The series gives local supporters several opportunities to catch games throughout the week.

The extended series format allows both teams to compete multiple times while giving Treasure Coast baseball fans consistent entertainment at the Port St. Lucie ballpark.
