Bonnie Tyler Out of Coma, Remains in Intensive Care in Portugal

Welsh pop star Bonnie Tyler has regained consciousness after spending time in an induced coma but continues to receive intensive care treatment at a hospital near her home in Faro, Portugal. The 75-year-old performer underwent emergency intestinal surgery in May, after which doctors placed her in a medically induced coma as part of her recovery process.

According to a statement released on her official website, Tyler is no longer in the coma but remains "very unwell" and hospitalized. Medical staff report that her condition is gradually improving, and doctors are confident she will make a full recovery, though the process is expected to take considerable time.

The health crisis has forced significant changes to Tyler's touring schedule. All remaining performances scheduled for the summer through the end of August have been cancelled or postponed to the following year where possible. The statement indicated hope that some shows scheduled for autumn may still proceed as planned. Tyler had been due to perform at Sunshine Festival in Worcester in August and at various European venues, as well as at Cardiff's Utilita Arena on December 17.

The singer's family released the update after expressing concern about "lurid and untrue rumours" circulating regarding her health condition. They requested understanding from fans during this difficult period and expressed gratitude for the worldwide support and well-wishes Tyler has received.

The statement noted that Tyler is aware of and grateful for the outpouring of love from people across the globe.

Tyler, born Gaynor Hopkins, became an international sensation in the 1980s following the release of her signature single "Total Eclipse of the Heart." The song topped UK charts for two weeks and spent four weeks at number one on the US charts in 1983, becoming one of the best-selling singles of all time. Her distinctive, soulful husky voice made her other hits instantly recognizable, including "Holding Out For a Hero," "It's a Heartache," and "If You Were a Woman (And I Was a Man)."

Originally from Neath in south Wales, Tyler was discovered by a talent scout in Swansea, and released her first single in 1976. Her career has spanned decades, during which she continued performing and recording throughout Europe and beyond.

In 2013, Tyler represented the United Kingdom in the Eurovision Song Contest held in Malmö, Sweden, performing the song "Believe in Me," where she finished in 19th place. More recently, she was awarded an MBE in 2023 for her services to music.
