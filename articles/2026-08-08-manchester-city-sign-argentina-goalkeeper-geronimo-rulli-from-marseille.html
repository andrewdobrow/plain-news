Manchester City sign Argentina goalkeeper Geronimo Rulli from Marseille

Manchester City have agreed a deal to sign Argentina goalkeeper Geronimo Rulli from French club Marseille. The transfer brings the 34-year-old international to the Premier League as City strengthen their goalkeeping options.

Rulli has experience at the highest levels of European football, having played for clubs across Spain, France, and previously in England. The goalkeeper was part of Argentina's squad during their recent international campaigns and brings significant experience to City's roster.

The move comes as Manchester City continue to build their squad ahead of the new season. Rulli will compete for playing time in a goalkeeper position that has been crucial to City's recent success in domestic and European competitions. His signing adds depth and international experience to the position.

Marseille, who finish mid-table in Ligue 1 last season, will lose an experienced goalkeeper who provided stability between the posts. For City, the acquisition represents another strategic addition as they prepare for multiple competitions across the campaign.

The transfer fee and contract length were not disclosed by either club. Rulli is expected to join City's training camp and could feature in upcoming preseason fixtures as he integrates with his new teammates.
