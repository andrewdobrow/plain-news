Nelly Korda wins first U.S. Women's Open title with record payout

Nelly Kord claimed her first U.S. Women's Open championship on Sunday with a dramatic finish at Riviera Country Club in California. The American secured victory by one shot over England's Charley Hull and Gaby Lopez, finishing with a steady two-under 69 in the final round.

The decisive moment came on the 18th green when Kord's second putt from just over two feet caught the edge of the cup and circled nearly halfway around before dropping in. That clutch putt sealed her fourth career major championship victory and her second consecutive major title.

Hull came agonizingly close to capturing her first major championship but fell just short of matching Kord's winning score. Lopez also finished one stroke back, unable to overtake the American down the stretch.

Kord's victory comes with a record-breaking financial prize, reflecting the ongoing push to increase prize money across women's professional golf. She claimed the first-place prize of $2.5 million, underscoring the tournament's commitment to matching compensation levels in men's major championships.

The win represents a significant breakthrough moment for Kord, who had pursued the U.S. Women's Open title without success in previous attempts. Her ability to close out the tournament under pressure, particularly with that nerve-wracking final putt, demonstrates her composure in high-stakes situations.

Kord's path to victory included moments of strong play throughout the weekend. She positioned herself competitively through 54 holes and maintained her composure when facing stiff competition from accomplished challengers. Her two-under final round proved sufficient to edge out the field on one of golf's most prestigious courses.

The Riviera Country Club setting provided a stern examination for the field. The storied venue tested players with its demanding layout and challenging finishing holes. Kord navigated these obstacles to secure her breakthrough U.S. Women's Open title.

This championship marks another highlight in what has been a strong season for the American golfer. Her consecutive major victories demonstrate sustained excellence and position her among the sport's elite performers. The U.S. Women's Open success adds considerable momentum to her career trajectory and validates her status as one of the game's best players.

Hull's near-miss continues a pattern of the English golfer finishing close to breakthrough major victories. Despite her strong play throughout the week, she could not quite overtake Kord down the stretch. The result leaves Hull still seeking her first major championship triumph.

Kord's triumph continues the American representation at the top of women's professional golf and highlights the growing competitive depth in the sport's major championships.
