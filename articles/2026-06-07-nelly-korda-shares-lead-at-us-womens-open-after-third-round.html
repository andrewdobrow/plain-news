Nelly Korda wins U.S. Women's Open with final putt at Riviera

Nelly Korda won the U.S. Women's Open at Riviera Country Club, sinking a final putt to claim the major championship. The American golfer finished the tournament with the victory after her closing putt dropped in on the final hole.

Korda moved into a share of the third-round lead with a late birdie spree during Saturday's play. She carried that momentum into the final round, navigating the challenging Riviera layout to secure the win. The course, which has traditionally hosted PGA Tour events, presented a stern test for the field throughout the week.

The victory adds another major championship to Korda's already impressive resume. She has been one of the dominant players on the LPGA Tour in recent years, and this win at one of golf's most prestigious venues further cements her place among the sport's elite. Charley Hull also mounted a strong challenge, storming up the leaderboard during the third round to stay in contention.

Korda credited her preparation and routine for her success during the tournament. The final round at Riviera featured drama down the stretch, with multiple players still in position to claim the title as the leaders approached the closing holes. The narrow fairways and difficult greens at Riviera made scoring challenging throughout the championship.

The U.S. Women's Open is one of the five major championships in women's professional golf and is conducted by the United States Golf Association. This year's edition at Riviera marked a significant moment for the women's game, bringing the championship to one of the sport's most storied courses in Southern California.
