Nelly Korda wins first U.S. Women's Open title

World number one Nelly Kord captured her first U.S. Women's Open championship, holding off England's Charley Hull by a single stroke at Riviera Country Club. The victory represents the fourth major title of Kord's career and marks the fulfillment of a longtime goal for the American golfer.

Kord secured the win with a steady two-under 69 in the final round. The decisive moment came on the 18th green, where her second putt from just over two feet caught the edge of the cup. The ball toured nearly half the circumference of the hole before dropping in, giving Kord the one-shot victory she needed.

The tournament also saw Gaby Lopez finish within one stroke of the champion, making for an exceptionally tight competition at the finish. Kord's ability to maintain composure through the closing holes proved crucial to her success.

This marks Kord's second consecutive major victory, extending her recent dominance in professional women's golf. Her performance throughout the week demonstrated the consistency that has established her as the top-ranked player in the sport.

For Hull, the runner-up finish represents another near miss in pursuit of a first major championship. The English golfer has repeatedly contended in the biggest tournaments on the women's golf calendar but continues to search for her breakthrough victory at the major level.

Kord's triumph at Riviera Country Club continues a strong period for American players in major championships. The victory adds another chapter to her family's notable golfing legacy.

The U.S. Women's Open had remained the one major title missing from Kord's resume before this week. Having already collected three other major championships, winning at Riviera filled the final gap in her major championship collection.

Kord's path to victory showcased the mental strength required to succeed in major championships. Her ability to execute crucial shots down the stretch and handle the pressure of a close final round separated her from the competition.

The win comes as part of what has been a successful period for Kord on the women's golf circuit. Her status as the world's top-ranked player now comes with the validation of major championship success across all the sport's most prestigious events.

For the women's golf world, Kord's dominance shows little sign of slowing. Her combination of consistent play and the ability to deliver in high-pressure moments makes her the player to beat in upcoming tournaments.
