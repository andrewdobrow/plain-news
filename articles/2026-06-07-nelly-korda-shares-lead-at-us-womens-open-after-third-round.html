Nelly Korda wins first US Women's Open title by one stroke

World number one Nelly Kord captured her first US Women's Open championship on Sunday, holding off England's Charley Hull and Gaby Lopez by a single stroke. The victory marks a significant milestone in Kord's career and represents her second consecutive major championship triumph.

Kord secured the title with a steady two-under 69 in the final round, but the winning moment came down to an dramatic final hole. On the 18th green, her second putt from just over two feet caught the edge of the cup and circled nearly halfway around before dropping in. The moment encapsulated the tension of a closely contested tournament where every shot carried immense weight.

This US Women's Open triumph is the fourth major victory of Kord's career. The win also earned her the $2.5 million first prize. Hull, the English competitor, finished as the runner-up and missed out on claiming her first major championship title. Lopez shared second place alongside Hull at one stroke behind the champion.

The US Women's Open stands as one of five major championships in women's golf and is organized by the United States Golf Association. Winning the tournament places Kord among an elite group of players who have captured the title. The championship tests competitors across four rounds on a demanding course setup that separates the world's best players.

Kord entered the tournament as the top-ranked player in the world and delivered under significant pressure. Her consistent play throughout the week, combined with her composure in the crucial final moments, demonstrated why she has become one of golf's premier competitors. The victory continues a remarkable run of form for the American golfer, who has now won back-to-back major championships.

The narrow one-stroke margin meant that every decision and execution in the final rounds proved critical. Kord's ability to maintain her composure while Hull challenged her down the stretch highlighted the mental toughness required to win at this level. The dramatic conclusion on the 18th green, with the putt curling around the cup before dropping, provided a fitting ending to what was clearly a tightly contested championship.

This triumph reinforces Kord's position at the top of women's golf. She has established herself as a consistent performer in major championships and continues to add to her growing legacy in the sport. The victory cements her status as one of the game's most dominant players and suggests she will likely feature prominently in future major championships. For Hull, the narrow defeat serves as a close call in her ongoing pursuit of a maiden major title.
