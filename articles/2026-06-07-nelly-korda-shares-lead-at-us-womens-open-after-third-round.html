Nelly Korda wins US Women's Open for first time

Nelly Kord claimed her first US Women's Open title on Sunday, holding off England's Charley Hull and Mexico's Gaby Lopez by a single stroke. The world number one secured the victory with a dramatic final putt on the 18th green that caught the edge of the cup and dropped in after touring nearly half the circumference of the hole.

Kord's winning round included a steady two-under 69 in the final day of competition. The decisive moment came on the 18th hole, where her second putt from just over two feet took an unlikely path before falling into the cup to seal the championship.

This US Women's Open victory marks Kord's fourth major championship overall and her second consecutive major win. The triumph fulfills a childhood dream for the American golfer, who has become the dominant force in women's professional golf this season.

Hull mounted a strong challenge throughout the final round but ultimately fell just short of capturing her first major title. The English golfer's runner-up finish represents her best result at a major championship to date. Lopez also finished one stroke behind, sharing second place with Hull.

Kord's performance during the tournament demonstrated the consistency and skill that have established her as the world's top-ranked player. Her ability to perform under pressure in crucial moments proved decisive in separating her from a competitive field.

The victory at the US Women's Open adds to Kord's impressive resume and reinforces her position as women's golf's leading player. Her win continues what has been a dominant season at the highest level of professional women's golf, showcasing her exceptional talent across major championships.

Kord claimed the $2.5 million first prize for her victory, further cementing her status as one of the sport's elite competitors. Her success comes after building momentum from her previous major championship win, which carried into this tournament as she fought her way to the top of the leaderboard.

The narrow one-shot margin reflected the competitive nature of the championship, with multiple players positioned to challenge for the title entering the final holes. Kord's composure and execution when it mattered most ultimately separated her from the rest of the field.

This maiden US Women's Open title represents a significant career milestone for Kord, who has established herself as a major championship winner. Her performance throughout the event showcased the mental toughness and technical skill required to compete at golf's highest level and emerge victorious in close competitions.
