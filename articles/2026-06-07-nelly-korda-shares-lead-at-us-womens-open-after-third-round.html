Nelly Korda wins U.S. Women's Open at Riviera for fourth career major

Nelly Kord captured the U.S. Women's Open on Sunday for her second consecutive major victory, securing her fourth career major championship with a dramatic finish at Riviera Country Club. She won by one shot over England's Charley Hull and Gaby Lopez.

Kord's victory came down to a tense final moment on the 18th green. Her second putt from just over two feet caught the edge of the cup, traveled around half the hole's circumference, and dropped in for the winning birdie. The dramatic conclusion capped a steady final round where she shot a two-under 69.

"I'm incredibly proud to win this championship," Kord said after the tournament. The victory represents significant momentum in her career, coming immediately after winning another major in her previous tournament.

The win secures Kord's place among golf's elite major championship performers. With four major titles to her name, she has established herself as one of the dominant forces in women's professional golf. The U.S. Women's Open, one of golf's most prestigious tournaments, rounds out her major championship portfolio.

Riviera Country Club, traditionally known for hosting PGA Tour events, presented a formidable challenge to the field. The course's demanding layout tested all competitors, but Kord's performance under pressure on Sunday demonstrated the mental toughness required to win at the highest level of professional golf.

Hull of England came close to claiming her first major title, finishing just one shot behind Kord. Lopez also mounted a strong challenge throughout the tournament but ultimately fell short of the leader.

The victory also brought significant financial rewards, with Kord claiming the $2.5 million first prize that comes with winning the U.S. Women's Open.

Kord's recent success marks a remarkable period in her career. Back-to-back major victories showcase her consistency in the biggest tournaments and her ability to perform when the stakes are highest. Her fourth major championship places her among an exclusive group of golfers who have won multiple majors at the professional level.

The birdie that sealed her victory at Riviera will be remembered as one of the tournament's most dramatic moments. As her putt curled around the cup before falling, it delivered Kord another major championship and continued what has become one of the most successful stretches in recent women's golf history. Her ability to win under pressure, particularly in the closing moments of major championships, has become a defining characteristic of her game.
