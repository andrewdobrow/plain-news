Nelly Korda wins first U.S. Women's Open title by one shot

World number one Nelly Kord claimed her first U.S. Women's Open championship on Sunday, finishing one stroke ahead of England's Charley Hull at Riviera Country Club. The victory represents her fourth major championship and marks the achievement of a lifelong goal for the American golfer.

Kord secured the win with a steady final round, posting a two-under 69 to hold off both Hull and Gaby Lopez. The dramatic conclusion came on the 18th green, where her second putt from just over two feet caught the edge of the cup and curved around the circumference before dropping in. That final putt proved decisive in capturing the title.

This U.S. Women's Open win is Kord's second consecutive major victory, continuing a strong run of form at golf's biggest events. Her triumph at Riviera demonstrates her ability to perform under pressure when the stakes are highest. The championship adds one of golf's most prestigious titles to her growing collection of accomplishments.

Hull, who has established herself as one of England's leading golfers, came up just short in her pursuit of a first major championship. Despite solid play throughout the tournament, she could not overcome Kord's steady performance down the stretch. The narrow one-shot margin underscores the competitive level of women's professional golf at the major championship stage.

Kord's victory at Riviera Country Club highlights her all-around skills. The course is known for testing every aspect of a golfer's game, and her win there demonstrates her versatility and consistency. The $2.5 million first-prize check represents the financial reward for her triumph.

The win adds momentum to Kord's 2026 season and continues American dominance in women's golf on the global stage. Her status among the sport's elite players is further cemented by this major championship victory. Throughout the tournament, Kord's performance under pressure showcased why she maintains her world number one ranking.

Kord has been one of women's golf's most reliable performers at the highest level of competition. Her ability to close out tournaments and deliver when it matters most remains a defining characteristic of her game. With this U.S. Women's Open title secured, she adds another chapter to an already impressive career resume and fulfills a childhood dream of winning one of professional golf's most coveted trophies.
