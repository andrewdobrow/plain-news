Russian strikes on Kyiv kill at least 30 in largest attack on capital

Russia launched a large-scale assault on Ukraine's capital overnight, deploying the largest number of weapons used in a single attack on the city. The barrage killed at least 30 people and wounded numerous others across multiple neighborhoods.

The overnight strikes involved ballistic missiles, cruise missiles, and drones targeting the capital. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said on social media that the Russian tactics remained consistent: "Strike drones, cruise missiles and a significant amount of ballistics."

He noted that most targets were civilian infrastructure in cities. "Residential buildings, energy, and enterprises have been damaged," Zelenskyy wrote, describing the destruction across Kyiv and other Ukrainian cities.

Ukrainian air defense systems worked to intercept incoming weapons throughout the night. The sheer volume of projectiles in this assault appeared to challenge defensive capabilities in some areas. Loud explosions shook Kyiv for hours, with many residents sheltering in subway stations as emergency crews dug through rubble searching for victims.

The attack extended beyond the capital. Russian drones also struck Odesa and Kharkiv, injuring at least three people. Authorities reported repelling more than 600 drones across the country, described as the largest attack in several days.

The death toll makes this one of the deadliest single attacks on Kyiv since Russia's invasion began. Ukrainian officials characterized the assault as unprecedented in scale for strikes specifically targeting the capital. While the city has faced periodic missile and drone attacks throughout the conflict, none had deployed such a large number of weapons across such a wide area.

The overnight assault came after a prisoner exchange on Friday, in which Russia and Ukraine traded 193 service members. These periodic exchanges represented one of the conditions of US-brokered negotiations between Moscow and Kyiv, though the talks have made no progress on key issues regarding ending the invasion.

Russia has maintained its strategy of long-range strikes on Ukrainian population centers despite international criticism of attacks on civilian areas. Ukraine has continued to seek additional air defense systems from Western allies to protect its cities and critical infrastructure against sustained Russian bombardment.

Meanwhile, Ukraine carried out some of its longest-distance drone strikes deep inside Russian territory. In Russia's Belgorod region near the border, a woman was killed and a man seriously injured in a drone strike, demonstrating the expanding scope of the conflict beyond Ukraine's borders.

The continued Russian attacks on civilian infrastructure and population centers represent an escalation in the aerial campaign targeting Ukraine's capital and other major cities as the war continues.
