Russian Strikes Kill at Least 18 in Kyiv After Ukraine Hits Deep Into Russia

Russian forces launched a massive overnight attack on Kyiv using drones and missiles, killing at least 18 people and injuring 86 others, according to local authorities. The death toll may still rise as emergency crews continue searching through rubble.

The assault struck residential buildings across several districts of Ukraine's capital, with fires breaking out at multiple locations. A hotel on one of Kyiv's central boulevards was also hit. The intensity of the bombardment forced many residents to shelter in subway stations as loud explosions shook the city for hours.

Kyiv's mayor declared a day of mourning following what officials described as one of the most massive Russian assaults on the capital in recent months. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky had warned days earlier that Moscow was preparing a large-scale strike in response to Ukrainian operations hitting deeper into Russian territory.

The attack reflects an intensifying pattern in the conflict. Ukraine has carried out some of its longest-distance drone strikes deep inside Russia, targeting the country's oil sector and other strategic infrastructure. These operations have damaged Russian refineries and military fuel supplies. In response, Russia has maintained its strategy of launching devastating attacks on Ukrainian population centers rather than altering its military approach.

According to Zelensky, Russia used drones, cruise missiles, and a significant amount of ballistic weapons in the latest assault. "Most of the targets are ordinary infrastructure in cities," he stated on social media. "Residential buildings, energy, and enterprises have been damaged."

The overnight bombardment also extended beyond Kyiv. Ukrainian authorities reported that Odesa and Kharkiv were targeted by drones, with at least three people injured in those cities. Authorities said they repelled most of more than 600 Russian drones launched during what appears to have been the largest attack in several days.

In Russia, a woman was killed and a man seriously injured when Ukraine struck the border region of Belgorod with drone strikes.

The escalation comes as Russia and Ukraine conducted a prisoner exchange on Friday, in which both sides exchanged 193 service members. These periodic exchanges were among the conditions outlined in US-brokered negotiations between Moscow and Kyiv, though those talks have delivered no progress on key issues needed to end Russia's invasion.

The exchange of increasingly intense strikes represents a new phase in the conflict, with Ukraine demonstrating expanded offensive capabilities while Russia continues to target major Ukrainian cities and civilian infrastructure. The broader regional security concerns generated by the intensified fighting prompted Poland to scramble jets and Finland to restrict its airspace.
