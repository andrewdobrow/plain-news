Ben Stokes withdrawn from England squad after London nightclub incident

Ben Stokes has been removed from England's squad for the second Test against New Zealand at The Oval following an incident at a London nightclub. Joe Root will serve as interim captain in his place.

The England and Wales Cricket Board launched an investigation into a breach of team protocol after Stokes and Gus Atkinson were present at the Rex Rooms on Kings Road in the early hours of Monday morning. The incident occurred shortly after England won the first Test at Lord's and involved players from Saracens rugby club at their end of season party.

The removal of Stokes as captain comes at a particularly sensitive time for England. The team has faced multiple disciplinary issues in recent months. Harry Brook, the white-ball captain and Test vice-captain, was involved in an altercation outside a nightclub in Wellington last November and later misled the media about the incident, resulting in a reprimand from the Cricket Regulator. Additionally, videos of inebriated England players circulated on social media during a break between Ashes Tests in Australia, prompting management to introduce a midnight curfew.

Brendon McCullum, England's men's coach, had previously emphasised the importance of discipline and responsible conduct. "Playing professional sport is a great career and it's great fun, but ultimately you're representing your country and you've got some responsibilities and obligations to carry yourself in the right way," he stated.

Both Stokes and Atkinson have been referred to the Cricket Regulator, an independent disciplinary body with authority to impose suspensions and unlimited fines. A decision on whether they will participate in the second Test at The Oval starting June 17 was expected imminently.

Stokes is understood to be considering his position as captain. The Guardian reported that he spent time in meetings with his agent and advisers discussing whether to stand down from the role permanently or potentially end his international career. Former England captain Michael Vaughan has argued that breaking curfew should not result in losing the captaincy, describing it as unsuitable grounds for such a drastic measure.

Atkinson, though less high-profile than Stokes, remains an important squad member. The 28-year-old made his Test debut in July 2024 and has taken 76 wickets in 17 matches, making him England's leading wicket-taker in that period.

Root brings substantial captaincy experience to the role, having previously led England in 64 Test matches. His appointment provides continuity during this unexpected disruption to England's preparations for the series against New Zealand. The ECB has indicated that further updates will be provided when possible.
