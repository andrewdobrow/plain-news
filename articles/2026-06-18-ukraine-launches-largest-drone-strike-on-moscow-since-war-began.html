Ukraine Launches Largest Drone Strike on Moscow Since War Began

Ukrainian forces struck Moscow with nearly 200 drones in what officials confirmed as the largest aerial assault on the Russian capital since the start of the full-scale invasion. The operation targeted a major oil refinery and other locations in the southeastern areas of the city, forcing the temporary closure of Russia's biggest airport and setting multiple sites ablaze.

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy characterized the attack as retaliation for Russia's earlier strike on a historic monastery complex in Kyiv. The operation demonstrated Ukraine's continued capacity to conduct large-scale coordinated strikes against Russian infrastructure deep within the country, despite the presence of air defense networks around the capital.

The assault struck a refinery and a shopping center, both catching fire from the drone impacts. Moscow's airports halted operations for several hours as authorities managed the security threat and worked to clear the airspace. Flight operations eventually resumed after the immediate danger passed.

Russian air defense systems engaged the incoming drones throughout the operation, though officials have not disclosed how many were successfully intercepted compared to those reaching their targets. Similarly, Russian authorities have provided limited information about the full extent of damage to the refinery or other struck facilities, or any casualties resulting from the attack.

The strike reflected an ongoing pattern of Ukrainian targeting of Russian energy infrastructure and military assets located far from active front lines. These operations have intensified in recent months as Ukraine seeks to disrupt Russia's economy and military capabilities. The scale and coordination of this particular assault underscored Ukraine's ability to maintain offensive operations despite Russian defensive measures.

The attack occurred amid broader developments in the conflict. Peace negotiations between Ukraine and Russia have accelerated in recent weeks, with Ukrainian officials suggesting a deal was substantially advanced. Zelenskyy stated that a potential agreement was nearing completion, though significant disagreements remained on critical issues including territorial control and security guarantees.

This drone operation also came after Moscow made claims that Ukraine had attempted to strike one of Putin's official residences in northwestern Russia days earlier. Ukrainian officials denied these accusations, characterizing them as false claims intended to undermine ongoing peace discussions. The CIA reportedly determined that no such attack had taken place, contradicting Russian assertions.

The strike also highlighted the broader military picture in the region. Ukrainian officials noted increased Russian drone activity along Belarus's border, with intelligence drones from Russian-controlled airspace entering Ukraine from Belarusian territory. In response, Ukraine has strengthened defensive fortifications along its northern border, adding obstacles and reinforcing positions against potential new threats.

The Moscow drone strike represented a significant demonstration of Ukrainian military capability and resolve, occurring against the backdrop of diplomatic efforts aimed at ending the nearly four-year conflict.
