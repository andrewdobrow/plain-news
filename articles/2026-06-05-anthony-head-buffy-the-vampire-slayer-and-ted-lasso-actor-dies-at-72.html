Anthony Head, British Actor Known for Buffy and Ted Lasso, Dies at 72

Anthony Head, the British actor best known for playing Rupert Giles in Buffy the Vampire Slayer, has died at age 72. He passed away peacefully from complications related to pneumonia, surrounded by his family, according to a statement from his daughters Emily and Daisy Head.

"It has been, and forever will be, an honour and a privilege to be his daughters, and to have witnessed first-hand the impact both he and his work have had on so many," they said.

Head became widely recognized for his role as the school librarian and mentor figure to Sarah Michelle Gellar's vampire slayer in Buffy the Vampire Slayer, which aired from 1997 to 2003. The character of Giles served as a guide throughout the series' seven-season run and became a fan favorite. His portrayal established him as a beloved figure in television history.

Born in Camden, London, Head came from an artistic family. His father was a documentary filmmaker who founded Verity Films, while his mother was an actor. Head's early life immersed him in performance, with his mother handmade his dressing-up outfits as Christmas presents.

Beyond Buffy, Head maintained a prolific career across British and American entertainment. He began his professional career on stage, appearing in the 1978 West End revival of Godspell. He later performed in productions including the original West End staging of the Abba musical Chess in 1988 and The Rocky Horror Show in 1990. In the early 1980s, he sang backing vocals for the British pop group Red Box and appeared on their 1986 debut album.

Head became widely recognized in the United Kingdom for his role in Nescafé Gold Blend television advertisements, which featured a slow-burn romantic storyline between a British couple. His film roles included appearances in The Iron Lady and The Inbetweeners Movie.

After Buffy ended, Head took a recurring role in the BBC sketch comedy show Little Britain, where he played a British prime minister. More recently, he appeared in the Apple TV+ series Ted Lasso as former football club owner Rupert Mannion, where he worked regularly with co-creator Jason Sudeikis and earned praise for their on-screen chemistry.

During Buffy's production, Head lived apart from his family, spending five years in Los Angeles while his partner Sarah and two young daughters remained in England. He told the Guardian that he would return home every three or four weeks whenever possible.

"We know how dearly he will be missed by friends, colleagues and fans," his daughters said. "We know his legacy will live on in the shows he was a part of and in the audiences that love them."

Sarah Michelle Gellar paid tribute to Head on Instagram, writing that she was lucky to have known him.
