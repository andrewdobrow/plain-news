Martin Scorsese Faces Backlash Over AI Partnership

Legendary filmmaker Martin Scorsese has sparked controversy in the film industry after becoming an adviser and partner with Black Forest Labs, a German-based artificial intelligence company specializing in text-to-image technology. The New York Times first reported Scorsese's involvement with the firm in 2025.

Scorsese has defended his partnership, arguing that using AI-generated storyboards allows him to communicate his creative vision to cast and crew more effectively. He described the technology as "creatively freeing," emphasizing the immediacy it provides in translating his ideas for film production.

However, the announcement has triggered significant pushback from fellow filmmakers and industry professionals who view the partnership as problematic. Many in the creative community see AI technology as a potential threat to artists and traditional creative work.

Boots Riley, a notable filmmaker, was particularly vocal in his criticism. He suggested that Scorsese accepted the adviser role primarily for financial compensation, stating that the director received "a gang of money" for the partnership. Riley also speculated that Scorsese may not genuinely believe in AI's long-term viability, suggesting the director thinks the technology will ultimately fail but accepted the deal regardless.

The backlash reflects broader concerns within the entertainment industry about artificial intelligence replacing human artists and creative professionals. Many workers in film, animation, and visual effects have expressed anxiety that AI tools could displace their jobs and undermine the value of their craft.

Critics have accused Scorsese of abandoning solidarity with artists by endorsing technology that many view as exploitative to the creative community. His high profile and influential position in cinema make his support for AI particularly consequential, as it could encourage other major filmmakers and production companies to adopt similar tools.

Scorsese's partnership differs from some other high-profile controversies involving institutional endorsements. Unlike situations where organizations face criticism for working with countries or entities with problematic human rights records, Scorsese's backlash centers on the ethical implications of the technology itself and its impact on creative professionals.

The director's embrace of AI storyboarding comes amid ongoing industry debates about where and how artificial intelligence should be integrated into filmmaking. Some professionals argue that AI can be a useful tool for pre-visualization and planning, while others contend that it threatens fundamental aspects of creative work and artistic integrity.

As the controversy continues, Scorsese's partnership with Black Forest Labs remains a focal point for discussions about technology's role in cinema and the film industry's responsibility to protect artistic labor.
