Actor James Handy Stabbed to Death in Los Angeles

Veteran actor James Handy, 81, died following a stabbing at his home in the Tarzana neighborhood of Los Angeles. Police discovered Handy unconscious in the front yard of his residence at 9:30am on Wednesday with multiple stab wounds to the chest.

Michael Gledhill, 44, the son of Handy's girlfriend, was arrested at the scene and has been charged on suspicion of murder. Bail was set at $2 million. According to the Los Angeles Police Department, a 911 caller stated: "I am the son of man, I just killed the man of sin." When officers arrived, Gledhill told them he was "the one they were looking for." Police noted that the suspect lives at the residence with his mother, who is Handy's girlfriend.

Handy had a long career in entertainment spanning multiple decades. He was born in New York City and began his acting work in 1977 on the television series Ryan's Hope. He became known for character roles in films including Jumanji and Top Gun: Maverick, which was released in 2022. His television credits included appearances on Law and Order, NCIS: Los Angeles, and Criminal Minds. He also appeared in the films Logan and K-911.

The Los Angeles Police Department is continuing to investigate the circumstances of the incident. Authorities have not disclosed additional details about what preceded the fatal encounter or the nature of the relationship between Handy and the suspect beyond the fact that Gledhill is the son of Handy's girlfriend.

Handy's death has prompted tributes from his colleagues and fans, who have shared memories of his work on social media. The actor was a consistent presence in supporting roles throughout his career, contributing to numerous productions across film and television over his five decades in the industry.

The suspect remains in police custody as the case proceeds through the legal system. Additional charges may be forthcoming as the investigation develops. The case adds to concerns about violent incidents affecting people in the Los Angeles area in recent times.
