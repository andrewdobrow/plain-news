Actor James Handy Stabbed to Death at Home, Girlfriend's Son Arrested

Veteran character actor James Handy, 81, was fatally stabbed at his residence in Tarzana, California on Wednesday morning. Police discovered him in the front yard with multiple stab wounds to the chest at approximately 9:30am. He was pronounced dead at the scene.

Michael Gledhill, 44, the son of Handy's girlfriend who lived at the residence, was arrested and charged with murder. Bail was set at 2 million dollars.

According to police reports, a 911 caller stated: "I am the son of man, I just killed the man of sin." When officers arrived, Gledhill told them he was the person they were looking for. The Los Angeles Police Department confirmed that the suspect resided at the location with his mother, who is Handy's girlfriend.

Handy built a steady career as a character actor across film and television spanning several decades. His filmography includes roles in the 2022 action sequel Top Gun: Maverick, the 1995 adventure film Jumanji, and the 2017 superhero film Logan. He also appeared in K-911, as well as numerous television series including Law and Order, NCIS: Los Angeles, and Criminal Minds.

Born in New York City, Handy began his career in 1977 with the television series Ryan's Hope. Top Gun: Maverick, released in 2022, was his most recent film credit. Over his career, he maintained a recognizable presence in Hollywood through supporting roles in both major studio productions and smaller projects.

His death marks a loss for the entertainment community, where he was known for his consistent work as a reliable character performer across multiple generations of film and television.

The circumstances that led to the fatal encounter remain under investigation by the Los Angeles Police Department. Detectives continue to work on establishing the full details of what occurred at the Tarzana residence. The department has not publicly disclosed a motive for the stabbing.
