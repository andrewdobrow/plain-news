Actor James Handy killed in stabbing at 81

James Handy, a veteran actor known for character roles in Top Gun: Maverick and Jumanji, has died following a stabbing at his Los Angeles home. He was 81 years old.

Police discovered Handy unconscious in the front yard of his Tarzana, California residence at 9:30 a.m. on Wednesday. He had sustained multiple stab wounds to the chest. Michael Gledhill, 44, the son of Handy's girlfriend, was arrested at the scene and charged on suspicion of murder. Bail was set at $2 million.

According to the Los Angeles Police Department, the 911 caller stated: "I am the son of man, I just killed the man of sin." When officers arrived, Gledhill told them he was "the one they were looking for." The police statement noted that the suspect resides at the location with his mother, who is the victim's girlfriend.

Handy built a lengthy career in entertainment that began in 1977 on the television series Ryan's Hope. Over the decades, he appeared in numerous film and television productions, often taking on supporting character roles in major studio projects. Beyond Jumanji and Top Gun: Maverick, his credits included appearances in Logan, K-911, Law and Order, NCIS: Los Angeles, and Criminal Minds. His most recent film work was Top Gun: Maverick in 2022.

Born in New York City, Handy became a familiar presence to audiences through his work across both blockbuster films and smaller productions. Though his name may not be as widely recognized as some of his co-stars, he contributed to many acclaimed projects throughout his four-decade career.

The Los Angeles Police Department continues to investigate the circumstances surrounding Handy's death. Details about what occurred at the actor's home are expected to emerge through court proceedings as the case moves forward in the criminal justice system.

Handy's death marks another loss for the entertainment community during a period that has seen the passing of several notable figures in recent days.
