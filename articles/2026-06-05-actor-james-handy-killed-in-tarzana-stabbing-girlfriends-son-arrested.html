Actor James Handy killed in stabbing at California home

Veteran actor James Handy, 81, was fatally stabbed at his home in Tarzana, California on Wednesday morning. He was discovered in the front yard with multiple stab wounds to the chest and was unconscious when found at 9:30am. He was pronounced dead at the scene.

Michael Gledhill, 44, the son of Handy's girlfriend, has been arrested and charged with murder. Bail was set at $2 million. According to Los Angeles police, Gledhill resided at the location with his mother, who is Handy's girlfriend.

A 911 caller reported the incident with the statement: "I am the son of man, I just killed the man of sin." When officers arrived at the scene, Gledhill told them he was "the one they were looking for," according to the police statement.

Handy had built a lengthy career in film and television spanning nearly five decades. He was born in New York City and began his acting career in 1977 on the television series Ryan's Hope. Over the years, he appeared in numerous productions across different genres and formats.

His film credits include character roles in Jumanji, Top Gun: Maverick, and Logan. He also appeared in K-911 and had television roles in shows including Law and Order, NCIS: Los Angeles, and Criminal Minds. His most recent film appearance was in Top Gun: Maverick in 2022.

Handy was known for taking supporting roles that allowed him to showcase his range as a character actor. These parts spanned major studio productions as well as smaller projects throughout his career.

The Los Angeles Police Department responded to the scene and conducted an investigation into the circumstances of the stabbing. The killing marks another violent incident in the Los Angeles area involving a member of the entertainment community.

Handy's death concludes a career marked by consistent work in Hollywood over many decades. His steady presence in the industry was built through character work across film and television, contributing to productions that reached audiences both large and small.
