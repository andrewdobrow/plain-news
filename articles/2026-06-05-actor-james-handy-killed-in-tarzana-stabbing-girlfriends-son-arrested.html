Character Actor James Handy Killed in Stabbing at Los Angeles Home

James Handy, an 81-year-old veteran character actor known for roles in "Top Gun: Maverick" and "Jumanji," died after being stabbed at his home in Tarzana, California. Michael Gledhill, 44, the son of Handy's girlfriend, has been arrested and charged with murder in connection with the killing.

Police discovered Handy unconscious in the front yard of his residence at 9:30 a.m. on Wednesday. He had suffered multiple stab wounds to the chest. He was transported to the hospital but was later pronounced dead.

Gledhill, who lived at the location with his mother, Handy's girlfriend, was taken into custody at the scene. According to the Los Angeles police department, a 911 caller stated: "I am the son of man, I just killed the man of sin." When officers arrived, Gledhill told them he was "the one they were looking for." He has been charged on suspicion of murder, with bail set at $2 million.

Handy built a lengthy career as a character actor spanning several decades. He began his work in television in 1977 on the series "Ryan's Hope" and went on to accumulate roles across numerous productions. Beyond "Top Gun: Maverick" and "Jumanji," his credits included appearances in "Logan," "K-911," "Law and Order," "NCIS: Los Angeles," and "Criminal Minds." His most recent film work was in "Top Gun: Maverick" in 2022.

Born in New York City, Handy represented the working character actor tradition in Hollywood. These performers typically appear in supporting roles throughout their careers, building substantial bodies of work through consistent presence across multiple productions and genres. Though often recognized by audiences, character actors like Handy frequently remain less familiar by name than their more prominent counterparts.

The circumstances that led to the confrontation between Handy and Gledhill have not been disclosed by authorities. The investigation into the incident remains ongoing as the case proceeds through the criminal justice system.

Handy's death marks the loss of an actor who dedicated his professional life to contributing to the entertainment industry. His decades-long career demonstrated the commitment many character actors maintain to their craft, appearing regularly in film and television productions while building a recognizable presence across the industry. The case against Gledhill continues to move forward in Los Angeles courts.
