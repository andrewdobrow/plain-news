Actor James Handy Killed in Tarzana Stabbing, Girlfriend's Son Arrested

James Handy, an 81-year-old character actor with a decades-long career in television and film, was fatally stabbed at his home in the Tarzana neighborhood of Los Angeles on Wednesday, June 3. The Los Angeles Police Department arrested his girlfriend's son in connection with the killing.

According to law enforcement, the suspect called 911 to confess to the crime. Police responded to Handy's residence and took the suspect into custody without incident. Authorities have not released the suspect's identity or disclosed a motive for the attack.

Handy was perhaps best known for his role as the bartender in the 2022 film Top Gun: Maverick. He also appeared in Jumanji and maintained a steady presence in Hollywood through numerous guest appearances on television shows throughout his career. His work spanned multiple generations of film and television, showcasing his versatility as a character actor across different genres and formats.

The stabbing occurred at the home Handy shared with his girlfriend. Detectives continue to investigate the circumstances surrounding the fatal encounter, gathering evidence and interviewing witnesses related to the case. The investigation remains active as authorities work to understand what led to the violence.

Handy's death has drawn attention from colleagues and fans across the entertainment industry, who have paid tribute to the veteran performer. His extensive resume demonstrated his commitment to his craft and his ability to contribute meaningfully to productions throughout his long career in entertainment.
