Actor James Handy stabbed to death in Los Angeles

James Handy, an 81-year-old veteran character actor, was fatally stabbed at his home in Tarzana, California. Police discovered him in the front yard with multiple stab wounds to the chest at around 9:30 a.m. on Wednesday. He was unconscious when officers arrived at the scene.

Michael Gledhill, 44, the son of Handy's girlfriend, was arrested and charged on suspicion of murder. Bail was set at $2 million. According to the Los Angeles Police Department, Gledhill called 911 and told the dispatcher, "I am the son of man, I just killed the man of sin." When officers arrived, Gledhill told them he was "the one they were looking for."

The police statement noted that Gledhill resides at the location with his mother, who is Handy's girlfriend. No other suspects are being sought in connection with the killing.

Handy built a lengthy career in film and television spanning multiple decades. Born in New York City, he began his acting work in 1977 on the television series Ryan's Hope. Over his career, he appeared in numerous productions across both mediums, often taking on character roles and supporting parts in ensemble casts.

His filmography includes appearances in the 1995 adventure film Jumanji and the 2022 action sequel Top Gun: Maverick, which introduced him to audiences of a new generation. He also appeared in Logan and K-911. His television credits include roles in Law and Order, NCIS: Los Angeles, and Criminal Minds. His most recent film work was Top Gun: Maverick in 2022.

The Los Angeles Police Department is continuing to investigate the incident. The case will be presented to prosecutors for potential charges against Gledhill.

Handy's death marks a violent conclusion to a career that stretched across multiple generations of filmmaking. As a veteran character actor, he had established himself as a reliable presence in supporting roles throughout Hollywood productions. His work in recent major films had demonstrated his continued relevance in the industry even as he entered his ninth decade.

The incident has drawn significant media attention given Handy's appearances in well-known films and television programs. His contributions to entertainment spanned from the late 1970s through 2022, providing a substantial body of work across numerous genres and production types.
