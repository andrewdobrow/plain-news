Bitcoin Falls Below $60,000, Erasing All Post-Election Gains

Bitcoin dropped below $60,000 for the first time since October 2024, erasing all gains made since the November presidential election. The decline marks a significant reversal for the cryptocurrency, which had rallied on expectations of favorable regulatory treatment under the incoming administration.

The selloff represents one of the most significant price corrections in recent months for the digital asset. Bitcoin had climbed substantially following the election results, with investors anticipating policies that would support cryptocurrency markets. The current price level puts Bitcoin back near its 2026 low, wiping out months of gains in a matter of days.

The sharp decline comes amid broader market turbulence affecting cryptocurrency assets across the board. Multiple digital currencies experienced significant losses during the same period, suggesting systemic pressure on the crypto market rather than issues specific to Bitcoin. Trading volumes increased as prices fell, indicating active selling by investors.

The cryptocurrency market has experienced heightened volatility throughout the week, with several major selloffs occurring in rapid succession. Analysts point to various factors that may be contributing to the downturn, though the precise catalysts remain subject to debate. The speed and magnitude of the decline have caught many market participants off guard, particularly given the optimistic sentiment that prevailed following the election.

The drop below $60,000 represents a psychological barrier for many cryptocurrency investors. This price level had previously served as support during earlier trading periods in 2024. The breach of this threshold has raised questions about where the cryptocurrency might find stability and whether further declines could follow. Market observers are now watching to see whether Bitcoin can recover or if the asset will continue to face downward pressure in the coming days.
