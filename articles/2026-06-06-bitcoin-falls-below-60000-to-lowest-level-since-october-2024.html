Bitcoin Falls Below $60,000 to Lowest Level Since October 2024

Bitcoin's price fell below the $60,000 threshold, sinking to levels not seen since October 2024. The decline marks a significant retreat for the cryptocurrency and has wiped out billions in value from bullish crypto positions.

The drop occurred despite former President Trump's recent embrace of cryptocurrency, suggesting that political support has not been sufficient to stabilize prices. The decline represents one of the roughest weeks for the crypto market in recent memory, with the breakdown of Bitcoin's price revealing ongoing volatility in digital asset markets.
