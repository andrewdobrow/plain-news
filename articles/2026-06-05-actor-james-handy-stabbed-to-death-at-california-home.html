Actor James Handy Found Stabbed to Death, Girlfriend's Son Charged with Murder

James Handy, an 81-year-old character actor known for roles in films including Top Gun: Maverick and Jumanji, was found stabbed to death outside his home in Tarzana, California. Michael Gledhill, 44, the son of Handy's girlfriend, has been arrested and charged with murder in connection with the killing.

Police discovered Handy unconscious in the front yard of his residence on Wednesday morning at 9:30 a.m. with multiple stab wounds to the chest. He was transported to a hospital where he was later pronounced dead.

According to the Los Angeles police department statement, a 911 caller stated: "I am the son of man, I just killed the man of sin." When officers arrived at the scene, Gledhill told them he was "the one they were looking for." Police noted that the suspect resides at the location with his mother, who is Handy's girlfriend. Gledhill has been held on bail set at 2 million dollars.

Handy built an extensive career as a character actor spanning decades in the entertainment industry. Born in New York City, his television career began in 1977 with the series Ryan's Hope. Beyond his recent work in Top Gun: Maverick, he appeared in numerous films and television programs including Logan, K-911, Law and Order, NCIS: Los Angeles, and Criminal Minds. Top Gun: Maverick, released in 2022, marked his most recent film appearance.

His long career demonstrated his range and reliability in supporting roles across both film and television productions. Handy became a recognizable fixture in Hollywood productions, contributing to major franchises and acclaimed series throughout his professional life.

The incident has drawn attention across the entertainment industry, with colleagues and fans acknowledging his contributions to film and television. His death represents a sudden end to a career that lasted nearly five decades in the entertainment business.

The case is now proceeding through the Los Angeles County criminal justice system as prosecutors move forward with the murder charge against Gledhill. Police continue their investigation into the circumstances surrounding the fatal stabbing.
