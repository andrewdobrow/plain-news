Los Angeles Rams acquire defensive end Myles Garrett in blockbuster trade

The Los Angeles Rams have acquired star defensive end Myles Garrett from the Cleveland Browns in a major trade deal. The Browns received linebacker Jared Verse, a 2027 first-round pick, a 2028 second-round pick, and a 2029 third-round selection in exchange.

Garrett, 30, is a two-time NFL Defensive Player of the Year and seven-time Pro Bowl selection. Since Cleveland drafted him first overall in 2017, he has recorded 125.5 sacks, 413 pressures, and 149 tackles for loss. Last season, he set a single-season sacks record with 23 performances, earning All-Pro recognition despite playing for a struggling team.

The trade came after Garrett requested a move from the Browns following their disappointing 3-14 season in 2024. Though he signed a four-year, $160 million extension with Cleveland shortly after his trade request, which made him the highest-paid non-quarterback at the time, the team's continued struggles prompted him to seek a fresh start. The Browns finished the 2025 season with a 5-12 record and missed the playoffs for the second consecutive year.

To facilitate the trade, the Browns and Garrett agreed to modify his contract, deferring option payments across the 2026-28 seasons. The change moved a payment of around $10 million from March 28 to the start of the regular season, enabling the deal to progress.

Browns General Manager Andrew Berry acknowledged the difficult decision in a statement. "As discussions intensified we were stuck at a legitimate crossroads: do we hold on to a truly generational player who has become the identity of our team, or do we make the difficult decision that we think is best for the organization over the long run?" Berry said.

The Rams, who lost to the Seattle Seahawks in last season's NFC Championship game, have a history of making bold roster moves. In 2021, they acquired Matthew Stafford from Detroit, and he led the team to a Super Bowl victory in his first season. That championship was won at their home SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, where next February's Super Bowl LXI will also be played.

Verse, the 25-year-old linebacker sent to Cleveland, was a first-round pick by the Rams in 2024 who earned Defensive Rookie of the Year honors. He recorded 12 career sacks and 22 tackles for loss while earning Pro Bowl selection this past season.

The acquisition creates a historic pairing in Los Angeles, as Matthew Stafford won the AP NFL MVP and Defensive Player of the Year awards last season, marking the first time these honors will belong to teammates.
