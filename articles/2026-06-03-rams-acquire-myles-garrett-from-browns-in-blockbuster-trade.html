Cleveland Browns trade Myles Garrett to Los Angeles Rams

The Cleveland Browns traded star edge rusher Myles Garrett to the Los Angeles Rams on Monday in exchange for linebacker Jared Verse and three draft picks. The deal marks a significant shift for both franchises as the Browns move on from their generational defensive talent and the Rams add one of the league's premier pass rushers.

Garrett, 30, is a two-time NFL Defensive Player of the Year and seven-time Pro Bowl selection. Since the Browns selected him first overall in 2017, he has established himself as one of the league's best defensive players. He holds the record for the most sacks (125.5), pressures (413), and tackles for loss (149) among all players since his draft year. Last season, he set a single-season sacks record with 23 despite playing for a struggling team.

The All-Pro defender had requested a trade from the Browns in 2024 after the franchise finished with a 3-14 record. Garrett expressed his desire to play for a team with genuine championship potential. However, a month later, he signed a four-year, $160 million extension that made him the highest-paid non-quarterback in league history at the time, with an average annual salary of $40 million.

The Browns' struggles continued, finishing the 2025 season with a 5-12 record and missing the playoffs for the second consecutive year. Garrett remained the lone Browns player to earn All-Pro recognition. The franchise fired head coach Kevin Stefanski in January and replaced him with former Baltimore Ravens offensive coordinator Todd Monken.

General manager Andrew Berry was able to facilitate the trade after the Browns and Garrett agreed to modify the contract and defer option payments over the 2026-28 seasons in March. The first payment of around $10 million was postponed from March 28 to near the start of the regular season.

"As discussions intensified we were stuck at a legitimate crossroads: do we hold on to a truly generational player who has become the identity of our team, or do we make the difficult decision that we think is best for the organization over the long run?" Berry said after announcing the trade.

The Browns received Verse, a 2027 first-round selection, a 2028 second-round pick, and a 2029 third-round selection. Verse, 25, was a 2024 first-round draft pick by the Rams who earned Defensive Rookie of the Year honors. The standout pass rusher was also selected to the Pro Bowl this past season and has recorded 12 career sacks and 22 tackles for loss.

The Rams, fresh off losing to the Seattle Seahawks in last season's NFC Championship Game, are positioning themselves as championship contenders. The addition of Garrett marks the first time the reigning AP NFL MVP and Defensive Player of the Year, Matthew Stafford, will have a teammate of such caliber on the defensive side.
