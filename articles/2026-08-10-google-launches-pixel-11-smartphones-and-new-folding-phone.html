Google launches Pixel 11 smartphones and new folding phone

Google has unveiled its latest smartphone lineup at the Made by Google event, introducing the Pixel 11, Pixel 11 Pro, and a new Pixel 11 Pro Fold device alongside the Pixel Watch 5 and updates to its Gemini artificial intelligence platform.

The Pixel 11 Pro represents a significant update to Google's flagship smartphone range. The device incorporates new camera technology and processing capabilities designed to make the most of the company's artificial intelligence features. The Pro model includes enhanced computational photography features that build on the strengths of previous Pixel camera systems.

The Pixel 11 Pro Fold continues Google's investment in foldable smartphone technology. The device features a screen that unfolds to provide a larger display area, putting it in direct competition with similar products from Samsung and other manufacturers. The folding phone integrates the same camera and AI features as the standard Pixel 11 models.

Google also announced the Pixel Watch 5 at the event. The smartwatch incorporates health tracking features and integrates with the company's Fitbit platform, which Google acquired in previous years. The watch runs on Google's Wear OS operating system and connects to Android smartphones.

This announcement reflects Google's broader strategy to expand its hardware business beyond its core search and advertising operations. The Pixel line competes directly with Apple's iPhone and Samsung's Galaxy devices in the premium smartphone market. Pricing and availability details for the new devices were presented during the event, with products expected to arrive in the coming weeks.

The timing of the announcement comes as the smartphone market continues to evolve, with foldable technology becoming an increasingly competitive space. Samsung recently launched its Galaxy Z Fold 8, which features a distinctive squat, passport-like design that opens to reveal a tablet-style display. That device starts at £1,699 for the standard model, with the Fold 8 Ultra beginning at £1,899, highlighting the premium positioning of foldable phones in the market.

Google's latest Gemini updates suggest the company is prioritizing artificial intelligence as a key differentiator for its hardware products. The integration of advanced AI capabilities across the Pixel lineup aims to enhance user experience through improved computational photography, intelligent features, and seamless device integration.

The Made by Google event demonstrates the company's commitment to competing in the premium hardware segment. As smartphone innovation increasingly focuses on AI integration and foldable form factors, Google is positioning the Pixel 11 series to appeal to consumers seeking cutting-edge technology and advanced features. The combination of new hardware design, improved cameras, and AI enhancements suggests Google is targeting users looking for alternatives to established competitors in the high-end smartphone market.
