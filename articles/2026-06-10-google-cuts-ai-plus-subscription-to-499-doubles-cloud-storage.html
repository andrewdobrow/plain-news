Google cuts AI Plus subscription to $4.99, doubles cloud storage

Google reduced the price of its AI Plus subscription tier from $9.99 to $4.99 per month while doubling the included cloud storage from 1TB to 2TB. The change makes premium AI features more accessible and positions Google competitively against other AI subscription services.

The AI Plus plan includes access to Google's advanced AI models and tools integrated across its product ecosystem. The price reduction represents a significant move in the growing competition among tech companies offering AI-powered subscription services.

Existing Google One subscribers can benefit from the new pricing structure without losing their current storage allocations. The company combined AI capabilities with cloud storage in a single subscription tier rather than maintaining separate service plans.

The pricing change follows broader competition in the AI subscription market, where companies are testing different price points to attract users. Google's decision to nearly halve the subscription cost while increasing storage benefits suggests the company is prioritizing user growth over short-term revenue.

The updated pricing applies to new subscribers immediately and will roll out to existing subscribers in phases. Google has not announced changes to other subscription tiers or indicated whether additional AI features will be added to the plan in the future.
