Google cuts AI Plus subscription price to $4.99 per month

Google reduced the price of its AI Plus subscription from $9.99 to $4.99 per month, making it one of the most affordable premium AI services on the market. The plan includes access to Gemini Advanced, Google's most capable AI model, along with priority access to new features and extended conversational capabilities.

The price cut comes with an additional benefit for subscribers. Google doubled the cloud storage included with AI Plus from 1 terabyte to 2 terabytes, matching the storage capacity previously available only with higher-tier Google One plans. Existing subscribers will automatically receive the increased storage and reduced price on their next billing cycle.

The move positions Google as a price leader in the competitive AI subscription market. Other major tech companies offer similar AI services at higher price points, with some premium AI subscriptions costing $20 or more per month. Google's pricing strategy appears designed to attract a broader user base and compete more directly with free AI services that have gained widespread adoption.

Google AI Plus was launched as a standalone tier separate from traditional Google One storage plans, though the company has maintained integration between the services. Subscribers to AI Plus receive the AI capabilities alongside the expanded storage, while traditional Google One subscribers can add AI features for an additional fee. The company has positioned the service as a budget option for users who want advanced AI assistance without committing to enterprise-level pricing.

The announcement follows increased competition in the consumer AI market, where companies have been adjusting their pricing models to attract and retain users. Google has been working to expand the capabilities of its Gemini AI across its product lineup, including integration with Google Workspace applications and mobile devices. The price reduction may signal a shift in strategy toward volume-based growth rather than premium pricing for AI services.
