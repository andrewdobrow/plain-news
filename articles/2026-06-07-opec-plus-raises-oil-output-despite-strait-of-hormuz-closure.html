OPEC Plus Raises Oil Output Despite Strait of Hormuz Closure

OPEC Plus announced a plan to increase oil production by 188,000 barrels per day even as efforts to reach a ceasefire in Iran stalled. However, the cartel's move is largely symbolic because the effective shutdown of the Strait of Hormuz, a critical chokepoint for global oil transport, has stranded vast quantities of petroleum and severely limited market access.

Rising oil prices have triggered concerns about demand destruction—a sustained loss of demand caused by elevated prices. Goldman Sachs has warned that global oil demand faces significant headwinds, raising questions about the cartel's production increase amid these economic pressures.
