Knicks defeat Spurs in Game 1 of NBA Finals behind Brunson's performance

The New York Knicks defeated the San Antonio Spurs 105-95 in Game 1 of the NBA Finals on Wednesday night in San Antonio, maintaining an 11-game winning streak that has dominated the postseason. Jalen Brunson led the Knicks to victory with 30 points, including 13 in the fourth quarter as New York pulled away from their opponents.

The game proved competitive throughout, with the Spurs holding a 14-point lead in the third quarter before the Knicks mounted their comeback. The fourth quarter became decisive, as New York went on an 11-0 run to secure the win. The Knicks also benefited from strong performances by OG Anunoby, who scored 12 of his 17 points in the final quarter, and maintained a crucial advantage by committing zero turnovers in the fourth quarter.

"Just sticking together, it wasn't really our night and wasn't really my night most of the night but we kept finding a way, kept chipping away," Brunson said after the game. "Just knowing we have each other's back, there's a lot of things we could have done better, but I think our togetherness was really the biggest difference."

The Spurs struggled down the stretch, committing five turnovers in the fourth quarter while shooting just 28.6 percent. Victor Wembanyama, the Spurs' young superstar and the league's most exciting player, led San Antonio with 26 points, 12 rebounds and three blocks but shot just 6-for-21 from the field. Wembanyama briefly left the court with an injury scare in the third quarter before returning.

"I was bad tonight, it's not more complicated than that," Wembanyama said afterward. He dismissed suggestions that the occasion affected the Spurs' performance, denying that nerves played a role in their loss.

Brunson dealt with injury concerns of his own when he collided with San Antonio's Harrison Barnes at the end of the first quarter, leaving the game with a knee injury. He returned in the second quarter and appeared to hurt his ankle on a layup attempt but remained in the game.

The victory moves the Knicks within three wins of their first NBA championship since 1973, ending a 53-year title drought. The series comes after the Spurs knocked out the defending champion Oklahoma City Thunder in the previous round.

Game 2 is scheduled for Friday night in San Antonio before the best-of-seven series moves to Madison Square Garden on Monday. The Knicks' strong fourth-quarter performance and their ability to overcome significant deficits positions them well as they pursue their first championship in over five decades.
