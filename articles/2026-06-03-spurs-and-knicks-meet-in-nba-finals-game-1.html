Knicks rally from 14 down to beat Spurs 105-95 in Game 1 of NBA Finals

The New York Knicks opened the NBA Finals with a 105-95 victory over the San Antonio Spurs, overcoming a 14-point deficit with a decisive 11-0 run down the stretch. The win gives New York the advantage heading into Game 2 in San Antonio.

Jalen Brunson led the Knicks' comeback effort with 30 points, driving the late surge that shut out the Spurs in the final minutes. The guard's performance proved crucial as New York stole Game 1 on the road despite trailing by double digits through much of the contest.

The Spurs earned their Finals berth after defeating the Oklahoma City Thunder 111-103 in Game 7 of the Western Conference finals. Victor Wembanyama, the 7-foot-4-inch French center, delivered 22 points and seven rebounds in that clinching victory, earning series MVP honors. Julian Champagnie added 20 points for San Antonio, including six three-pointers, while other role players stepped up during their run to the Finals.

Despite the Thunder missing Jalen Williams due to a hamstring injury, Oklahoma City fought hard throughout. Shai Gilgeous-Alexander, the reigning NBA MVP, scored 35 points with nine assists but couldn't prevent his defending champion team from falling short. Thunder coach Mark Daigneault acknowledged the loss but emphasized his team's resilience. "You have to grow from every experience, including the tough ones," he said. "And it's the NBA—there are tough ones."

Wembanyama was emotional after clinching his first Finals appearance. "Realizing that some part of a childhood dream is going to come true," the 22-year-old explained when asked about his tears. "We're still hungry, we want more. This feeling is so powerful, I can't explain it."

The Spurs have history on their side in this matchup. When the Knicks last reached the Finals in 1999, San Antonio defeated them 4-1. Champagnie expressed confidence in the road ahead, saying "when you've got the greatest player in the world things happen."

Brunson suffered a minor injury during Game 1 but returned to the court after a visit to the locker room. His status for the remainder of the series remains unclear beyond his completion of the first game.

The Finals will continue in San Antonio for Game 2 before shifting to New York for Games 3 and 4. The Knicks are seeking their first NBA championship since 1973, while the Spurs aim to add a sixth title to their franchise legacy. Notable attendees at Game 1 included celebrities such as Spike Lee and Ben Stiller supporting the Knicks.
