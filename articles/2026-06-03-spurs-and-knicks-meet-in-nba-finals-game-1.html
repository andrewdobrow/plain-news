Knicks rally from 29 points down to stun Spurs in Game 4, move within one win of title

The New York Knicks completed one of the most remarkable comebacks in NBA Finals history, erasing a 29-point second-half deficit to defeat the San Antonio Spurs in Game 4. The victory pushed the Knicks within one win of securing their first championship in more than 50 years.

OG Anunoby delivered the decisive play with a tip-in that sealed the historic comeback and sent the Madison Square Garden crowd into celebration. The moment instantly entered Knicks lore as one of the franchise's most memorable plays. The improbable rally marked one of the largest comebacks in Finals history and shifted momentum decisively in the series.

The Knicks trailed by 29 points in the second half before mounting their furious charge. The team's defense tightened considerably in the final period while their offense found rhythm after struggling through much of the first half. The comeback required contributions across the roster as New York chipped away at the seemingly insurmountable lead.

The victory puts the Knicks in position to clinch their first NBA title since 1973. The franchise has endured decades of playoff disappointments and near-misses, making this Finals run particularly significant for longtime fans. One more victory would end the 53-year championship drought that has defined the modern era of Knicks basketball.

The Spurs now face elimination after holding what appeared to be a commanding advantage. San Antonio led comfortably for most of the game before the collapse in the final period. The team will need to regroup quickly to extend the series and prevent the Knicks from celebrating a championship on their home court in the next game.
