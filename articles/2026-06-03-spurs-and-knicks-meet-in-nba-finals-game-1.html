Knicks lead Spurs at halftime in Game 2 of NBA Finals

The New York Knicks hold a halftime lead over the San Antonio Spurs in Game 2 of the NBA Finals at the Frost Bank Center in San Antonio. The matchup represents a crucial contest as both teams battle for control of the championship series.

Karl-Anthony Towns has emerged as the standout performer through the first half, scoring 17 points in 18 minutes while also contributing seven rebounds, three assists, a steal and a block. His strong play has helped the Knicks build their advantage and has been instrumental in containing Victor Wembanyama, who has managed just seven points on 2-for-4 shooting through two quarters. Wembanyama does have five rebounds and two blocks.

Mikal Bridges continues his strong playoff performance, shooting 4-for-5 for 11 points. Jalen Brunson, despite some shooting struggles, has 10 points on 3-for-11 shooting to go along with two steals. For San Antonio, De'Aaron Fox leads the scoring with 12 points, while Devin Vassell adds 11 points.

The officiating has drawn attention at times during the game, with several questionable calls affecting the flow of play. One sequence saw Towns called for an offensive foul that drew criticism from observers, as replays appeared to show he was being held before freeing himself.

Wembanyama's performance will be critical for the Spurs moving forward. The 7-foot-4 center needs to deliver on both ends of the floor to help San Antonio stay competitive in this series. His ability to impact the game defensively and provide scoring will be essential as the Spurs look to prevent the Knicks from taking control of the Finals.

Game 3 is scheduled to take place at Madison Square Garden in New York. President Donald Trump has accepted an invitation to attend the game, prompting heightened security measures at the venue. The NBA has implemented a ban on all bags at Madison Square Garden for the event. Knicks center Mitchell Robinson has expressed enthusiasm about the opportunity to play in front of the president.

The series carries significant weight for both franchises. The Knicks are pursuing their first championship in 53 years, while the Spurs aim to add another title to their storied history. The outcome of Game 2 will determine whether San Antonio can tie the series or if New York will take a commanding lead heading into their home games in Manhattan.

The game tips off at 8:30 p.m. ET from the Frost Bank Center in San Antonio as the two teams continue their battle for NBA supremacy.
