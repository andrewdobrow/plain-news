Knicks complete largest comeback in NBA Finals history to take 3-1 lead over Spurs

The New York Knicks mounted an extraordinary rally from a 29-point deficit to defeat the San Antonio Spurs 107-106 in Game 4 of the NBA Finals on Wednesday night. OG Anunoby's tip-in with 1.2 seconds remaining secured the victory and gave New York a commanding 3-1 series lead.

The comeback marks the largest deficit erased in NBA Finals history, surpassing the previous record of 24 points set by the Boston Celtics in 2008. The Knicks now stand one win away from their first championship since 1973.

The Spurs controlled most of the game, jumping out to an enormous lead early. San Antonio shot exceptionally well from three-point range in the first quarter and built a 41-22 advantage after one period. The visitors extended their lead to 76-49 at halftime, the largest halftime lead ever held by a visiting team in an NBA Finals game. By the third quarter, the Spurs had pushed their advantage to 29 points.

The Knicks' turnaround began in the third quarter when San Antonio scored just 14 points on 4-for-20 shooting. New York's defense stifled the Spurs offense while the Knicks chipped away at the deficit, entering the fourth quarter down 15 points.

The decisive fourth-quarter stretch saw the Knicks rattle off a 28-9 run in seven minutes, converting defensive stops into fast-break opportunities. Jalen Brunson delivered a crucial layup with 1:22 remaining to give New York its first lead of the game at 105-104. After the Spurs regained the lead on free throws, Brunson missed a contested three-pointer with seconds left. Anunoby, however, slipped through the defense and tipped in the winning basket.

Anunoby finished with 33 points and made seven of nine three-pointers, both career highs for the playoffs. Brunson added 36 points and seven assists. Jose Alvarado contributed eight points off the bench during the fourth-quarter surge.

For San Antonio, Victor Wembanyama posted 24 points, 13 rebounds and three blocks. Dylan Harper scored 21 points while De'Aaron Fox and Devin Vassell each added 18 points. The Spurs managed just a 3-for-17 shooting performance from three-point range after halftime and were outscored 58-30 in the second half.

The Knicks led for only 53.8 seconds throughout the entire game, with those final 1.2 seconds proving decisive. The victory was celebrated by thousands of fans who poured into Manhattan streets following the final buzzer.

The Spurs must now win three consecutive games to claim the title. Only the 2016 Cleveland Cavaliers have successfully overturned a 3-1 Finals deficit. Game 5 takes place in San Antonio on Saturday night.
