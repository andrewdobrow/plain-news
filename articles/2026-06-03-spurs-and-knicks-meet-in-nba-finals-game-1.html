Knicks rally from 25 points down to beat Spurs, take 3-1 NBA Finals lead

The New York Knicks completed the largest comeback in NBA Finals history, erasing a 29-point deficit to defeat the San Antonio Spurs 107-106 in Game 4 on Wednesday night. OG Anunoby's tip-in with 2.3 seconds remaining proved decisive, giving New York a commanding 3-1 series lead and putting them one win away from their first championship in 53 years.

The Spurs dominated much of the game, building their substantial lead as Victor Wembanyama controlled stretches of play. San Antonio's advantage appeared insurmountable as the Knicks fell further behind, but New York mounted an extraordinary rally in the second half that shocked the Madison Square Garden crowd.

Jalen Brunson led the Knicks' comeback effort, scoring 32 points and hitting crucial shots down the stretch. Anunoby added 28 points during the regular season but delivered the game-winner when it mattered most. Brunson's missed three-pointer on the final possession provided Anunoby with the opportunity for the winning tip-in.

The game featured intense physical play between the teams. Wembanyama was involved in multiple flagrant foul incidents with Knicks players Karl-Anthony Towns and Mitchell Robinson. The French center drew some calls while committing others, though disagreements over the officiating emerged as the contest wore on. The Knicks believed they received unfavorable treatment from referees, particularly in the fourth quarter when San Antonio attempted significantly more free throws.

The atmosphere at Madison Square Garden reflected the magnitude of the occasion. The game marked New York's first NBA Finals contest at home since 1999. Notable attendees included President Donald Trump, who received heavy boos when shown on the jumbotron during the national anthem. New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani also attended, having purchased a standing-room-only ticket for approximately one thousand dollars. Celebrities including Spike Lee, Timothée Chalamet, Ben Stiller, and Larry David sat courtside.

The comeback overshadowed the pre-game drama surrounding the event. A watch party had been cancelled amid a public dispute involving Knicks ownership, and the NYPD imposed restrictions on spontaneous gatherings outside the arena ahead of tipoff.

San Antonio now faces elimination as the series shifts back to their home court. The Spurs must win three consecutive games to capture the championship, a feat no team has accomplished after falling behind 3-1 in the Finals. Wembanyama and his teammates must regroup quickly to extend the series and avoid squandering their home court advantage.

For the Knicks and their fanbase, the historic victory represents a remarkable opportunity to end the franchise's 53-year championship drought with a victory in Game 5.
