Knicks take 2-0 lead in NBA Finals with last-second win over Spurs

The New York Knicks moved within two wins of their first NBA championship since 1970, defeating the San Antonio Spurs 105-104 in Game 2 of the NBA Finals. The victory gives the Knicks a commanding 2-0 series lead as the Finals shift to Madison Square Garden for Game 3.

The game came down to the final moments, with Jalen Brunson sinking the go-ahead free throw with 9.5 seconds remaining after Victor Wembanyama committed a turnover. Wembanyama then had a clean look from the elbow at the buzzer, but the shot caromed off the back rim. This victory made the Knicks only the third team to win the first two games of an NBA Finals on the road, joining the 1993 Chicago Bulls and the 1995 Houston Rockets.

Brunson continued his standout performance in crucial moments, leading the Knicks' offense through another clutch game. The All-NBA guard has been instrumental in the team's success, particularly in close contests. After stealing Game 1 with a furious fourth-quarter comeback, the Knicks demonstrated their ability to win in hostile environments.

Josh Hart made an unusual impact in Game 2, with what was described as a quiet statistical performance by his standards. Despite his lower numbers, Hart's teammates praised his willingness to adapt to whatever the team needed. He matched a Finals record previously held by Larry Bird in the game.

The Knicks are riding exceptional momentum heading into Game 3. The team has won 11 consecutive games, many by substantial margins, and brings a fresh appearance with a week of rest before the Finals began. Their defense has been particularly strong, posting the best defensive net rating of the NBA playoffs, which could prove crucial against the Spurs' young core.

The Spurs face an uphill battle returning to New York. No team has ever overcome a 3-0 deficit in the NBA Finals, making Game 3 critical for San Antonio's championship hopes. Coming off a physically demanding series against the Oklahoma City Thunder, the Spurs may be dealing with fatigue as they attempt their comeback.

Wembanyama, the young star who has drawn attention from various figures, will need to bounce back from his missed final shot. The 7-foot-4 generational talent remains a constant scoring and blocking threat for San Antonio.

Game 3 at Madison Square Garden will take place under heightened security measures. The venue has implemented a ban on all bags for the game as part of enhanced security protocols related to high-profile attendance. The atmosphere is expected to be intense as the Knicks attempt to take control of the series in front of their home fans.
