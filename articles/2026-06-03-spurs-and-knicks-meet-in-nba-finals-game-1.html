Knicks complete largest comeback in NBA Finals history to take 3-1 series lead over Spurs

The New York Knicks mounted an extraordinary comeback from a 29-point deficit to defeat the San Antonio Spurs 107-106 in Game 4, securing a commanding 3-1 series lead and moving within one victory of their first NBA championship since 1973.

OG Anunoby delivered the decisive play with 1.2 seconds remaining, tipping in a missed three-pointer from Jalen Brunson to complete the historic rally in front of a stunned Madison Square Garden crowd. The 29-point deficit represents the largest comeback in NBA Finals history, surpassing the previous record of 24 points set by the Boston Celtics against the Los Angeles Lakers in 2008.

The Spurs dominated the early stages of the game, building a commanding lead behind their hot outside shooting. San Antonio hit 11 of their first 16 three-point attempts and led 41-22 after the opening quarter. By halftime, the Spurs had extended their advantage to 76-49, the largest halftime lead ever held by a visiting team in an NBA Finals game.

The Knicks' turnaround began in the third quarter when they held San Antonio to just 14 points on 4-for-20 shooting. The Spurs failed to score a single basket from the paint during that period, allowing New York to chip away at the deficit. A crucial fourth-quarter run sealed the comeback, with the Knicks outscoring San Antonio 28-9 over a seven-minute stretch.

Brunson broke through with a layup with 1:22 remaining to give New York its first lead of the game at 105-104. After the Spurs briefly retook the lead, Anunoby's tip-in secured the victory. Remarkably, the Knicks led for only 53.8 seconds of the entire game.

Anunoby finished with 33 points and made seven of nine three-pointers, both playoff career highs. Brunson added 36 points and seven assists, while Jose Alvarado contributed eight crucial points off the bench during the fourth-quarter rally.

For the Spurs, Victor Wembanyama recorded 24 points and 13 rebounds. Dylan Harper added 21 points while De'Aaron Fox and Devin Vassell each scored 18. San Antonio shot just 3-for-17 from three-point range after halftime and were outscored 58-30 in the second half.

The stunning victory took place in front of a star-studded Madison Square Garden crowd that included celebrities like Taylor Swift, Spike Lee, Ben Stiller, and Tracy Morgan.

With the series heading to San Antonio for Game 5, the Knicks can clinch their first championship in 53 years with a road victory. The Spurs must win to force the series back to New York. Only the 2016 Cleveland Cavaliers have previously overcome a 3-1 Finals deficit to win the championship.
