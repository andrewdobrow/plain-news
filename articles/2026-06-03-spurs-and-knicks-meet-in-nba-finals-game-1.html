Spurs defeat Knicks 115-111 in Game 3 of NBA Finals at Madison Square Garden

The San Antonio Spurs defeated the New York Knicks 115-111 in Game 3 of the NBA Finals at Madison Square Garden on Monday night, snapping the Knicks' 13-game postseason winning streak and cutting the series deficit to 2-1.

Victor Wembanyama delivered his best performance of the Finals, finishing with 32 points, eight rebounds, six assists and three blocks. Stephon Castle added 23 points, including two crucial free throws with 6.8 seconds remaining in the contest. The Spurs' ability to execute in the closing moments proved decisive in what was described as a nail-biting finish.

The Knicks' Jalen Brunson led his team with 32 points while OG Anunoby contributed 28, but the rest of New York's roster struggled in the fourth quarter during a back-and-forth affair. The loss represented a significant setback for the home team, who had not lost since April 23.

The atmosphere at Madison Square Garden was extraordinary, with the spectacle surrounding the event nearly overshadowing the basketball itself. The game marked New York City's first home NBA Finals game since June 25, 1999. Tickets reportedly sold for as much as $10,000, and heightened security measures were implemented throughout the venue.

The event drew an array of notable attendees. Donald Trump, a longtime Knicks fan invited as a guest of team owner James Dolan, watched from a suite. When he appeared on the jumbotron during the national anthem, the crowd responded with loud boos and jeers. The president maintained a salute throughout the brief eight-second appearance. New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani attended separately, having purchased a standing-room-only ticket for approximately $1,000.

Celebrity row featured an impressive lineup including Spike Lee, Timothée Chalamet, Ben Stiller, Tina Fey, Tracy Morgan and Larry David.

The Spurs' victory kept their championship hopes alive despite the historical odds against them. No NBA team has lost the first two games of the Finals on its home floor and successfully won the championship, but San Antonio demonstrated they can compete effectively in hostile territory.

The Knicks continue their pursuit of their first championship since 1973. Game 4 is scheduled for Wednesday in New York, where the series will continue with the Knicks holding a series advantage.

The win sparked celebrations in San Antonio, where heavy traffic was reported on highways following the Spurs' triumph. Both teams now prepare for the next contest, with momentum clearly shifting as the Finals continue.
