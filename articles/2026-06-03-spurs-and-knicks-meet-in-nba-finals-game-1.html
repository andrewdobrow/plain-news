Knicks erase 29-point deficit to defeat Spurs 107-106 in Game 4 of NBA Finals

The New York Knicks completed the largest comeback in NBA Finals history, rallying from 29 points down to defeat the San Antonio Spurs 107-106 in Game 4. The victory keeps the series alive and sets a new record for the biggest deficit overcome in a Finals game.

The Spurs appeared to be cruising to a commanding series lead as they built their substantial advantage during the game. Victor Wembanyama drew a flagrant foul early in the contest and was later called for a flagrant himself after hitting Karl-Anthony Towns. The physicality continued when Mitchell Robinson delivered a hard forearm to Wembanyama's face, prompting the Spurs center to tell Robinson he was in his head.

Despite the Spurs' early dominance and Wembanyama's efforts to get into the Knicks' heads during the game, New York mounted an improbable rally. The comeback erased what had been the previous record for the largest Finals comeback, demonstrating the Knicks' determination to avoid falling into a deep series hole.

The one-point margin of victory reflected the intensity of the Knicks' surge and the Spurs' inability to hold their massive lead. The game was highly anticipated, with betting tips and daily fantasy sports analysts expecting stars like Wembanyama and Jalen Brunson to deliver significant performances. The back-and-forth nature of the contest lived up to those expectations, with key players on both sides contributing to the dramatic finish.

The result shifts momentum in the series and forces the Spurs to regroup after squandering a historic lead. For the Knicks, the victory provides crucial breathing room and demonstrates their ability to overcome adversity on the biggest stage in professional basketball.
