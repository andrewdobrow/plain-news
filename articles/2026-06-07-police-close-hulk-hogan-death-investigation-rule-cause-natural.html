Police Close Hulk Hogan Death Investigation, Rule Cause Natural

Clearwater police have released their final investigative report on the death of wrestling legend Hulk Hogan, concluding that he died of natural causes. The police department made public their case files and issued a formal statement closing the investigation that began following Hogan's death in 2025.

Hogan, born Terry Gene Bollea, was one of professional wrestling's most influential figures. He played a central role in bringing wrestling into the mainstream during the 1980s and 1990s, becoming a major star with the World Wrestling Federation, which later became known as WWE. His career extended well beyond the wrestling ring into film, television, and reality programming.

The Clearwater Police Department released interview materials and supporting documentation as part of their official case closure. Investigators found no suspicious circumstances surrounding the death. Instead, authorities determined that medical factors were responsible. Florida law enforcement had been conducting the investigation since Hogan's passing in 2025.

According to the police statement, detectives found no evidence that required further investigation. The department completed all necessary interviews and reviewed all available documentation before closing the case. The determination that Hogan died from natural causes matched the initial reports released when his death was first announced.

Hogan's wrestling career was marked by numerous championship titles and major event appearances that attracted millions of viewers. He became an American cultural icon through his distinctive persona, which included his trademark mustache, bandana, and memorable catchphrases. His influence on professional wrestling and popular culture remained significant throughout his life.

The release of the final police report represents the formal end of the official investigation. Law enforcement stated that the case closure came after a thorough review of all evidence and witness statements. No further inquiries were deemed necessary based on the findings.

Hogan's legacy in wrestling history continues to be significant despite the controversies that marked his later years. His early career established him as one of the most recognizable athletes of his generation. The investigation's conclusion now brings official closure to the circumstances surrounding his death, allowing focus to remain on his considerable impact on professional wrestling and entertainment.
