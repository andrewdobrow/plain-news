Israeli strikes kill 11 in Gaza City, including women and children

Israeli strikes on four residential buildings in Gaza City killed 11 people, according to medics. Women and children were among those killed in the attacks.

Israel's military said the strikes killed four Hamas security officials. The operations targeted residential structures in Gaza City, resulting in civilian casualties alongside the stated military targets.
