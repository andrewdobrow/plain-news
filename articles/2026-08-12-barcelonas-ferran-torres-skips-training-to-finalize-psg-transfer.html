Barcelona's Ferran Torres skips training to finalize PSG transfer

Spain forward Ferran Torres missed Barcelona training Wednesday as he works to complete a transfer to Paris St-Germain. The club granted him permission to be absent while finalizing the move to the French capital.

Torres played a central role in Spain's World Cup triumph, cementing his status as one of the tournament's standout performers. His potential departure marks a significant shift for Barcelona, who signed the forward from Manchester City in January 2022. He has made over 100 appearances for the Catalan club across all competitions.

Paris St-Germain has been active in the transfer market as they rebuild their squad. The French champions are looking to add attacking depth following recent departures and see Torres as a key addition to their forward line. The 26-year-old offers versatility across the front three and brings experience from top European competitions.

Barcelona's willingness to let Torres leave suggests the club is managing its squad and potentially making room for other signings. The Spanish side has faced financial constraints in recent seasons, and player sales have become part of their strategy to balance the books while remaining competitive. Torres' departure would free up wages and potentially provide transfer funds.

The move is expected to be completed in the coming days, with Torres set to undergo a medical and finalize personal terms with PSG. Both clubs are working to process the necessary paperwork before the transfer window closes. Once official, Torres will join a PSG squad competing in both Ligue 1 and the Champions League, reuniting with several international teammates in Paris.
