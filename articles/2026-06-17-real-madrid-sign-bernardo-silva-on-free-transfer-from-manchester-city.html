Real Madrid sign Bernardo Silva on free transfer from Manchester City

Real Madrid completed the signing of Bernardo Silva on a free transfer after the Portuguese midfielder left Manchester City at the end of the season. Silva joins the Spanish giants under new manager Jose Mourinho, reuniting with the coach who previously worked with Portuguese talent during his time at Porto and the national team setup.

The 32-year-old midfielder departed City after a decorated spell that saw him win multiple Premier League titles and contribute to the club's domestic and European success. His contract with City expired at the season's end, allowing him to negotiate a move without a transfer fee. Silva's technical ability and experience make him a significant addition to Mourinho's project at the Bernabeu.

Mourinho recently took charge at Real Madrid, bringing his tactical approach and winning mentality to one of European football's most storied clubs. The Portuguese manager has historically favored players he trusts and understands, making Silva's arrival a natural fit for his system. The midfielder's versatility allows him to operate in multiple positions across the middle of the park, giving Mourinho tactical flexibility.

Silva's move represents a shift in Real Madrid's transfer strategy, capitalizing on the opportunity to acquire an established player without the financial outlay of a transfer fee. The club can instead focus resources on Silva's wages and other squad reinforcements. His arrival adds depth and quality to a Madrid midfield that has undergone significant changes in recent seasons.

The signing positions Real Madrid to compete across multiple competitions next season. Silva brings Champions League pedigree and the ability to perform in high-pressure matches, qualities Mourinho values as he builds a squad capable of challenging for domestic and European honors. The partnership between manager and player will be closely watched as Madrid seeks to return to the top of Spanish and European football.
