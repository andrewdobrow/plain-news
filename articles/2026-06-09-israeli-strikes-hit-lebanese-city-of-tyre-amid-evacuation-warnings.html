Israeli Strikes Hit Lebanese City of Tyre Amid Evacuation Warnings

Israeli air strikes targeted the Lebanese city of Tyre as Iran warned Israel that it could resume hostilities if attacks on Hezbollah continue. Israeli authorities ordered residents to evacuate ahead of the operations in southern Lebanon.

Christian leaders in Tyre called for swift international intervention following the Israeli evacuation warning. The ancient coastal city faced bombardment despite not being a major target in previous Israeli military operations against Hezbollah in the region.
