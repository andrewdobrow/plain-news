LG Display unveils FLiPP OLED technology with improved brightness and lifespan

LG Display announced a new OLED screen technology called FLiPP that increases brightness by up to 1.6 times compared to existing OLED displays while extending their lifespan. The technology addresses one of the longstanding limitations of OLED screens, which have historically struggled with brightness levels and degradation over time.

The FLiPP technology represents a significant advancement in display manufacturing. OLED screens are known for their superior contrast and color reproduction compared to traditional LCD panels, but lower peak brightness and concerns about burn-in have limited their adoption in certain applications. By improving both brightness and longevity, the new technology could expand the use of OLED displays across more device categories.

LG Display indicated the technology could be used in screens up to 100 inches in size, suggesting applications beyond smartphones and tablets. The company has been a leading supplier of OLED panels for televisions, computer monitors, and mobile devices. Competitors are also advancing display technology, with Samsung showcasing a 300Hz OLED display designed for gaming laptops at the same time.

The timing of the announcement coincides with growing demand for high-quality displays across consumer electronics. Brighter screens improve visibility in outdoor settings and well-lit rooms, while longer lifespans reduce the total cost of ownership for consumers and manufacturers. The technology could influence product development cycles for companies that rely on LG Display as a supplier.

LG Display has not provided a timeline for when FLiPP-based screens will appear in commercial products or disclosed which manufacturers have committed to using the technology. The company also has not specified the exact mechanisms behind the brightness and lifespan improvements or whether the technology requires changes to manufacturing processes.
