Golden Knights rally past Hurricanes in Stanley Cup Final opener

The Vegas Golden Knights overcame an early deficit to defeat the Carolina Hurricanes 5-4 in Game 1 of the Stanley Cup Final. Tomas Hertl scored the deciding goal 16 minutes and 36 seconds into the third period, his fourth goal of the postseason, capping a comeback victory for Vegas.

The Hurricanes jumped out to an early lead before the Golden Knights mounted their rally. Hertl's late third-period strike proved decisive in a tightly contested opening game that featured nine combined goals. The victory gives Vegas home-ice advantage heading into Game 2.

The Golden Knights are seeking their second Stanley Cup championship in franchise history. Vegas won its first title in 2023, just six years after entering the league as an expansion team. The Hurricanes last won the Stanley Cup in 2006 and are appearing in their first Finals since 2019.

Hertl has emerged as a key contributor for Vegas during this postseason run. His four goals have come at critical moments, and his Game 1 winner continues that trend. The veteran forward joined the Golden Knights in a trade from San Jose and has become an integral part of their offensive attack.

The series continues with Game 2 in Vegas before shifting to Carolina. The Hurricanes will need to regroup quickly after surrendering a lead in the opener. Both teams showed offensive firepower in a back-and-forth contest that suggests this series could extend deep into the schedule.
