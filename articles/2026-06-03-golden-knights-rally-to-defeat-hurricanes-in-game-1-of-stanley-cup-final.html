Hurricanes even Stanley Cup Final with overtime win over Golden Knights

Seth Jarvis scored on a power play 3:56 into overtime to lift the Carolina Hurricanes past the Vegas Golden Knights 4-3 in Game 2 of the Stanley Cup Final on Thursday night, tying the series at one game each.

The Hurricanes mounted an impressive comeback in the third period after falling behind. Carolina erased a deficit in regulation play, but Vegas responded with a late goal to force the game into overtime. The dramatic period featured four goals being scored and another goal being disallowed due to a goaltender interference call following a failed Vegas coach's challenge.

That crucial challenge proved pivotal to the outcome. The disallowed goal and subsequent penalty gave Carolina the power play opportunity that Jarvis capitalized on to secure the victory. The sequence of events highlighted how a single coaching decision can dramatically shift momentum in playoff hockey.

The game showcased resilience from the Hurricanes, who refused to quit despite facing adversity. After Vegas tied the game late in regulation, Carolina kept pushing and ultimately prevailed in the overtime period. The back-and-forth nature of the contest demonstrated both teams' commitment to winning a championship.

With the series now even at one win apiece, both teams return to competition knowing the Stanley Cup remains within reach. The Hurricanes proved they can come back from deficits and win in high-pressure situations. Vegas, despite the loss, showed they can score goals and compete with Carolina in a tightly contested series.

The dramatic finish in Game 2 set the tone for what promises to be a competitive Finals matchup. Neither team appears to have a clear edge, with both capable of winning on any given night. The series will continue with the next game scheduled soon, with momentum potentially shifting based on how each team responds to this result.

Jarvis's overtime heroics give Carolina a significant confidence boost heading forward. The power play goal demonstrates the team's ability to execute when opportunities present themselves. For Vegas, the loss stings but provides motivation to refocus and improve their performance in upcoming games.

The Stanley Cup Final has delivered the kind of compelling hockey that makes the playoffs so exciting. With two games played and the series even, fans can expect the intensity to continue escalating as the series progresses. Both teams will make adjustments based on what they learned in Game 2, and the outcome will likely be determined by which squad capitalizes on their chances and minimizes mistakes in critical moments.
