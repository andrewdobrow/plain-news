Marner scores Stanley Cup Final hat trick as Golden Knights edge Hurricanes in double overtime

Mitch Marner scored three goals in the second period of Game 3 of the Stanley Cup Final, making NHL history as he helped the Vegas Golden Knights defeat the Carolina Hurricanes in double overtime. The Golden Knights now lead the series 2-1 after the dramatic finish in Las Vegas.

Marner's second-period hat trick marked a historic achievement in Stanley Cup Final play, giving Vegas momentum in a game that required two overtime periods to decide. The Hurricanes mounted a comeback attempt after falling behind, forcing the extra frames before ultimately falling short.

The double overtime thriller capped a game built on unusual turns and momentum swings. Carolina fought back from Marner's three-goal outburst to tie the game and send it to overtime, but Vegas ultimately prevailed in the second extra period. The Hurricanes now face a series deficit heading into Game 4.

The Golden Knights have taken control of the series with their home ice advantage, while Carolina must regroup after watching a potential comeback slip away in the second overtime period. The loss leaves the Hurricanes in a difficult position as the series continues.

Game 4 will determine whether Vegas can extend its series lead or if Carolina can even the matchup. The Hurricanes showed resilience in forcing overtime but could not complete the comeback in what became one of the most dramatic games in this year's Final.
