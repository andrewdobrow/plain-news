Golden Knights defeat Hurricanes in double overtime of Stanley Cup Final Game 3

The Vegas Golden Knights won Game 3 of the Stanley Cup Final in double overtime, defeating the Carolina Hurricanes 5-4 and taking a 2-1 series lead.

Shea Theodore scored the game-winning goal at 5:38 of the second overtime period. The puck deflected off goalie Brandon Bussi's skate before going in, giving Vegas the victory after they had blown a four-goal lead during the contest.

Mitch Marner was a dominant force for the Golden Knights, recording the fastest hat trick in Stanley Cup Final history. His three-goal performance came before Theodore's overtime heroics secured the win.

The Hurricanes mounted a dramatic comeback from a significant deficit but ultimately fell short in the extended play. Their late surge brought them back into contention, yet they could not complete the comeback despite the effort.

The game has been widely described as one of the most memorable Stanley Cup Final contests in recent history. Both teams played at a championship level throughout the marathon matchup that required two additional periods to determine a winner.

With the victory, Vegas now has the advantage in the series and retains home ice for the remaining games. The Golden Knights avoided what could have been a potentially damaging loss after nearly surrendering their substantial lead.

Carolina will need to regroup quickly as they trail in the best-of-seven series. The loss comes after their impressive comeback attempt, which showed resilience but ultimately proved insufficient against Vegas's experience and performance from Marner.

The series continues with both teams having demonstrated the skill and determination required to compete at the highest level of professional hockey. Theodore's game-winning goal will be remembered as the decisive moment in a contest that provided plenty of drama and highlight-reel moments for fans.
