Golden Knights rally to defeat Hurricanes in Game 1 of Stanley Cup Final

The Vegas Golden Knights defeated the Carolina Hurricanes in Game 1 of the 2026 Stanley Cup Final, recovering from an early deficit to claim victory in a high-scoring matchup broadcast on ABC.

Carolina got off to a strong start, with Nikolaj Ehlers leading the Hurricanes' early offensive push that put pressure on Vegas from the opening period. The fast-paced beginning gave the Hurricanes momentum and forced the Golden Knights to play catch-up in a hostile environment.

Vegas responded by rallying back into the contest, ultimately securing the road win. The Golden Knights' comeback demonstrated their ability to stay composed under pressure and execute when it mattered most in a playoff setting. Their performance suggested they have the resilience needed to compete in a championship series.

The game delivered compelling hockey that lived up to expectations for a Stanley Cup Final matchup. Both teams displayed offensive capability throughout, creating a back-and-forth contest that showcased why each squad advanced to the championship round. The scoring chances generated by both sides indicated that Carolina and Vegas possess the attacking talent to make this series competitive.

The opening game set an energetic tone for what promises to be a closely contested championship series. The fast pace and high-scoring nature of the game suggested that fans can expect more of the same quality hockey as the series progresses.

Looking ahead, Game 2 will be played in Carolina, giving the Hurricanes a chance to even the series on home ice. The Golden Knights will attempt to capitalize on their Game 1 victory and build momentum heading into the next contest. Vegas will be looking to establish a 2-0 series lead before returning to their home arena.

Both teams have demonstrated they can generate scoring chances and compete at a high level. Carolina's strong start in Game 1 showed they can compete with the Golden Knights, even though they came up short in the final result. How Carolina responds at home in Game 2 will be critical to their Stanley Cup hopes.
