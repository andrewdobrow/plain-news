Hurricanes even Stanley Cup Final with Game 4 win over Golden Knights

The Carolina Hurricanes defeated the Vegas Golden Knights 5-3 on Tuesday night to tie the Stanley Cup Final at two games apiece. Jordan Staal proved to be the difference maker, scoring twice including the decisive goal while stretched out on his stomach during the third period.

Staal's game-winning goal came at 6:32 of the third period and put the Hurricanes ahead for good in what turned out to be a chaotic contest. The forward's two-goal performance helped Carolina overcome what had been another difficult second period to pull even in the championship series.

The victory shifts momentum back toward the Hurricanes, who will host Game 5 on Thursday night at home. Carolina now has the opportunity to win their first Stanley Cup in two decades, with potentially two remaining games on home ice. The Golden Knights, seeking their second title in four years, failed to extend their series advantage despite entering Game 4 with a 2-1 lead.

The loss represents a missed opportunity for Vegas to take commanding control of the series while playing at home. The Golden Knights were unable to capitalize on their position and will now travel to Carolina facing increased pressure. Both teams have demonstrated resilience throughout the playoffs, setting up what promises to be a competitive conclusion to the championship.

Goaltender Pyotr Kochetkov delivered strong play for the Hurricanes, making key stops during crucial moments. His performance was instrumental in keeping Vegas at bay and securing the victory.

The Hurricanes' ability to recover after struggling in the middle frame proved critical to their success. Despite the challenges they faced early in the game, Carolina regrouped and found their rhythm to claim the win and even the series.

With the Stanley Cup Final now tied, both teams face elimination scenarios in the games ahead. The Hurricanes will look to maintain their momentum and build on their home ice advantage, while the Golden Knights will attempt to reclaim their footing and reassert control of the series.

The remainder of the series will be played in Carolina for now, with the format allowing for potential games back in Vegas if necessary. Both franchises have shown the ability to win games on the road throughout these playoffs, indicating that the location may not be a determining factor in how the championship concludes.

Game 5 takes place Thursday night in Carolina, marking the beginning of what could be the final stretch of the 2026 Stanley Cup Finals.
