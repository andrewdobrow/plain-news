Trump pardons former congressman convicted of insider trading

President Donald Trump pardoned Stephen Buyer, a former Republican congressman from Indiana, who served nearly two years in prison for insider trading. Buyer was sentenced in 2023 to 22 months after making illegal stock trades while working as a consultant and lobbyist. He was ordered to forfeit over $350,000 in illegal gains and pay a $10,000 fine before his release in 2025.

The pardon comes as Trump's administration promotes efforts to crack down on fraud in Democratic-led states. This marks another instance of Trump using his pardon power to excuse financial crimes committed by Republicans and political allies.
