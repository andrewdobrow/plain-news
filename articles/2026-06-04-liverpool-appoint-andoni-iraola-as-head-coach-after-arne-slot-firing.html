Liverpool appoint Andoni Iraola as head coach after Arne Slot firing

Liverpool have confirmed the appointment of Andoni Iraola as their new head coach on a two-year contract, just six days after sacking Arne Slot. The 43-year-old Spanish coach will lead the club until the end of the 2027-28 season.

Iraola arrives from Bournemouth, where he worked under sporting director Richard Hughes, who also led Liverpool's decision to make the change. Hughes and Michael Edwards, chief executive of football for owner Fenway Sports Group, conducted a review of the team's poor title defence under Slot and concluded that a more aggressive, attacking style was needed.

Several clubs pursued Iraola's services, including AC Milan, Bayer Leverkusen and Crystal Palace. However, Liverpool identified him as their preferred candidate and were the only club to hold formal discussions with him. Other managers considered included Stuttgart's Sebastian Hoeness and Lens' Pierre Sage, but Iraola made clear from the start that he wanted only Liverpool.

Speaking to Liverpool's media channel, Iraola acknowledged the challenge ahead. "At the beginning when you arrive at any club, I think you need to kind of prove a little bit yourself. You need to earn the right also to belong," he said. "For me, football is about emotions. About passion. No better place than Anfield."

On his ambitions with the club, Iraola stated: "I think Liverpool gives me the chance to coach top players, and top players give you the chance to fight for titles. To win titles. Obviously when you arrive at a place, you cannot promise everything. But it's true that I understand where I'm coming and what is expected. I'm ready for the challenge."

Negotiations progressed quickly after Iraola's agent, Iñaki Ibáñez, opened formal talks earlier in the week. Hughes' close working relationship with Ibáñez, who also represents Xabi Alonso, helped facilitate discussions.

Iraola plans to bring his Bournemouth backroom team to Anfield. Tommy Elphick and Shaun Cooper will join as assistants, along with analyst Tom Webber and fitness coach Pablo de la Torre. Elphick and Cooper remain under contract at Bournemouth, and Liverpool have not yet finalised any additional staff appointments.

Liverpool view Iraola as one of the best available coaches and believe he is perfectly suited to the club's preferred style. His proven track record in the Premier League impressed the club, particularly his ability to develop Bournemouth into an exciting, high-pressing team while operating with limited resources. His reputation for improving players individually and collectively, combined with his leadership qualities, made him an attractive candidate to restore Liverpool's ambitions at the highest level.
