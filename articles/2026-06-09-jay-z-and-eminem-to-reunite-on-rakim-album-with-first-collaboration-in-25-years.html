Jay-Z and Eminem to Reunite on Rakim Album With First Collaboration in 25 Years

Jay-Z and Eminem will appear together on a new collaborative album from rap icon Rakim, marking their first track together in a quarter century. The upcoming album, which also features Kurupt and Masta Killa, represents a reunion of some of hip-hop's most influential voices spanning multiple generations.

The collaboration pays homage to Rakim, widely regarded as one of the most influential MCs in hip-hop history. Rakim rose to prominence in the 1980s as part of the duo Eric B. & Rakim, known for revolutionizing rap with complex internal rhyme schemes and multisyllabic patterns that influenced countless artists who followed.

Jay-Z and Eminem last collaborated on a track 25 years ago, before either had reached the heights of their careers. Since then, both have become defining figures in rap music. Jay-Z has released multiple platinum albums and built a business empire spanning music, sports, and entertainment. Eminem became one of the best-selling music artists of all time, known for technical wordplay and storytelling that drew comparisons to Rakim himself.

The album brings together artists from different eras of hip-hop. Kurupt emerged in the 1990s as part of Death Row Records and Tha Dogg Pound, while Masta Killa is a member of the Wu-Tang Clan, the influential collective that helped define East Coast hip-hop in the mid-1990s. The combination of these artists on a Rakim project creates a cross-generational collaboration that spans nearly four decades of rap history.

No release date for the album has been announced. The project comes at a time when veteran hip-hop artists have increasingly collaborated on albums that celebrate the genre's history and legacy.
