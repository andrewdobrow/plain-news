Trump considers government stake in top AI companies

President Trump said his administration is considering taking government ownership stakes in top artificial intelligence companies. The discussions include possible government equity in OpenAI, with talks already underway about the arrangement.

Trump indicated he may meet with artificial intelligence companies as soon as next week to discuss a government profit-sharing plan. The proposal would give the federal government partial ownership in exchange for supporting AI development, representing a significant departure from traditional government-industry relationships in the technology sector.
