Klay Thompson joins Heat after Mavericks buyout

Klay Thompson has agreed to join the Miami Heat after reaching a contract buyout with the Dallas Mavericks. The four-time NBA champion will sign a two-year deal with Miami, bringing him together with Giannis Antetokounmpo on the Heat roster.

Thompson's departure from Dallas comes after a turbulent period with the Mavericks. Despite the chaos within the organization, Thompson handled the situation gracefully according to reports. The exact terms of the buyout agreement were not disclosed, but the arrangement clears the way for the veteran guard to pursue a championship with his new team.

The 36-year-old Thompson spent the majority of his career with the Golden State Warriors, where he won four NBA titles alongside Stephen Curry and Draymond Green. He signed with the Mavericks last summer but the relationship proved short-lived. His tenure in Dallas was marked by organizational instability, though Thompson maintained professionalism throughout.

The Heat acquisition reunites Thompson with Antetokounmpo, forming a veteran pairing that adds significant firepower to Miami's roster. Thompson's shooting ability and championship experience make him a valuable addition as the Heat look to contend in the Eastern Conference. The two-year commitment suggests both Thompson and the Heat organization view this as a serious push for another title run.

The move represents the latest shift in what has been a nomadic final chapter of Thompson's career. After 13 seasons in Golden State, his brief stop in Dallas gives way to a new opportunity in South Beach. Miami gains a proven scorer and defender with extensive playoff experience, while Thompson gets a fresh start with a team built around another superstar in Antetokounmpo.
